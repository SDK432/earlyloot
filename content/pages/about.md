**EarlyLoot** is an independent, fan-made site covering the biggest game launches: right now that's **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4**, **ARC Raiders** and **Grand Theft Auto VI**.

## What we do

- **Launch coverage:** release dates, platforms, editions and what's actually confirmed.
- **Update breakdowns:** patch and season content, explained in plain English.
- **Guides:** practical tips to help you hit the ground running on day one.

## How we source information

We only report facts we can trace to an official source, such as publisher blogs, official support pages and press releases. Every article links to its sources. We don't publish leaks or rumors as fact, and we update articles when official details change.

## Images

The images on EarlyLoot are our own gameplay screenshots or original artwork. We don't use official logos, cover art or promotional wallpapers.
