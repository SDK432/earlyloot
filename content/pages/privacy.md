This Privacy Policy explains what information **EarlyLoot** ("we", "us") collects when you visit **earlyloot.com**, how it is used, and the choices you have. EarlyLoot is run by Ricardo Aules. If you have any questions, email us at [y2rick432@gmail.com](mailto:y2rick432@gmail.com).

## Information we collect

- **No accounts.** You can read EarlyLoot without signing up, and we don't ask for your name, address or payment details.
- **Information you send us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply and to fix or improve the site.
- **Server and security logs.** Our hosting and network provider (Cloudflare) processes standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, the pages you request and the time of the request, to deliver the site, keep it secure and prevent abuse.
- **Analytics and advertising data** collected through cookies and similar technologies, described below.

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we never knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files that a website or a third party stores in your browser. EarlyLoot and our partners use cookies, web beacons, local storage and similar technologies to measure how the site is used and, where enabled, to show advertising.

### Google Analytics 4

We use **Google Analytics 4** (provided by Google LLC) to understand how visitors use the site, for example which articles are read, how long people stay and which devices they use. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (such as `_ga` and `_ga_*`) and collects information such as pages viewed, approximate location derived from your IP address, browser and device details, and referral source. We use this data only in aggregate to improve our content.

You can learn more in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and opt out of Google Analytics on all websites by installing the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Advertising, Google AdSense and the DoubleClick cookie

EarlyLoot may display ads served by **Google AdSense** and other third-party vendors. Please note:

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies, including the **DoubleClick cookie** (and cookies on domains such as `doubleclick.net` and `googlesyndication.com`), enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- Third-party ad vendors and ad networks may also use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies to measure ad performance and to show you relevant ads. These companies have their own privacy policies, and we don't control the cookies they set.
- Where we show non-personalized ads, cookies may still be used for frequency capping, aggregated ad reporting and to combat fraud and abuse.

You can see the list of Google's advertising partners and how they use data in [Google's Advertising Policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## How to opt out of personalized advertising

- **Google:** you can opt out of personalized advertising from Google by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) (My Ad Center).
- **Other vendors:** you can opt out of many third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) (Digital Advertising Alliance) or, in Europe, [www.youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/).
- **Your browser:** most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. If you block cookies, the site will still work, but ads may be less relevant.
- **Consent tools:** where the law requires it (for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, or in US states with privacy laws), we will show a consent or privacy message, before personalized ads are served, that lets you accept, reject or manage cookies and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.
