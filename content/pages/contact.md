EarlyLoot is run by **Ricardo Aules (Rick)**, a gamer who covers Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone, ARC Raiders and GTA VI.

## Email

The best way to reach us is by email: **[y2rick432@gmail.com](mailto:y2rick432@gmail.com)**. We usually reply within 2–3 business days.

## What to contact us about

- **Corrections:** spotted a wrong date, price or detail? Send the article link and the official source, and we'll update it.
- **News tips:** official announcements, patch notes or events we should cover.
- **Copyright or trademark concerns:** include the page URL and details, and we'll review it promptly.
- **Privacy requests:** questions about your data or cookies (see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy/)).
- **Advertising and business inquiries.**

Please note that EarlyLoot is an independent fan site. We can't help with game accounts, purchases, bans or technical support. For those, contact the game's publisher (Activision, Embark Studios or Rockstar Games) directly.
