**Grand Theft Auto VI** still has no official PC date, but a fresh report says Rockstar is aiming for early 2027. On October 13, 2026, [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/grand-theft-auto-vis-pc-release-is-slated-for-q1-2027/) wrote that the studio plans to ship the PC version **within the first three months of 2027**, citing four anonymous industry sources familiar with Rockstar's plans. A Rockstar representative did not respond to GamesBeat's request for comment. Treat this as a **report**, not a confirmation — Rockstar has not announced PC at all.

## What GamesBeat and others are saying

According to [GamesBeat's Alexander Lee](https://gamesbeat.com/grand-theft-auto-vis-pc-release-is-slated-for-q1-2027/), those sources describe a Q1 2027 PC window — roughly three to four months after the console launch. [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/the-pc-version-of-grand-theft-auto-6-is-reportedly-coming-before-the-end-of-march-2027/) framed the same rumor as a PC release **before the end of March 2027**. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/report-claims-grand-theft-auto-6-will-launch-on-pc-sooner-than-anyone-expected/) called the reported gap shorter than many fans expected, and [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-pc-release-q1-2027-rumor/) noted that *Red Dead Redemption 2* took about a year to reach PC after its console debut.

For context, [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/grand-theft-auto-vis-pc-release-is-slated-for-q1-2027/) points to **GTA V**: consoles in September 2013, PC in April 2015 — about **one year and seven months** later. A Q1 2027 PC build for GTA VI would be a much tighter follow-up.

## Why a fast PC launch would matter

GamesBeat ties a quicker PC release to Rockstar's investment in roleplay and user-generated content. The studio [acquired Cfx.re](https://gamesbeat.com/grand-theft-auto-vis-pc-release-is-slated-for-q1-2027/) — the team behind FiveM — in August 2023, and FiveM runs on PC *GTA V*. Separately, Twitch CEO Dan Clancy told Bloomberg at Tokyo Game Show that GTA VI online launches **"next year."** Rockstar has **not** announced an online launch date, so that comment is also unconfirmed.

## Rockstar: Nov 19 console date is "definitely" on time

While PC remains a rumor, Rockstar is publicly defending the console schedule. Rockstar North co-studio lead **Rob Nelson** told Famitsu, as quoted by [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-release-date-rockstar-statement-on-time/): "I'd be thrilled if you feel confident about the quality. And it will definitely be released on time."

That matters after a long delay trail: announced for **2025** in December 2023, pushed to **May 2026**, then to **November 19, 2026**. Officially, [rockstargames.com/VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) still lists **November 19, 2026** on **PS5** and **Xbox Series X|S**, with preload starting **November 12**. No PC SKU appears on the page.
