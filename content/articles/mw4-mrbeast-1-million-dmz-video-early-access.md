Call of Duty just handed DMZ the biggest possible hype man. On Monday, October 19, MrBeast published his Modern Warfare 4 video, titled "If You Escape, You Win $1,000,000," [according to GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/mr-beast-teases-a-huge-call-of-duty-video-focused-on-dmz-with-real-life-content-creators/). The official [Call of Duty account summed up the rules](https://x.com/CallofDuty/status/2112217767566848070): "10 teams, $1M on the line." It lands exactly one day before **MW4 DMZ Early Access opens on Tuesday, October 20**.

## How MrBeast's DMZ challenge works

[Infinity Ward laid out the format](https://x.com/InfinityWard/status/2112221107923423295) in its own post:

- **10 teams** drop into **Hajin**, each starting with **$100,000**, for **$1 million** in total.
- Teams eliminate rivals to **take their dog tags and cash**.
- Nobody keeps anything unless they **make it to exfil** with their winnings.

That's DMZ's core loop of risk, loot and extraction, with real money in place of in-game gear. Call of Duty calls it "DMZ, done @MrBeast's way."

## A full-circle moment, and a big cast

GameSpot notes that MrBeast, who has more than 500 million YouTube subscribers, teased it as "one of the biggest COD videos of all time." He also pointed out that his channel started with Call of Duty videos. Other creators joined in: SnowBikeMike's teaser promised "Call of Duty x Mr. Beast x 19 Incredible Creators and ME."

Some fans asked why it dropped before DMZ was even open. Call of Duty [answered directly](https://x.com/CallofDuty/status/2112226751849848929): "We dropped the video a day early so players can download and be ready to drop in for DMZ Early Access starting October 20."

## DMZ launch-week intel dropped the same day

Activision paired the video with a wave of launch-week progression details:

- **DMZ gets its own progression**, with [70 levels of Level Unlocks](https://x.com/CallofDuty/status/2112227907846242699). They cover FOB Stations, upgrades and 3D Printer recipes, plus gear, Operator skins, consumables, survival kits and fire support.
- **Returning DMZ veterans get rewards.** If you played the Modern Warfare II DMZ Beta, link the same Activision account to claim the "Beta On Record" emblem and "Beta Squad" calling card in MW4 DMZ Early Access, [per Call of Duty](https://x.com/CallofDuty/status/2112229122453713023). There's an extra tier for players who logged 50+ hours.
- **DMZ has its own Mastery Camos**: Chiral, Ripple Tide and Helio, [separate from Multiplayer and Warzone](https://x.com/CallofDuty/status/2112228658790244435).

If you want the basics first, our [DMZ bounties and Wanted status explainer](/articles/mw4-dmz-bounties-wanted-explained/) covers how hunting other squads works. For more on the map, see [Hajin's role in Warzone's MW4 era](/articles/warzone-mw4-integration-hajin-vondel-zipline/).

## What's next

- **October 20:** DMZ Early Access opens for digital pre-orders. Our [early access guide](/articles/mw4-dmz-early-access-october-20/) explains who qualifies.
- **Now through October 23:** The full campaign stays playable early for digital pre-orders, [per the official blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-campaign-early-access-live-now).
- **October 23:** Modern Warfare 4 launches worldwide on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Nintendo Switch 2.

Whether you watch MrBeast's squads fight over a million dollars or just download and wait, tomorrow is when DMZ stops being a video and becomes your problem. Bring a plan for exfil.
