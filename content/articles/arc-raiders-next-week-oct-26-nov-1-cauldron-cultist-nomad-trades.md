Embark's weekly radio brief is out, and this week has an autumn theme. In its [Sunday post on the official ARC Raiders account](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2114356310426685627), the studio previewed **October 26 to November 1**: Cassio is bringing in "autumnal gear", the **Cauldron Backpack Attachment** and the **Cultist Backpack**. Two ARC parts are also becoming tradeable. It's a busy stretch, with the [Soul's Offering Raider Project](/articles/arc-raiders-souls-offering-raider-project-october-27/) starting Tuesday and the game's first anniversary on Friday.

## Nomad offers: Cauldron, Cultist and a research book

Embark's pitch is to "brew up a seasonal stew" with the Cauldron and to "embrace the old ways of the Nomads" with the Cultist Backpack. The [weekly graphic](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2114356310426685627) lists three Nomad offers:

- **Cauldron (backpack attachment):** priced at 50K in Epic/Legendary ARC cores, plus a firearm
- **Exodus Technical Handbook (rare research item):** 15K in ARC parts plus a firearm of any tier
- **Cultist (backpack):** 100K in Epic/Legendary ARC cores

The Cultist isn't brand new. The [community wiki](https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Backpack_(cosmetic)) lists it as an unlock from the earlier Forgotten Relics event, so this week gives players who missed it another chance. The Exodus Technical Handbook is useful beyond looks. The [wiki's Bully Fragmenter page](https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Bully_Fragmenter) lists it in the Research Station 3 upgrade.

## Bully Fragmenter and Skulker Targeter join trades

Embark also says that "starting from next week's update", the **Bully Fragmenter** and **Skulker Targeter** are [part of Nomadic trades](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2114356310426685627). Both come from enemies that arrived with [Frozen Trail](/articles/arc-raiders-frozen-trail-pendola-pass/). Last week's Trials sent players [after the Bully for its Fragmenter](/articles/arc-raiders-next-week-oct-19-25-yank-grenades-trials/). The wiki lists the Fragmenter as an Epic item that the Bully and ARC Couriers drop. You need six of them for Research Station 3 and five for the Anvil blueprint, so getting them through trades could save a lot of grinding.

## This week's Trials

According to the same graphic, this week's [Trials Season 6](/articles/arc-raiders-trials-season-6-frozen-trials/) challenges are:

- **Pendola Pass:** search ARC Husks
- **Search backpacks**
- **Damage flying ARC** with Medium ammo weapons
- **Damage ARC** using the Deadline
- **Deal as much damage as possible** with a Blaze Grenade

## Soul's Offering is almost here

Embark is also building up to Tuesday's event. On Monday it posted a [short piece of lore](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2114763990177886215) from a Speranza resident who remembers "The First Wave" as a child. The narrator mentions sunburned "gunslingers from Murteta" and ends: "this Soul's Offering, I think it's time. I'm going to go topside – and finally pay my respects." That fits the remembrance theme Embark set with [the original teaser](/articles/arc-raiders-souls-offering-raider-project-october-27/).

If you want some background before the event, Embark also shared [episode 4 of The Evolution of ARC Raiders](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2114401610566709545). In it, Danny O'Dwyer talks to the developers about the enemies added since launch, Embark's machine-learning tech, and how the ARC get their names.
