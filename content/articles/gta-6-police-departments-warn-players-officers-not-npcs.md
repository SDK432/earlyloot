GTA 6 has been out for barely a day, and real police departments are already speaking the game's language. Across the US, agencies are flooding social media with one message for players: **keep the crimes in Leonida**. It started on November 17, when the Atlanta Police Department posted, ["GTA is not a real place, but Atlanta is!"](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/local/no-respawn-restart-undo-button-police-warn-drivers-against-mimicking-grand-theft-auto-vi/4O52VAJMKFFNBPSHRYRIPPXAF4/) Since then the warnings have spread from Georgia to Texas and Colorado.

## The posts, department by department

- **Atlanta PD (Georgia):** Atlanta's post listed drunk driving, reckless driving, auto theft and violence as things that "have real consequences," and it ended with ["Keep it in the game. Don't bring it to our streets,"](https://www.livenowfox.com/news/police-warn-gta-6-players-real-life-crimes-carry-consequences) LiveNOW from FOX reports. Its graphics read "Play the game, don't play yourself."
- **South Fulton PD (Georgia):** South Fulton skipped the jokes: ["There's no respawn, restart, or undo button,"](https://www.wsbtv.com/news/local/no-respawn-restart-undo-button-police-warn-drivers-against-mimicking-grand-theft-auto-vi/4O52VAJMKFFNBPSHRYRIPPXAF4/) it wrote on Facebook. WSB-TV ties the warning to metro Atlanta's ongoing fight against street racing.
- **Floresville PD (Texas):** This is where the viral line came from. Per the [San Antonio Express-News](https://www.expressnews.com/news/article/texas-police-gtavi-crime-in-game-22539773.php), Floresville said the town is "NOT an extension of Los Santos OR VICE CITY" and that its officers are "NOT NPCs!" Its other reminders: running a red light gets you a traffic stop, not bonus points, and repainting your car in an H-E-B parking lot won't clear your wanted level.
- **San Juan PD (Texas):** San Juan posted a jail-cell video with the series' "Busted" screen and the line ["what happens in GTA VI stays in GTA VI,"](https://www.expressnews.com/news/article/texas-police-gtavi-crime-in-game-22539773.php) the same paper reports.
- **Denver and Aurora (Colorado):** Denver police asked followers to ["keep all grand theft auto activities inside the video game,"](https://www.denvergazette.com/2026/11/19/denver-police-warn-grand-theft-auto-is-still-illegal-amid-gta-6-joy/) and the post came with an AI-made, GTA-style officer. Aurora PD added, "Stealing a car can still be a felony. Even if it's parked outside and looks really nice."

[Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/us-police-forces-worried-gta-6-players-will-start-committing-real-crimes-warn-officers-are-not-npcs-3430007/) also points out the opposite take: Greensboro police in North Carolina said they couldn't wait for launch because the game might keep people indoors.

## The backlash to the backlash

Not everyone is laughing along. [WIRED calls the trend "corny,"](https://www.wired.com/story/cops-are-being-extremely-corny-about-gta-vi/) noting that GTA 6 openly mocks its own fictional Vice City police. Some departments even turned the hype into AI-generated recruitment ads.

On cable news, things got more heated. On Fox News' *Outnumbered* on November 19, retired NYPD inspector Paul Mauro said letting a child play the game is ["almost the equivalent of giving a kid a gun at an age when they don't understand the responsibility,"](https://www.newsweek.com/fox-news-panelist-sparks-debate-by-comparing-gta-6-to-giving-a-child-a-gun-12742844) Newsweek reports. Co-host Kayleigh McEnany said her son will be ["nowhere near anything like this while in my household!"](https://www.mediaite.com/media/news/fox-outnumbered-panel-rails-against-gta-6-not-in-my-household/)

## The long-running debate

Here's the context the panel skipped. GTA 6 is [rated M (Mature 17+) by the ESRB](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/grand-theft-auto-vi-shatters-entertainment-box-office-records/), so it was never meant for kids. As [Newsweek notes](https://www.newsweek.com/fox-news-panelist-sparks-debate-by-comparing-gta-6-to-giving-a-child-a-gun-12742844), the American Psychological Association said in 2020 that there is insufficient scientific evidence of a causal link between violent games and violent behavior. A 2019 Oxford study of about 1,000 teens found no link between violent gaming and aggression.

## Why everyone's talking about it

The scale makes the attention impossible to avoid. Take-Two's [official November 20 release](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/grand-theft-auto-vi-shatters-entertainment-box-office-records/) reports **$3.79 billion in day-one retail sales** and **32.5 million units sold pre-release**. That's well above the [$2.36 billion Sensor Tower estimate we covered earlier](/articles/gta-6-2-36-billion-day-one-sensor-tower-vs-gta-5-spider-man/). When a game moves that many copies, every police account in America wants a piece of the moment.

So go ahead and chase six stars in Vice City. Just don't try the paint-shop trick at your local H-E-B.
