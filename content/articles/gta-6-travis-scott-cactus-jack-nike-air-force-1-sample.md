Travis Scott has given fans their first look at a **GTA VI-themed Cactus Jack x Rockstar Games x Nike Air Force 1**. The rapper previewed a sample pair in an Instagram Story on **November 6, 2026**, [Complex reports](https://www.complex.com/sneakers/a/victor-deng/cactus-jack-rockstar-games-nike-air-force-1-gta-6-release-date), less than two weeks before the game launches. The Story is the only first-hand evidence so far: Nike, Rockstar and Cactus Jack have not officially announced the shoes, a price or a release date.

## What Travis Scott showed

The pair in the Story is a multicolor Air Force 1 Low. According to [GTANotes](https://gtanotes.com/en/news/gta-6-cactus-jack-nike-air-force-1-friends-family-preview/), it carries **GTA branding on the midsole** and mixes pink, purple, black and white panels. Scott's text overlay said only a couple of pairs were made, which points to a **friends-and-family** release rather than a retail model.

[Complex](https://www.complex.com/sneakers/a/victor-deng/cactus-jack-rockstar-games-nike-air-force-1-gta-6-release-date) also describes this version as "very limited," while noting that other, less limited colorways are rumored for stores.

## The reported retail lineup

The wider collection is still unconfirmed. Instagram account **@Sneakermarkertro** (Sneaker Market RO) first reported the collab, and on **October 31** it listed three Air Force 1 Low '07 colorways at **US$130 each**, releasing **November 19**, [GTA BOOM reports](https://www.gtaboom.com/travis-scott-rockstar-nike-air-force-1-gta-6-launch-9f9c). The reported style codes are:

- **Black/Fire Pink:** JJ7604-001
- **White/Fire Pink:** JJ7602-100
- **Multi-Color/Pale Ivory:** JJ7603-900

Earlier photos covered by [GTA BOOM](https://www.gtaboom.com/travis-scott-rockstar-nike-air-force-1-gta-6-launch-9f9c) showed a black pair with a reverse Swoosh, pink trim around the toe and pink stars across the sole. Treat all of this as reported, not confirmed: [GTANotes](https://gtanotes.com/en/news/gta-6-cactus-jack-nike-air-force-1-friends-family-preview/) stresses that Nike and Rockstar have not announced pricing, quantities or a retail date.

[Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/entertainment/rockstar-reportedly-teaming-up-with-and-nike-for-130-gta-6-sneakers-3423514/) adds that Sole Retriever has reported a matching clothing collection and an expected Miami Heat tie-in. Neither company has confirmed those either.

## Why Travis Scott fits

Scott is already part of the GTA VI launch. His track "RHYNO" came out on **September 17** as "RHYNO (from GTAVI: The Album)," [per GTA BOOM](https://www.gtaboom.com/travis-scott-rockstar-nike-air-force-1-gta-6-launch-9f9c), and it appears in the album tracklist we covered when Rockstar [revealed the GTA 6 radio stations](/articles/gta-6-radio-stations-revealed/).

The sneakers would also join a growing list of real-world launch promos, from the [six real cars West Coast Customs built for Rockstar's Miami campaign](/articles/gta-6-west-coast-customs-six-real-cars-miami/) to the [Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell stands at the Kaseya Center](/articles/gta-6-burger-shot-cluckin-bell-kaseya-center-miami/).

## What's next

- **November 10:** GTA VI digital pre-load opens on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S ([details and the 212GB file size](/articles/gta-6-preload-moved-november-10-212gb/)).
- **November 18:** the Miami Heat's "A Night in Vice City" game, as [we reported earlier](/articles/gta-6-tv-ad-nba-alternate-launch-trailer-cut/). That is a natural moment for any Heat tie-in to surface.
- **November 19:** GTA VI launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. It is also the reported retail date for the Air Force 1 colorways.

Until Nike or Rockstar posts an official release, expect the Story sample to remain a rare friends-and-family pair. We'll update this story if the drop is confirmed.
