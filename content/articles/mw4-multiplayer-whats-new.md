**Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4** launches on **October 23, 2026** on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Nintendo Switch 2. Infinity Ward has already laid out the core multiplayer changes in the [official reveal](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/05/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-announcement). Here's what's confirmed.

## Gunplay: "Ballistic Authority" and no bloom

MW4 introduces a new weapon tech stack that Infinity Ward calls **Ballistic Authority**. The headline changes:

- **Bloom has been removed.** Hipfire shots go where the weapon is pointed.
- Recoil, convergence and weapon handling have been rebuilt to reflect player input more directly.
- Depth of field and visual effects have been refined to make enemies easier to see.

## Movement

There are expanded options for **mantling, climbing, hanging and jumping**, built to let you reposition, flank and handle vertical spaces. Transitions between traversal, movement and gunplay are designed to keep your momentum.

## Maps and modes

- **12 Core maps** at launch, each with its own custom infil
- **Kill Block:** set in the Westbridge Training Facility, it reconfigures between rounds from modular sections, with **more than 500 possible layouts**. It supports 3v3 and 10v10 Gunfight formats, with more modes planned.
- Dedicated **Gunfight** maps
- **Big War** maps for large-scale vehicle and infantry combat

## Loadouts: Create-a-Class, Gunsmith and Gunny

- A redesigned **Create-a-Class** puts Operators, weapons, Equipment and Killstreaks into a single loadout.
- **Gunsmith** returns. Attachments are now **shared by weapon class**.
- **Gunny** is new: it builds a close-, mid- or long-range weapon from your unlocked attachments, using configurations from Infinity Ward's Weapons Team.

## Apex Attachments and Prestige

**Apex Attachments** unlock when you fully level a weapon. They can change its handling, firing behavior, tactical utility, stealth or combat role.

There are also **two Prestige paths**:

- **Classic Prestige:** relocks Create-a-Class progression in exchange for higher XP rates and exclusive rewards.
- **Regular Prestige:** restarts you at Level 1 but keeps your unlocked loadouts, with its own rewards.

## DMZ returns

**DMZ**, Call of Duty's extraction mode, is back. You play as an off-the-books asset behind enemy lines, with changing weather and dynamic objectives. You can go in solo or with a squad.

## Warzone and last-gen consoles

Starting with **Season 1**, Call of Duty: Warzone will integrate Modern Warfare 4 content and progression. As part of that change, **Warzone on PS4 and Xbox One stops being playable once Season 1 begins**.

## PC features

The PC version is developed with Beenox. Confirmed features include **DLSS 4.5 support**, multiple upscaling and frame generation options, expanded real-time ray tracing, and competitive settings that prioritize high frame rates.
