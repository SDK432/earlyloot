Cassio is headed back to Speranza with a tribute-friendly restock. In the [weekly radio brief posted Sunday, November 8, 2026](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2119429733880635752), the official ARC Raiders account says Cassio is "rolling into Speranza with fresh stock, just in time for anyone scrambling to pull together an offering for the ongoing event." The window is **November 10 to 16** on both the post and the attached graphic, and the headliners are a **Gas Mask** backpack charm, the **Angry** emote and **Mask Fragment** trinkets.

## Nomad offers: Gas Mask, Angry and Mask Fragment

The [weekly graphic](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2119429733880635752) lists three Nomad offers:

- **Angry (emote):** 75K Epic/Legendary ARC parts + a firearm
- **Gas Mask (backpack charm):** 75K Epic/Legendary ARC parts + a firearm
- **Mask Fragment (Epic trinket):** 15K ARC parts + a firearm, any tier

The graphic also flags "& more", so there may be extra shop items once the rotation goes live. Embark does not spell out what those extras are.

The studio tells Raiders to grab the Gas Mask charm and "pick up a few of his trinkets if you're still short on your tribute." The 15K Mask Fragment is the practical pickup if you still need a tribute piece. The [community wiki](https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Mask_Fragment) describes it as "Half a face, cast in bronze," an Epic trinket added in Update 2.3 that you normally scavenge in Old World areas. Both the wiki and [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/all-souls-offering-level-requirements-in-arc-raiders-and-how-to-get-them/) list a Mask Fragment among the materials for [Soul's Offering](/articles/arc-raiders-update-2-3-souls-offering-live-heavy-shield-flame-spray/); Destructoid places it in Murteta's Offering.

## This week's Trials

The same graphic says the [Trials Season 6](/articles/arc-raiders-trials-season-6-frozen-trials/) challenges for the week are:

- **Open Containers**
- **Deal melee damage to ARC enemies**
- **Damage ARC using a Hullcracker**
- **Damage any ARC enemies**
- **Loot ARC enemies**

Four of the five objectives involve hitting or looting ARC, which matches Embark's note that the Trials "call for serious ARC damage" and that you should "make every hit count as you tear through their hulls."

The Hullcracker challenge is the one to plan around. Anyone who picked up a launcher from [last week's Nomad rotation](/articles/arc-raiders-next-week-nov-2-8-snap-hook-hullcracker-vintage-mic/) already has the tool. The [wiki](https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Hullcracker) describes it as a pump-action special that fires explosive rounds which only detonate on ARC, with "Very Strong" 2.5x ARC armor penetration. A separate melee objective means close-range hits count too, and Open Containers can ride along on the same Topside runs.

## Offerings and tribute

Sunday's brief is unusually pointed about the ongoing event. Cassio's stock is timed for Raiders who are "scrambling to pull together an offering," and Embark hopes you will "crawl back from Topside with your offerings intact." Our earlier coverage identified that project as [Soul's Offering](/articles/arc-raiders-update-2-3-souls-offering-live-heavy-shield-flame-spray/), which launched with Update 2.3. [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/all-souls-offering-level-requirements-in-arc-raiders-and-how-to-get-them/) reports it runs **until November 16** — the same day this Nomad window ends — though Embark does not repeat that end date in Sunday's post. Buying the Fragment from Cassio still means extracting with it.

## What's next

- **November 10–16:** Gas Mask charm, Angry emote and Mask Fragments in Nomad offers, plus ARC-damage Trials that include melee hits and the Hullcracker
- **Through November 16:** [Soul's Offering](/articles/arc-raiders-update-2-3-souls-offering-live-heavy-shield-flame-spray/) keeps running, according to [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/all-souls-offering-level-requirements-in-arc-raiders-and-how-to-get-them/)
