**Grand Theft Auto VI** hasn't launched yet, and it's already setting records. According to a [Circana report published October 28](https://www.circana.com/post/nearly-1-in-3-u-s-console-and-pc-gamers-plan-to-buy-grand-theft-auto-vi-setting-a-new-industry-rec), **29% of US console and PC gamers** say they plan to buy GTA 6. That's the highest purchase intent the research firm has ever recorded for a video game, which makes it the most anticipated release in Circana's tracking history.

## Nearly 1 in 3 players plan to buy it

The number comes from Circana's PlayerPulse survey, and the firm sums it up as "nearly 1-in-3" US console and PC gamers, [per its press release](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/10/28/3390533/0/en/nearly-1-in-3-u-s-console-and-pc-gamers-plan-to-buy-grand-theft-auto-vi-setting-a-new-industry-record-circana-reports.html). Keep in mind that this measures intent, not sales: it shows how many players say they want the game, not how many copies will actually sell.

Circana analyst **Mat Piscatella** called GTA 6 "a rare cultural and commercial moment for the video game industry" and pointed to the **13-year wait** since GTA V as a big reason why, [Circana says](https://www.circana.com/post/nearly-1-in-3-u-s-console-and-pc-gamers-plan-to-buy-grand-theft-auto-vi-setting-a-new-industry-rec). He also expects demand "to extend beyond software sales to include consoles, accessories, and gaming subscriptions."

## A Black Friday boost for consoles

The timing helps. GTA 6 arrives on **November 19**, just ahead of Black Friday and the holiday shopping rush, [GameSpot notes](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-breaks-a-record-and-its-not-even-out-yet/). Piscatella believes players who haven't upgraded yet may buy a **PS5 or Xbox Series X|S** just to play it. He also called the game a likely driver of "hardware upgrades, and gaming-related tech purchases throughout the fourth quarter," [according to Circana](https://www.circana.com/post/nearly-1-in-3-u-s-console-and-pc-gamers-plan-to-buy-grand-theft-auto-vi-setting-a-new-industry-rec).

Circana also points out that GTA V is still the best-selling premium game in its US tracking history, by both units and dollars.

## Sensor Tower: about 7 million pre-orders

Pre-order estimates keep climbing too. [GameSpot reports](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-breaks-a-record-and-its-not-even-out-yet/) that analytics firm Sensor Tower now puts GTA 6 at roughly **7 million units sold**, worth about **$675 million**. That's up from the [6.45 million estimate we covered on October 24](/articles/gta-6-pre-orders-6-45-million-sensor-tower/). Sensor Tower believes the game could reach **25 million pre-orders and $2 billion** in revenue before launch day.

These are **third-party estimates**. Rockstar and Take-Two haven't confirmed any pre-order figures.

## Console only at launch

One catch: Circana surveyed both console and PC players, but GTA 6 is confirmed **only for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** for now. A PC version is *expected* in early 2027, [GameSpot says](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-breaks-a-record-and-its-not-even-out-yet/), but Rockstar hasn't announced it.

Looking further ahead, **Bank of America** analysts expect a new GTA Online could arrive in the first quarter of 2027, [GameSpot reports](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-online-could-be-released-in-early-2027-bank-says/). That's an analyst forecast, not a Rockstar announcement.
