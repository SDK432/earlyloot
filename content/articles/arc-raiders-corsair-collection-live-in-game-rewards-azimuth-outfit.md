Yesterday the big unknowns were the price and whether the gear came with any in-game loot. Now we have answers. On Thursday, October 29, Embark Studios published the [official ARC Raiders x CORSAIR announcement](https://arcraiders.com/news/corsair-collab-exclusive-rewards) and preorders opened on corsair.com. **Every qualifying product comes with a single-use code for an exclusive in-game item.** If you buy the full set, you also get the **Azimuth Outfit**. The drop lands one day before ARC Raiders turns one on October 30. (Catch up with [our first report on the collab](/articles/arc-raiders-corsair-scuf-elgato-anniversary-collection-october-29/).)

## What's in the collection

According to [Embark's post](https://arcraiders.com/news/corsair-collab-exclusive-rewards) and [CORSAIR's own breakdown](https://www.corsair.com/us/en/explorer/gamer/keyboards/corsair-x-arc-raiders-a-special-edition-collection-built-for-the-surface/), the collection covers three brands:

- **SCUF:** the Omega (PS5 and PC), the Valor Pro Wireless and Wired (Xbox and PC), a Valor Pro faceplate kit, and the Nemesis Pro 8K for PC, which is marked "coming soon"
- **CORSAIR:** the CLIPPER PRO MINI 60 keyboard, the SABRE v2 PRO Wireless MG mouse, the HS80 RGB Wireless headset, and Large and 2XL mouse pads. The 2XL pad is printed with a weathered topographic map of the Rust Belt.
- **Elgato:** the Stream Deck MK.2 and a matching faceplate

So yes, PlayStation players get a controller. We flagged that as unconfirmed yesterday because the teaser only showed an Xbox-style pad.

## Every in-game reward

CORSAIR's [promotion terms](https://www.corsair.com/ww/en/s/arc-raiders-terms) list one item per product category:

- **SCUF controller:** Joystick Charm
- **CORSAIR keyboard:** Keyboard Attachment
- **CORSAIR mouse pad:** Fax Machine Attachment
- **CORSAIR mouse:** Mouse Charm
- **HS80 headset:** Pirate Hat Scrappy cosmetic
- **Stream Deck MK.2 (or its faceplate):** Courier Backpack
- **Grand Bundle:** Azimuth Outfit

To get the Azimuth Outfit, you need a controller, keyboard, mouse pad, mouse, headset **and** the Stream Deck MK.2 device. The faceplate alone doesn't count toward the bundle. Each item can be redeemed only **once per ARC Raiders account**, so buying two controllers won't get you two charms. Codes work on PlayStation, Xbox and PC through the [Embark ID giveaway portal](https://id.embark.games/id/arc-raiders/giveaway). The terms say the promotion runs until October 27, 2028, and it's open to buyers 18 or older.

## Prices and shipping

At the time of writing, CORSAIR's US store lists the [SCUF Omega ARC Raiders Edition](https://www.corsair.com/us/en/p/ps5/408-178-01-026-306/scuf-omega-controller-arc-raiders-edition) at **$239.99**, on backorder with an expected ship date of November 12. The [Ultimate Loadout Bundle](https://www.corsair.com/us/en/p/keyboards/hs80-sabremg-mm2xl-clipper-sd-omega-arcraiders-na/arc-raiders-edition-ultimate-loadout-bundle) costs **$739.99**, down from $869.94, and is listed as shipping December 6 to 9. It includes all six pieces you need for the Azimuth Outfit. Embark warns that quantities are limited and that shipping dates will vary.

## Also new today: the Contradas of Toledo

Embark also published a lore post, [The 'Contradas' of Toledo](https://arcraiders.com/news/who-are-the-contradas-of-toledo). It explains the neighborhoods behind the [Soul's Offering Raider Project](/articles/arc-raiders-update-2-3-souls-offering-live-heavy-shield-flame-spray/):

- **Speranza:** home, Toledo's oldest and most open Contrada
- **Murteta:** rules, order and strict hierarchy
- **Ferrovia:** engineers, artists and smugglers, who helped build the Ballistic Gondolas
- **Vaulto:** a secretive neighborhood whose jamming tech hides Toledo from ARC

Celeste wants offerings for Ferrovia, Murteta and Vaulto to keep relations from turning sour.
