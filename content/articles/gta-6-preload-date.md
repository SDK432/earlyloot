**Grand Theft Auto VI** launches on **November 19, 2026**, and you can start downloading it a week early. Rockstar has [confirmed](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi) that pre-loading begins on **November 12, 2026**.

## When does GTA 6 pre-load start?

According to [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions), pre-load begins at **local midnight on November 12, 2026**. That gives you seven days to download before launch day on November 19.

## Which platforms can pre-load?

GTA VI is launching on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S** only. Pre-load is available on both.

## Digital pre-orders

If you **pre-order a digital version**, you can start pre-loading at local midnight on November 12. Digital copies are sold through the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store and [Rockstar Store](https://store.rockstargames.com/game/buy-gta-vi), and at global retailers.

## Physical copies: no disc in the box

This one's important: the physical version of GTA VI **does not include a disc**. Rockstar says the box contains **a download code**, and it will be available from **November 12** to support pre-loading. If you redeem the code before launch, the pre-load starts on November 12.

Rockstar also recommends buying from a retailer in the same country as your platform account to avoid activation problems.

## Pre-order bonuses

According to Rockstar, pre-orders and purchases made before **November 20, 2026** include the **Vintage Vice City Pack**. Digital pre-orders also get **a free month of GTA+**.

## Key dates

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| Nov 12, 2026 | Pre-load opens (local midnight) and physical code-in-box copies become available |
| Nov 19, 2026 | GTA VI launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S |
