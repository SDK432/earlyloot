**Update (Nov 3, 2026): Rockstar has moved digital pre-load up to November 10. PS5 opens at local midnight and Xbox at 12 a.m. GMT, and the file needs up to 212GB. [Read the full update](/articles/gta-6-preload-moved-november-10-212gb/).**

**Grand Theft Auto VI** launches on **November 19, 2026**, and digital buyers can start downloading it more than a week early. Rockstar originally [said](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi) pre-loading would begin on November 12, but on November 3 it [moved digital pre-load up to **November 10, 2026**](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10).

## When does GTA 6 pre-load start?

Per Rockstar's [November 3 update](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10), digital pre-load begins on **November 10, 2026**: at **midnight local time** on PS5, and globally at **12 a.m. GMT** on Xbox Series X|S (7 p.m. ET on November 9 in the US). The pre-load file needs up to 212GB. That gives you nine days to download before launch day on November 19.

## Which platforms can pre-load?

GTA VI is launching on **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S** only. Pre-load is available on both.

## Digital pre-orders

If you **pre-order a digital version**, you can start pre-loading on November 10 (local midnight on PS5, 12 a.m. GMT on Xbox). Digital copies are sold through the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store and [Rockstar Store](https://store.rockstargames.com/game/buy-gta-vi), and at global retailers.

## Physical copies: no disc in the box

This one's important: the physical version of GTA VI **does not include a disc**. Rockstar says the box contains **a download code**, and it will be available from **November 12** to support pre-loading. If you redeem the code before launch, you can pre-load from then on.

Rockstar also recommends buying from a retailer in the same country as your platform account to avoid activation problems.

## Pre-order bonuses

According to Rockstar, pre-orders and purchases made before **November 20, 2026** include the **Vintage Vice City Pack**. Digital pre-orders also get **a free month of GTA+**.
