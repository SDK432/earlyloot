**DMZ fights just got a little longer.** Infinity Ward's November 23 update for Modern Warfare 4 lowered close- and mid-range damage on **18 guns in DMZ**: 9 assault rifles, 6 SMGs and 3 LMGs, according to the [official Season 1 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes). Damage ranges didn't move. And one sniper went the other way.

## Why Infinity Ward did it

The studio says the pass targets DMZ's player-vs-player time to kill, "slightly lengthening TTK at closer range while preserving balance at longer range." The goal, per [Infinity Ward](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes), is "to give players a little bit more time to react to combat while not making players too durable." It isn't a surprise: the November 19 notes already warned that a ["broad DMZ weapon balance pass"](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) was coming "sometime next week."

Every change sits under the DMZ heading of the notes. [timesaver.gg](https://timesaver.gg/blog/mw4-patch-notes) also reports that Multiplayer and Warzone guns weren't touched.

## The one buff: Signal 50

The Signal 50 got stronger in DMZ. Its max damage stays at 235, but [mid damage rises from 145 to 180, min damage from 140 to 168, and its headshot multiplier from 1.3 to 1.6](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes). The automatic weapons lose damage while the sniper gains it, so long-range picks get relatively stronger.

## Is slower TTK good for DMZ? Our take

In an extraction mode, a fast TTK punishes whoever gets seen first. That's harsh when your whole Stash is on the line. A little extra time to turn, plate or slide into cover should reward reactions over pure ambush. The risk is the one Infinity Ward named itself: players getting "too durable." That would make third-parties worse and squad wipes slower. Add [the reported SBMM bump in DMZ](/articles/mw4-dmz-sbmm-increased-skill-matchmaking-backlash/), and squad play and positioning now matter more than raw gunfights.
