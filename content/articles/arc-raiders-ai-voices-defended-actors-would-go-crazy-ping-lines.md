Embark Studios is still defending the AI voices in ARC Raiders, and the argument keeps spreading. In the studio's [**"The Evolution of ARC Raiders S2: EP5 - Sound & Music"**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_xwbP8KV530) video, posted on the official ARC Raiders YouTube channel on November 1, 2026, audio director **Bence Pajor** says the game's text-to-speech model, trained on recordings of its own actors, was the only practical way to keep up with the ping system and features that change all the time. Spanish outlet [Vandal picked the story up today](https://vandal.elespanol.com/noticia/2026318009/polemica-a-la-vista-el-shooter-arc-raiders-abre-la-puerta-a-usar-actores-de-voz-generados-por-ia/), so the debate is back for another round.

## What Embark says in the video

The episode is hosted by **Danny O'Dwyer** and covers the game's soundscape, music and voice work. In it, the narration explains that ARC Raiders launched with a hybrid setup: most dialogue was recorded by actors, while some quests and mission briefings came from an AI model Embark trained itself. The **ping system**, which lets you call out items and locations without a mic, mixed stitched-together recordings with generated lines.

Pajor's case, as [GamesRadar+ reports](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/third-person-shooter/arc-raiders-had-to-use-ai-voices-dev-insists-because-real-voice-actors-would-just-go-crazy-if-they-had-to-read-all-of-that-and-get-paid-for-doing-it/), is that the model was trained on the actors' performances, but recording every line by hand wasn't realistic. His most-quoted line: "the actors would just go crazy if they had to read all of that." [ConsolePCGaming notes](https://consolepcgaming.com/arc-raiders-developers-address-ai-voice-criticism-and-explain-ping-system-tradeoffs/) that every new item needs ping dialogue, which creates lots of variations, and that Pajor said bringing actors back for each change wasn't feasible.

## The voice actors weigh in

Two of the game's performers appear: **Jo Rideout**, who voices Shani, and **Diana Gardner**, who voices Celeste. According to [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/third-person-shooter/arc-raiders-had-to-use-ai-voices-dev-insists-because-real-voice-actors-would-just-go-crazy-if-they-had-to-read-all-of-that-and-get-paid-for-doing-it/), Gardner called the tech "freeing" because short sessions mean she can take other jobs. [Kotaku adds](https://kotaku.com/arc-raiders-ai-voice-acting-embark-studios-video-shani-celeste-2000752744) that the pair later wondered whether they could "truly own" performances that weren't fully theirs.

Chief creative officer **Stefan Strandberg** insists Embark is "not in the industry of replacing actors" and says the studio wants to "celebrate the performance," per [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/third-person-shooter/arc-raiders-had-to-use-ai-voices-dev-insists-because-real-voice-actors-would-just-go-crazy-if-they-had-to-read-all-of-that-and-get-paid-for-doing-it/).

## Why the backlash isn't going away

Not everyone is convinced. [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/arc-raiders-ai-voice-acting-embark-studios-video-shani-celeste-2000752744) called the explanations "odd" and pointed out that voice actors have recorded huge numbers of game lines for decades. Some context on how we got here:

- **October 2025:** ARC Raiders launched with a mix of human and AI voices, which drew criticism, [Kotaku recalls](https://kotaku.com/arc-raiders-ai-voice-acting-embark-studios-video-shani-celeste-2000752744).
- **March 2026:** studio head Patrick Söderlund told GamesIndustry.biz that some lines had been re-recorded with actors, while licensed text-to-speech was still used in some cases, including pings, [ConsolePCGaming reports](https://consolepcgaming.com/arc-raiders-developers-address-ai-voice-criticism-and-explain-ping-system-tradeoffs/).
- **The Finals:** Embark faced similar complaints over AI voices in its earlier shooter, as [Vandal notes](https://vandal.elespanol.com/noticia/2026318009/polemica-a-la-vista-el-shooter-arc-raiders-abre-la-puerta-a-usar-actores-de-voz-generados-por-ia/).

Embark has asked for player voices in the past too, with the [Speranza Security voice contest](/articles/arc-raiders-speranza-security-voice-contest-gondola-extract/) back in October.

## What's next

Embark hasn't given a date for replacing the remaining AI lines. Strandberg said he wants to give performances more room and hopes players will learn more about the characters over the coming years, [ConsolePCGaming reports](https://consolepcgaming.com/arc-raiders-developers-address-ai-voice-criticism-and-explain-ping-system-tradeoffs/). The next date on the calendar is the **December 1** start of the PC-only second beta in China.
