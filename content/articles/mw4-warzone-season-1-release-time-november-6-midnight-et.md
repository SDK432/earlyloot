Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone Season 1 go live **earlier than a typical CoD season drop**. Per the [official Call of Duty blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1) and [Polygon's Nov 4 release-time guide](https://www.polygon.com/mw4-modern-warfare-4-season-1-release-time-dmz-warzone-est-pst/), servers flip at **12:00 a.m. ET on Friday, November 6** — that's **9:00 p.m. PT on Thursday, November 5**, and midnight America/Guayaquil on Nov 6. Past seasons often landed midday; this one hits overnight.

We already broke down the [full Warzone Season 1 roadmap](/articles/warzone-season-1-roadmap-hajin-zodiac-verdansk-rebirth-switch-2/) and the [MW4 Season 1 content list](/articles/mw4-season-1-full-intel-x01-black-site-csg-35-hajin-zodiac-november-6/). This post is the clock: when it drops, your local time, and a short reminder of what unlocks at launch.

Steam's Call of Duty news feed also lists the same window — **0000 midnight ET Nov 6 / 2100 PT Nov 5** — matching Activision and [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/28/modern-warfare-4-season-1-everything-you-need-to-know-for-november-6-launch/). Expect a short maintenance window right before the flip; exact downtime often posts on the CoD status channels the day of.

## What's live the second Season 1 opens

At launch you get the Season 1 package, not a drip. Per Activision:

- **MW4 Multiplayer:** maps **Backstab**, **Canyon**, and a reimagined **Shoot House**, plus returning modes **Grind** and **Drop Zone**.
- **DMZ:** the **X01 Black Site** facility goes live.
- **New guns at launch:** **Cronen Osprey** assault rifle and **CSG-35** SMG (Battle Pass track).
- **Warzone:** **Hajin** Battle Royale and **Zodiac** Resurgence, full MW4 weapon integration, a level reset, and Warzone's first launch on **Nintendo Switch 2**.
- **Later in Season 1:** Verdansk and Rebirth Island return — not day-one.

Last week's [November 2 patch](/articles/mw4-november-2-patch-notes-dive-speed-dmz-uav-towers-sedative-inhaler/) (full dive speed restored, DMZ UAV towers cut 40%) was the final launch-window tune before this drop.

## How to be ready tonight

1. Finish any remaining pre-load / update now so you're not downloading at 9 PM PT.
2. Clear stash space if you play DMZ — X01 will eat inventory fast.
3. Decide whether you're starting on MW4 multiplayer, Hajin BR, or Zodiac Resurgence; playlists rotate hard in the first hour.
4. If you're on Switch 2, budget extra time for the first Warzone install.
