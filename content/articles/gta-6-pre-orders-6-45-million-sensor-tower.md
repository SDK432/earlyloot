**Grand Theft Auto VI** is still almost four weeks from launch, and the pre-order numbers are already huge. The latest update to analytics firm Sensor Tower's GTA VI sales tracker puts the game at roughly **6.45 million digital pre-orders**, worth an estimated **$630 million**, [GameSpot reports](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-is-148-ahead-of-this-other-popular-aaa-game-so-far/). Sensor Tower also says GTA 6 is pacing **148% ahead** of 2022's *Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2* in US digital pre-orders.

One important caveat before going further: these are **third-party estimates**. Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive have not published any pre-order figures, and they haven't confirmed Sensor Tower's numbers either.

## The headline numbers

Here's what the latest tracker update shows, per [TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/353934/gta-6-digital-pre-orders-climb-to-6-45-million-playstation-holds-74-share) and GameSpot:

- **About 6.45 million** digital pre-orders, worth around **$630 million** net of returns
- **PlayStation 5: 74%**, roughly 4.80 million copies and $468 million
- **Xbox Series X|S: 26%**, roughly 1.66 million copies and $162 million
- Up from about **6.08 million and $594 million** in the October 13 snapshot, so around 370,000 new pre-orders in one week

Do the math and the average pre-order works out to just under $98. That fits with reports that most buyers went for the $99.99 Ultimate Edition instead of the $79.99 Standard Edition. You can compare both in our [GTA 6 price, editions and platforms guide](/articles/gta-6-price-editions-platforms/).

## 148% ahead of Modern Warfare 2

The comparison that's getting the most attention comes from a Sensor Tower study first covered by GamesIndustry.biz. It lines up the pre-order curves of the biggest launches of the past six years: *Modern Warfare 2*, *Diablo 4*, *Hogwarts Legacy* and *Marvel's Spider-Man 2*.

[RockstarINTEL notes](https://rockstarintel.com/gta-6-pre-order-numbers-are-146-more-than-any-other-game/) that the window covers **149 to 45 days before launch**. Over that stretch, GTA 6 pulled in 148% more US digital pre-orders than MW2, the strongest performer in the group. MW2 went on to make $800 million in its opening weekend. RockstarINTEL adds that the Ultimate Edition on its own is more than 100% ahead of MW2.

The comparison only counts **US digital pre-orders**. Boxed copies and other countries aren't included. It's also a comparison of pacing at the same point before launch, not a final sales total.

## Why the real number is probably higher

Sensor Tower's data only covers digital storefronts. Physical editions of GTA VI come as a box with a download code rather than a disc, according to [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions), and those retail pre-orders aren't counted in the tracker. GameSpot adds that Sensor Tower expects most pre-orders to arrive in the final week before launch, with total pre-purchases possibly reaching **25 million**.

## What's next

- **November 12, 2026:** digital pre-load opens at local midnight ([how pre-load works](/articles/gta-6-preload-date/))
- **November 19, 2026:** GTA VI launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S
- Sensor Tower updates its tracker every week, so expect a new estimate before launch

Until Rockstar or Take-Two shares official figures, treat these numbers as a strong signal of demand rather than confirmed sales.
