If you open **Modern Warfare 4** this Sunday and one of your custom classes looks wrong, you're not imagining it. Overnight, the official [Call of Duty Updates account said](https://x.com/CODUpdates/status/2121841692986998795) the team is "investigating reports that players may rarely lose their customized Loadouts at the end of a match or when obtaining a Wager Match ticket." It lands in the middle of a busy weekend: [Shipment 24/7 is back](https://x.com/InfinityWard/status/2121472755099193849) and the first [Double XP weekend](https://x.com/CallofDuty/status/2121317012521836763) wraps up on **November 16**.

## The Loadout bug: what we know

The post went up early on **November 15** and is short on detail. Here is everything it confirms:

- It affects **Modern Warfare 4** custom Loadouts.
- Reports say the problem is **rare**.
- It can trigger at the **end of a match** or when you **earn a Wager Match ticket**.
- It is **under investigation**, with no fix or time frame announced yet.

There's no word on whether lost classes can be restored, so the safest move is simple: screenshot your favorite builds in the Gunsmith and Loadout screens before you play, so you can rebuild them quickly if they disappear. The Wager Match link matters because the event is still running; our [Wager Match event guide](/articles/mw4-warzone-wager-match-event-live-30-free-trophies-kastov-545-vitriol/) covers how tickets and trophies work.

## Shipment 24/7 and Hardcore Shipment are live

On the night of **November 13**, Infinity Ward [announced](https://x.com/InfinityWard/status/2121472755099193849) that **Shipment 24/7** and **Hardcore Shipment 24/7** are "NOW LIVE" in MW4. The tiny container map first [returned on October 27](/articles/mw4-shipment-returns-today-october-27-what-to-expect/), and the nonstop playlists are the classic way to level guns quickly. Infinity Ward hasn't said how long they will stay.

## Double XP ends November 16

That pairs well with the weekend boost. Call of Duty [confirmed](https://x.com/CallofDuty/status/2121317012521836763) that **Double Level XP and Double Weapon XP** are live **through November 16**. As [Dot Esports points out](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/guides/mw4-double-xp-weekend), this first event does **not** include Battle Pass XP, so tiers progress at the usual pace.

Stack it with the [Nov 13 weapon buffs](/articles/mw4-nov-13-update-less-flinch-m4-range-joint-fire-5x-xp/) that cut flinch and extended SMG and AR damage ranges, and this weekend is a good time to level the M4 or a newly buffed SMG.

## Joint Fire returns every Friday

One correction to what was known on launch day: Infinity Ward [clarified](https://x.com/InfinityWard/status/2121336059330204141) that the **Joint Fire** squad event "runs for 24 hours every Friday in Season 1." Party size still drives the boost, and you can make progress solo, just more slowly. So if you missed this Friday, you get another chance next week.

## More trophies in DMZ

DMZ players also got a quiet buff. Call of Duty Updates [confirmed](https://x.com/CODUpdates/status/2121482640511689066) that **trophy drop rates have been increased in DMZ playlists**, following the Multiplayer increase in the Nov 13 patch.

## What's next

- **November 16:** Double Level and Weapon XP ends.
- **Next Friday (November 20):** Joint Fire's next 24-hour window, based on Infinity Ward's weekly schedule.
- **TBA:** A fix or follow-up on the Loadout bug. We'll update when Infinity Ward posts one.
