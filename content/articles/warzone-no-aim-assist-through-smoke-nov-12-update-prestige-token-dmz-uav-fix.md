Smoke is real cover again. In the [Warzone Season 1 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) entry for **Thursday, November 12, 2026**, Raven Software says **aim assist is now disabled when shooting through smoke from Smoke Grenades**, even when gunfire or explosions punch gaps in the cloud. Infinity Ward pushed a matching [Modern Warfare 4 update](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) the same day, with no weapon balancing and a long list of Multiplayer and DMZ fixes.

## Warzone: smoke now blocks aim assist

It's the only gameplay change in the Warzone notes, but it matters for every controller player. Until now, aim assist could still help you track a target through Smoke Grenade smoke, especially once bullets thinned it out. That's gone: a gap in the cloud no longer brings it back.

As [TourneyTime points out](https://tourneytimenetwork.com/news/warzone-season-1-november-12-update-smoke-aim-assist/), the notes only name Smoke Grenades, so don't assume other smoke sources changed. Mouse and keyboard aim isn't affected. In practice, a well-placed smoke should once again protect a revive or a run across open ground.

## Warzone bug fixes, and owed Prestige tokens

- **Classic Prestige:** players who Prestiged on the Classic Prestige track now receive their prestige token. In a [follow-up post on X](https://x.com/CODUpdates/status/2121053806301429826), @CODUpdates said anyone who missed their **Permanent Unlock Token** "will be granted any owed Token(s) in an upcoming update", and that it will confirm once that's done.
- **Zodiac:** POI challenge progress now carries over between matches, so the completion reward can finally unlock.
- **Resurgence:** the category button shows squad size (1–4) and the 45-player count again.
- **Still under investigation:** squad HUD armor pips look dim unless that player's armor is full. It's a display bug, so read those pips with care for now.

## MW4 Multiplayer: Fast Hands and Wager Match

- **Fast Hands** now speeds up certain Tactical Equipment animations, as intended.
- **Wager Match trophies** no longer drop in Private Matches, and your trophy total no longer resets between rounds in round-based modes. Infinity Ward notes the UI will *still show* a reset, but your total keeps counting. Catch up on the event in our [Wager Match guide](/articles/mw4-warzone-wager-match-event-live-30-free-trophies-kastov-545-vitriol/).
- **Kill Block** Private Matches now allow up to **12 players**.

## DMZ fixes: UAV stealth, X01 insurance and the Grizzly

- **Thermal Padding** and an active **Sedative Inhaler** hide you from enemy UAVs boosted by the UAV Upgrade Trait again.
- Some **BlackCell camo challenges** now track correctly in DMZ.
- **Operator and weapon insurance** no longer stops working after you die in X01 having deployed there from Hajin. More on the zone in our [Update 1.112 recap](/articles/mw4-warzone-update-1-112-x01-uavs-disabled-dmz-scavenger-bonus/).
- **Grizzly** vehicles no longer keep driving after their driver is eliminated.
- Wager Match trophies in DMZ now drop at the intended rate.

## Fixes in both games

Both sets of notes list the same three global fixes: the **Schlager Indigo-RF** optic has its **sniper glint** back, weapon attachment pips show orange instead of white, and the Battle Pass no longer says "In Progress" once you've hit the maximum level. [COD France](https://www.cod-france.com/actualites/mw4-warzone-patch-notes-12-novembre-aim-assist-fumee/) also notes that no weapon was buffed or nerfed, so your current loadouts still hold.

## What's next

The big gunplay change this week remains the [3.91x hitscan range increase](/articles/mw4-hitscan-range-3-91x-ballistics-34ms-faster-ranked-season-2/). Next up is the promised update that grants owed Prestige tokens; Activision hasn't given a date yet. Infinity Ward says it keeps its Trello board updated with live issues and shares news on @CODUpdates, so watch there for the armor pip fix.
