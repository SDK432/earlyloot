YouTube is turning **Grand Theft Auto VI** launch day into a full-day broadcast. On **November 12, 2026**, YouTube and Rockstar Games [announced a 24-hour GTA VI launch livestream](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-rockstar-games-gta-vi-launch/) on the main **youtube.com/youtube** channel. It starts with a pre-show hosted by **RDCWorld** on the night of **November 18**, runs through release day on **November 19**, and comes with themed Live gifts, a Vice City Shorts effect and the official soundtrack on YouTube Music.

## The 24-hour launch stream

According to the [YouTube Blog](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-rockstar-games-gta-vi-launch/), the plan is:

- **Pre-show:** RDCWorld hosts from **11 PM ET on November 18**
- **Main stream:** 24 hours starting **12 AM ET on November 19**, so it wraps at 12 AM ET on November 20
- **Content:** live gameplay, creator podcasts and original shows
- **Finale:** live on-the-ground coverage from **Miami** with creators and special guests
- **Access:** a GTA VI-themed YouTube logo ("Yoodle") on the homepage links straight to the broadcast

YouTube says the stream is produced with **Entropico**.

## Who's streaming

YouTube names **Caylus, Zhong and Kat, Jelly, Rivers, Jake Baldino and Cinna** "and more". [GTA BOOM](https://www.gtaboom.com/gta-6-is-getting-a-24-hour-youtube-livestream-with-more-than-30-creators-5ad2) counts **more than 30 creators from over a dozen countries**, such as JuanSGuarnizo in Mexico and Windah Basudara in Indonesia. [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-youtube-collaborate-for-big-24-hour-livestream/) lists names from the X announcement, among them Loserfruit, GamerCosta and Kinda Funny Games. [TalkEsport](https://www.talkesport.com/news/total-gaming-payal-gaming-sharkshe-youtube-24-hour-gta-6-livestream/) points out the Indian creators Total Gaming, Payal Gaming and Sharkshe, and notes YouTube hasn't said how long each creator will be on.

## Free gifts, Shorts effect and soundtrack

Besides the stream, [YouTube is rolling out](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/youtube-rockstar-games-gta-vi-launch/):

- **Five limited-edition GTA VI Live gifts**, including **"Wanted Stars"** animations that appear on screen during streams. Eligible viewers watching select creator streams can claim and send one for free while supplies last.
- **"GTAVI: Visit Vice City"**, a Shorts effect with several Vice City backdrops for your own launch-week clips.
- **The official GTA VI soundtrack** on the YouTube Music homepage all launch week, including Morgan Wallen's "Last Thing You Need", next to a full GTA series anthology playlist.

## Why YouTube is going this big

YouTube's own data shows the **Grand Theft Auto series has passed 900 billion lifetime views** on the platform. Pre-launch videos about GTA VI alone have **topped 40 billion views**. YouTube's Culture & Trends team also put out a report on the series' cultural impact the same day.

The stream is part of a launch push that keeps growing. Pre-orders [have reportedly passed $1 billion](/articles/gta-6-1-billion-pre-orders-sensor-tower-xbox-take-two-10-year-deal/), and Rockstar is throwing [GTA VI: The Festival in Miami on November 21](/articles/gta-6-the-festival-miami-november-21-travis-scott-cardi-b-lottery/).
