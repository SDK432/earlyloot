Who is really steering GTA 6? According to [GamesRadar+, reporting on November 2, 2026](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-lead-says-rockstar-president-sam-houser-helped-shape-the-direction-of-the-game-and-is-involved-in-every-aspect-of-creative-development/) from Edge Magazine's GTA 6 cover story, Rockstar president and co-founder **Sam Houser** is deeply hands-on. Rockstar North co-studio head **Rob Nelson** says Houser helped set the game's direction from the start and is involved in every part of its creative development.

It's another piece of the big Edge feature that already gave us [12 new screenshots and the Justice Score](/articles/gta-6-edge-preview-12-screenshots-k9-police-criminal-profile-justice-score/), the [map size comparison](/articles/gta-6-map-size-gta-4-gta-5-rdr2-combined-600000-npc-animations/) and the news that you can [break up Jason and Lucia](/articles/gta-6-break-up-jason-lucia-relationship-rob-nelson/).

## Putting the "taste director" talk to rest

Houser rarely speaks in public, and that silence has fueled speculation that he has stepped back from day-to-day decisions. The theory held that he now acts more like a "taste director", the way record producer Rick Rubin guides artists, rather than a hands-on creative lead.

Nelson rejects that idea. Edge paraphrases most of his answer, but [GamesRadar+ notes](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-lead-says-rockstar-president-sam-houser-helped-shape-the-direction-of-the-game-and-is-involved-in-every-aspect-of-creative-development/) that he credits Houser with the game's original concept and overall direction. Nelson also says Houser stays closely tied to its creative choices throughout. In his own words:

> "Sam and I will identify problems or issues that we then solve with and across the teams."

## How the milestone reviews work

The most concrete detail is the review process. Per [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-lead-says-rockstar-president-sam-houser-helped-shape-the-direction-of-the-game-and-is-involved-in-every-aspect-of-creative-development/):

- Each time GTA 6 hits a new development milestone, Nelson spends **a couple of weeks** with Houser going through the build.
- Their feedback runs from small details like **lighting and audio** up to big questions about **game mechanics, tone and narrative**.
- The notes go out to the relevant teams. They work through the list, and then the cycle starts again at the next milestone.

That's a lot of senior attention for a game this large. No one person can check every corner of a map that Edge compares to GTA IV, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined. Still, the process puts Houser right in the middle of the decisions that shape how the game looks, plays and feels.

## Why it matters

Houser has been a creative force behind Grand Theft Auto for more than 30 years. Nelson's comments suggest GTA 6 isn't a hand-off to a new generation working under a hands-off boss. It's still built with direct input from the person most associated with the series. For fans worried about the departure of other long-time leaders over the years, that's a reassuring signal, even if Rockstar is still keeping the deeper details private.
