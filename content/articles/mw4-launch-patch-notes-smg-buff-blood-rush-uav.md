Modern Warfare 4 goes live tomorrow, **October 23**, and Infinity Ward has shown exactly how the game will play on day one. On October 21 the studio published the [full launch patch notes on callofduty.com](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-launch-patch-notes), covering weapon tuning, perks, movement, spawns and killstreaks since the Multiplayer Beta. The short version: SMGs get stronger, one-shot weapons get reined in, and Tactical Sprint now costs you a perk slot.

## Weapon balance: SMGs up, one-shots down

Infinity Ward says players clearly preferred the slower time-to-kill from Beta Weekend 2, so that TTK stays, but the [weapon balance needed more work](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-launch-patch-notes). For launch:

- **SMGs** are "more efficient in close to mid-range combat."
- **Powerful one-hit-kill weapons** are toned down with flinch and damage tuning.
- Every weapon got a "deep balance pass," and all attachments were retuned.
- **Advanced Weapon Stats** are in the game at launch, so you can compare numbers before building a class.

The arsenal is **33 weapons** in total, including five SMGs: the ISO Nightshade, VEL 46, Wz.55 Striga, PPSh-41 and X-58 Nyx.

## No more redundant unlocks

Attachments are shared. According to the [patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-launch-patch-notes), once you unlock a shared attachment it works right away on every weapon that can take it, so leveling each new gun gets faster. **Weapon Prestige** is back for players who want the old grind: prestiging a weapon locks its attachments and puts that gun on a classic level-by-level unlock path. A new Optic Rail Placement option also lets you move your sight for a cosmetic ADS tweak.

## Perks and Tactical Sprint

We covered the [Warzone Tactical Sprint debate](/articles/warzone-tactical-sprint-default-season-1/) earlier. In MW4 multiplayer, Infinity Ward is keeping the Beta change: **Tac Sprint is not available by default**. It now lives on **Blood Rush**, a Perk 2 option. To make up for the perk slot, Tac Sprint loses its extra sprint-out penalty, and the default input is "single tap run," so you Tac Sprint as long as stamina lasts. Double-tap and single-tap sprint options are still in Movement Advanced Settings.

Other perk buffs:

- **Battle Hardened** blocks more of the effects of Flash, Concussion and Screamer Mine.
- **Resupply** gives your first recharge faster.

Flash and Concussion grenades also get a slightly shorter range and duration.

## Spawns, UAVs and maps

Spawn logic has been [reworked across maps](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-launch-patch-notes) with a focus on player separation, squad spawns and Hardpoint predictability. **UAV health rises from 100 to 150**, while UAVs still cost 5 kills (4 with Hardline). The Wheelson also gets more aggressive targeting. At launch you get **20 multiplayer maps** (12 core 6v6, three Ground War, four Gunfight and Kill Block), over a dozen modes and **33 Operators**. For the full feature rundown, see our [MW4 multiplayer explainer](/articles/mw4-multiplayer-whats-new/).

## Known issues to watch

The notes also repeat this week's DMZ fixes, which we covered in our [early access patch report](/articles/mw4-patch-dmz-ai-zero-xp-fix-vrr-performance-mode/). Insider Gaming's [summary of the official Trello board](https://insider-gaming.com/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-known-issues/) lists seven open issues, including matchmaking quality, DLSS being unavailable while the VRRT Denoiser is on, and DMZ Free Loadouts loading in with no weapons.

## What's next

- **October 23:** Modern Warfare 4 launches worldwide, and the first in-game Event starts that same day.
- **Next week:** Infected and the new Possession mode arrive for Halloween.
- **Season 1:** the new Warzone and the shared camo grind. Weekly Challenges will not appear in Season 1, because Infinity Ward is focusing on Events.
