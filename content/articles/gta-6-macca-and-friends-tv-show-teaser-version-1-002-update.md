Grand Theft Auto VI's pink mascot finally has a show of his own. On **November 13, 2026**, the first teaser for **Macca & Friends**, an animated series that plays inside the game, went live as a DiscussingFilm exclusive, and [Rockstar Games shared it on its official X account](https://x.com/RockstarGames/status/2121287705258578290) a few hours later. The post's description reads: "Coming November 19 on WEAZEL." On the same day, some players got a roughly **1GB version 1.002 update** for the pre-loaded game.

## Who is Macca?

Macca the Gator is a pink alligator in sunglasses who lives in Leonida, GTA VI's take on Florida. According to the [original post](https://x.com/DiscussingFilm/status/2121241786525810907), the series "follows the adventures of Macca the Gator and his wetland friends across the state of Leonida." The outlets that watched the clip name the cast:

- **Macca**, the sunglasses-wearing pink gator in the lead role
- **Pinchie the crab**, Macca's landlord. [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-rockstar-macca-and-friends-animated-trailer/) jokes that he "is not actually his friend"
- **Chunkee the manatee**, who [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-in-game-tv-show-macca-friends-looks-ridiculous-in-the-best-way/) says wears a hat
- **An unnamed flamingo**
- **A human cop**, per Polygon

Macca already appeared in Rockstar's marketing before this teaser: the show was advertised in the Kaseya Center elevators, as covered in our [Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell piece](/articles/gta-6-burger-shot-cluckin-bell-kaseya-center-miami/). Polygon notes that a Macca figure and a Chunkee shot glass come in Rockstar's $399.99 Good Time State Vice City Collection, which doesn't include the game.

## What happens in the teaser

The plot is a very GTA idea in cartoon form: Macca has to come up with rent money, or his crab landlord will kick him out. GameSpot says the gang appears to dress up as cops and sneak into a police station. GameSpot guesses they're after cash in an evidence locker, but the clip doesn't say that.

[GamingBible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/gta-6-macca-and-friends-explained-rockstar-reveal-933637-20261113) describes the clip as about 30 seconds long, "pretty well animated" and fully voiced, with a BoJack Horseman feel. Polygon says it has "big Adult Swim energy." Both agree the show is aimed at adults.

The show will air on **Weazel**, the series' long-running parody TV network. Rockstar hasn't said how many episodes there are or where you'll watch them in the game. GameSpot and Polygon both think it could be a real multi-episode series, but that's their expectation, not something Rockstar has confirmed.

## Version 1.002 is rolling out now

[Polygon reports](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-nov-13-patch-102-what-is-download-size-ps5-xbox/) that on Friday morning, some players woke up to a notification for GTA VI **version 1.002**, a download of about **1GB**. Rockstar hasn't released patch notes. Polygon points out that notes for an unreleased game could include spoilers. Many fans think this is the day-one patch, but Rockstar hasn't said what it contains.

The update appears to be **staggered**, so not everyone will see it yet. Polygon thinks sending it out in batches could help launch day go more smoothly. If you haven't seen it, check your console's download queue before November 19. The base install is already **212GB**, so make sure you have room. Our [pre-load guide](/articles/gta-6-pre-load-live-30fps-ps5-pro-pssr-xbox-one-cloud/) covers the details.

## More launch-week teasing

The marketing push is speeding up. [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/gta-6-gets-new-in-game-video-recreating-real-world-footage-in-ocean-drive) reports that Rockstar shared a clip from a new "Only in Dade" account to its Instagram story. The in-game Ocean Drive clip appears to recreate real-world footage from a West Coast Customs collaboration.

## What's next

- **Now:** version 1.002 is rolling out in waves on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **November 18, 11 PM ET:** YouTube's [24-hour launch livestream](/articles/gta-6-24-hour-youtube-launch-livestream-30-creators-vice-city-shorts-live-gifts/) starts with its pre-show.
- **November 19:** GTA VI launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Macca & Friends starts airing on Weazel.
