We finally have a real answer to the biggest pre-launch question about Grand Theft Auto VI: how big is the map? Rockstar told [Edge magazine](https://www.gamesradar.com/edge/gtavi-cover/) that Leonida is **roughly twice the size of GTA V's map**, and that its total landmass, Vice City included, is **nearly as large as the playable maps of GTA IV, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined**. Since the feature went live, the figure has become the talking point of the week, picked up by [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6s-landmass-is-nearly-as-big-as-gta-4-gta-5-and-red-dead-redemption-2-combined/) and, on October 30, by [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-map-size-is-so-big-it-rivals-gta-4-gta-5-and-red-dead-redemption-2s-combined-and-rockstar-devs-are-still-discovering-places-theyve-never-seen/).

We covered the cops, K9 units and Criminal Profile in our [Edge preview breakdown](/articles/gta-6-edge-preview-12-screenshots-k9-police-criminal-profile-justice-score/). This piece is about scale.

## How big is Leonida?

Here's what Rockstar has actually said, and what it hasn't:

- **About 2x GTA V's map**, per [Edge](https://www.gamesradar.com/edge/gtavi-cover/)
- **Total landmass close to GTA IV + GTA V + RDR2 combined**, including Vice City and the surrounding regions
- **About three times Red Dead Redemption 2's map**, according to [GameSpot's](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6s-landmass-is-nearly-as-big-as-gta-4-gta-5-and-red-dead-redemption-2-combined/) report on Rockstar's comments
- **No official square-mile figure yet.** Any exact number you see online is fan math, not a confirmed stat

## "An order of magnitude more interiors"

Size isn't the real flex, Rockstar argues. Density is. Co-studio head Aaron Garbut told [Edge](https://www.gamesradar.com/edge/gtavi-cover/) that GTA 6 has "an order of magnitude more interiors" than the studio's earlier games, and that "every back alley is as densely detailed and interesting as the others."

Garbut also admitted that after years on the project he still walks into places he's never seen. On one session, he told Edge, he wandered into a house full of loving photos of the family dog, then found the next room trashed by that same dog. As [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-map-size-is-so-big-it-rivals-gta-4-gta-5-and-red-dead-redemption-2s-combined-and-rockstar-devs-are-still-discovering-places-theyve-never-seen/) notes, that's a direct answer to fans who found RDR2 full of locked doors.

His summary of the ambition: "I feel like we've built a pyramid on Everest."

## 600,000 animations for the people of Leonida

The crowds got the same treatment. Rather than relying mostly on procedural systems to build interchangeable pedestrians, Rockstar set up a **Los Angeles-based team of writers, directors and producers** just for Leonida's NPCs, [Edge reports](https://www.gamesradar.com/edge/gtavi-cover/). The studio art-directed every one of them and created **around 600,000 animations**, a number [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/news/rockstar-art-directed-600-000-animations-so-that-gta-6s-passers-by-feel-like-real-people) highlighted on October 31.

Each pedestrian gets personality traits that shape how they react:

- Temperament and aggression
- Where they are and what they've seen
- How you've treated them

Districts also have their own mix of demographics and jobs. In the demo, a man walking his dog didn't like Jason staring at him and snapped back, "What the f*** are you looking at?" It builds on what we already knew about Rockstar's [pedestrian dialogue writers](/articles/gta-6-pedestrian-dialogue-writers/).
