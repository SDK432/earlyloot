Two weeks out from launch day, [Rockstar Games](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-gallery) just opened **The Launch Gallery** — a fresh official media collection packed with brand-new screenshots of Vice City and Leonida. The page went live on **November 5, 2026**, with Rockstar's own pitch: "Explore Vice City and beyond through new screenshots." The same day, Rockstar Newswire published a post titled **"Explore the Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Gallery,"** summarizing that GTA VI is "now officially only two weeks away" and inviting players to enjoy "a stunning look at Vice City and beyond" on the official site.

## What's in The Launch Gallery

The gallery sits on the official GTA VI site at [rockstargames.com/VI/launch-gallery](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-gallery) (also linked from the [VI media hub](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/media)). It's a curated set of stills meant to show off the world right before launch — not a trailer drop, but a deep look at locations, characters, and everyday chaos across Leonida.

[Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-launch-update-screenshots-rockstar-games-nov-5/) counted **36 brand-new screenshots** in the collection (other outlets put the total closer to the high 30s). According to Polygon's write-up, the stills cover:

- **Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos** mid-heist and on the run — bank jobs, carjacking, and fleeing police with Leonida locals in the mix
- Nightlife across the state: dancing at beachside bars in the **Leonida Keys** and exclusive clubs in **Vice City**
- More of Leonida's supporting cast and everyday scenes
- Location highlights spanning **Vice City**, **Port Gellhorn**, the **Leonida Keys**, and the **Grassrivers**
- Fine detail on tattoo artwork, hairstyles, undersea creatures, and swamp folk
- A few cabaret / exotic-dancer shots that lean risqué (keep it PG when browsing at work)

This is a different drop from the [Edge magazine preview stills from late October](/articles/gta-6-edge-preview-12-screenshots-k9-police-criminal-profile-justice-score/), which showed a smaller set of screenshots tied to that cover story. The Launch Gallery is Rockstar's own official media dump ahead of day one.

## Why it matters with two weeks left

GTA VI launches on **November 19, 2026** for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Digital pre-load opens earlier — [November 10, with up to 212GB](/articles/gta-6-preload-moved-november-10-212gb/) to clear on your console. A big official screenshot gallery this close to launch is Rockstar's classic final marketing push: show the world, tease the vibe, and keep pre-order momentum going without spoiling the story.

If you've been waiting for more than trailers and TV spots, this is the cleanest place to browse high-res looks at Vice City and the wider state right now — straight from Rockstar, no datamines or leaks required.
