American teenagers are clearly ready for **Grand Theft Auto VI**, and most of them want to take it online. Investment bank Piper Sandler polled roughly **9,000 US teens**, and according to [GameSpot's November 4, 2026 report](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-survey-reveals-people-really-want-gta-online-but-it-hasnt-been-confirmed-yet/), **70%** of respondents who own a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S plan to buy the game. The catch: among those planning to buy, **90%** say they intend to play GTA Online, a mode Rockstar still hasn't announced for GTA VI.

## The key numbers

Here's what the survey found, per [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-survey-reveals-people-really-want-gta-online-but-it-hasnt-been-confirmed-yet/):

- **70%** of current-gen console owners plan to buy GTA 6.
- **55%** plan to buy it right away, while **15%** will wait several months.
- **90%** of would-be buyers intend to play GTA Online.
- **40%** expect to make additional in-game purchases.

These are statements of intent from a survey, not sales figures. Still, they line up with earlier data: GameSpot notes that Circana found nearly **one in three** US console and PC gamers plan to buy GTA 6, the highest purchase intent the research firm has ever measured.

## GTA Online for GTA 6 is not confirmed

That 90% figure is the headline, but it describes something that doesn't officially exist yet. As [GameSpot points out](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-survey-reveals-people-really-want-gta-online-but-it-hasnt-been-confirmed-yet/), retail listings describe GTA VI as a "single-player experience," and Rockstar has not announced an online mode for the new game. Given how GTA Online carried GTA V for more than a decade, expectations are understandable, but for now any multiplayer component remains unannounced.

## What the analyst took from it

[Vice Dispatch reports, citing the Piper Sandler note](https://gta6vicedispatch.com/news/gta-6-teen-survey-piper-sandler-70-percent/), that the survey points to an implied initial paying-user rate of about **44%**, which Piper considers higher than comparable platforms. The note also flags a wrinkle: roughly **21%** of the current-gen consoles in the survey belong to teens who own both a PS5 and an Xbox, which Piper treats as a drag on unit estimates, since one player can only count as one buyer.

Even so, according to the same report, analyst Callahan kept his expectation of **more than 45 million units** at launch, along with an overweight rating and a **$290 price target** on Take-Two. Vice Dispatch adds that Take-Two shares rose about 1.5% in premarket trading after the note.
