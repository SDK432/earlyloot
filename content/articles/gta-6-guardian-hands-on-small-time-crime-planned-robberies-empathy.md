Five days before launch, the most detailed hands-on look at Grand Theft Auto VI so far has landed. The Guardian's Keza MacDonald spent a day at Rockstar North in Edinburgh, and her cover feature in the paper's **Saturday magazine of November 14, 2026** describes a GTA that asks you to plan your crimes and look after the couple at its centre. [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/rockstar-knows-people-want-to-play-gta-6-for-the-chaos-but-the-studio-will-also-provide-mechanisms-that-encourage-empathy-for-the-first-time-in-a-gta/) and [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-gameplay-details-guardian-interview/) both summarised the piece today. Rockstar co-studio head **Rob Nelson** and senior exec **Jennifer Kolbe** walked her through Vice City.

Everything below comes from that demo and those interviews, as reported by the press. It isn't a leak, but Rockstar hasn't published these details itself.

## Jason and Lucia start small

Don't expect to steal a supercar in your first hour. According to [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-gameplay-details-guardian-interview/), Jason and Lucia begin by **hotwiring cheaper cars and picking basic locks**. Bigger jobs come later in the story. Nelson called them "a younger couple with their backs against the wall," which is why their early jobs are small and quiet rather than loud and bloody.

## Robberies reward planning

The hands-on describes heists you prepare for instead of just running in shooting. Before you hit a target, such as a gas station or a mall boutique, you can scope it out:

- **Disable the security cameras** or steal the footage
- **Find back doors** and **safe codes**
- Get in and out before witnesses show up

Skip the prep and things go wrong quickly. In the jewellery store robbery Rockstar showed the Guardian, a guard opened fire instead of surrendering, and the couple escaped on a motorbike with police cars and helicopters chasing them across the district. That fits what Rockstar told Edge about the police in our [Edge preview story](/articles/gta-6-edge-preview-12-screenshots-k9-police-criminal-profile-justice-score/).

## Empathy, "for those who want to"

The headline quote is about something the series is not known for. Asked whether GTA VI encourages empathy, Nelson said, per [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-empathy-first-time-rockstar-history/): "We've tried for the first time in a GTA to provide mechanisms that encourage it, for those who want to."

He was just as clear that chaos isn't going anywhere. "Crime is a fundamental part of GTA," he said, adding that Rockstar is "trying to make sure we're not punishing people for playing the game they are used to," but that "your actions will have some effect on how the game plays out."

## The relationship is a system

When Jason and Lucia are apart, they can **text and call** each other. When they're together, you can take them on dates. VICE lists the gym and a drink at a bar, and notes that a **liquor store robbery can count as a date too**. Neglecting the relationship has consequences. That builds on Nelson's earlier comments that the couple's relationship is yours to keep or lose, which we covered in [our break-up story](/articles/gta-6-break-up-jason-lucia-relationship-rob-nelson/).

## Small details

The demo also showed how far the world goes. VICE reports that Jason can ask a dog's owner if he can **pet the dog**, and that pedestrians react differently when you interrupt their conversations, from confusion to insults. Outlets that read the magazine say Jason used binoculars to log a gull in a **wildlife logbook with 62 bird types**. The [Guardian's earlier piece](https://www.theguardian.com/games/2026/nov/11/a-world-of-its-own-inside-the-making-of-the-most-detailed-grand-theft-auto-yet) traced that detail back to Miami research trips that started in 2015.
