Activision has published the full Season 1 breakdown for Modern Warfare 4, and it's a big one. According to the [official Call of Duty blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement), Season 1 goes live at **midnight ET on November 6** (9 PM PT on November 5) on all platforms, bringing a new DMZ location, new Multiplayer maps, four new weapons over the season and the full merge of MW4 into Warzone. We already covered [the roadmap tease and Shipment's return](/articles/mw4-roadmap-shipment-season-1-november-6/); this is the detailed content list.

## DMZ: the X01 Black Site

The headline for DMZ players is **X01**, a weapons facility seized by North Korean forces on the edge of Hajin. The [Call of Duty blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement) describes it as a former GRANIT Arms black site: cargo bays, vehicle depots, underground labs, production lines and a sealed Deep Vault, all linked by a maze of tunnels.

- **Shifting hazards and closing exfils:** as the facility starts sealing off extraction points, squads must complete set steps to get out.
- **New DMZ Orders**, with Ghost guiding Operators deeper into X01.
- **Threat Briefing** to take down DMZ's biggest Boss threats, plus **Resupply Runs**, per the [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/28/modern-warfare-4-season-1-everything-you-need-to-know-for-november-6-launch/).

## Multiplayer maps and modes

Three maps arrive at launch: **Backstab**, a snowy war-torn town; **Canyon**, a sun-baked ravine with a river and cliff paths; and a reimagined **Shoot House** set in North Korea. Mid-season adds **Ministry** and **Overflow**, plus the new **Counter Attack** mode.

On the mode side, **Grind** and **Drop Zone** return at launch, and **Gauntlet 1v1** Gunfight runs through the Wager Match Event. Before all that, the Halloween modes start now: **Possession** goes live today, October 29, and **Infected** follows on October 30, as we detailed in our [Halloween modes guide](/articles/mw4-halloween-possession-october-29-infected-october-30/).

## New weapons

- **CSG-35 SMG** (Battle Pass): Activision says it has the highest fire rate in its class.
- **Cronen Osprey Assault Rifle** (Battle Pass): built around speed with moderate power.
- **JAK 12 Shotgun** and **ZLR Talonhawk** melee weapon arrive later in the season, per the [Steam announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1938090/announcements/).

## A simpler Battle Pass with catch-up

The Season 1 Battle Pass is a **linear 100-tier track** with 100+ rewards. The big change is a catch-up system: premium owners who fall behind are automatically moved up to the reward that matches the current day of the season, **up to day 50**, guaranteeing 50+ rewards. The blog lists the premium pass at 1,100 COD Points, or 2,400 for the Bundle.

## Events

Season 1 events include the **Wager Match Event** (wager trophies in 1v1 Gunfight, winner stays on), **Weapon Camo Events** and a **Squad Up** event that boosts XP when you play with friends, with more planned at mid-season.

## Warzone integration

Warzone gets two new maps, **Hajin** and **Zodiac**, while **Verdansk** and **Rebirth Island** return as MW4 becomes fully integrated with the free-to-play game. Activision is holding the details back for a dedicated Warzone post and roadmap on the [Call of Duty blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement) on **Monday, November 2**.

## What's next

- **October 29:** Possession mode goes live.
- **October 30:** Infected returns.
- **November 2:** Full Warzone Season 1 intel and roadmap.
- **November 6, 00:00 ET:** Season 1 launches across MW4, DMZ and Warzone.
- **Mid-season:** Season 1 Reloaded brings Ministry, Overflow and Counter Attack; the JAK 12 and ZLR Talonhawk also land later in the season.
