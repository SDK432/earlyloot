Rockstar's **GTA 6** launch campaign just reached the biggest game in club football. During the LaLiga **El Clásico** between FC Barcelona and Real Madrid on **Sunday, October 25**, the pitchside LED boards at Spotify Camp Nou ran Grand Theft Auto VI ads several times, each ending with the game's title and the line **"Coming November 19,"** as [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-ads-el-clasico-football-match/) and Spanish outlet [Vandal](https://vandal.elespanol.com/noticia/2026298011/gta-6-se-estrena-en-espana-durante-el-clasico-rockstar-anuncia-su-juego-en-el-fc-barcelona-real-madrid/) report.

## What appeared on the boards

This wasn't a commercial break spot. It was the touchline advertising that TV viewers see all through live play. According to [Vandal](https://vandal.elespanol.com/noticia/2026298011/gta-6-se-estrena-en-espana-durante-el-clasico-rockstar-anuncia-su-juego-en-el-fc-barcelona-real-madrid/), the ad looped as a short sequence of images that ended on the release date.

- **Where:** pitchside LED boards at Spotify Camp Nou, Barcelona
- **When:** El Clásico on October 25, 2026, kicking off at 9pm local time
- **Message:** the Grand Theft Auto VI title plus "Coming November 19"
- **Confirmation:** none from Rockstar so far. [thegta6news](https://thegta6news.com/news/gta-6-el-clasico-ad) notes the studio hadn't posted about it, and that fans spotted it on X before any outlet did

Vandal calls it Rockstar's biggest marketing campaign in Spain to date. Beebom points out that clips from Brazilian broadcaster CazéTV's feed spread quickly once the match ended. On the pitch, Real Madrid won 2-1, with Kylian Mbappé scoring twice.

## How many people saw it?

That's hard to say. Beebom and Vandal say the match **reportedly** draws around **650 million viewers**, a figure that gets quoted for every Clásico. As [thegta6news](https://thegta6news.com/news/gta-6-el-clasico-ad) points out, nobody has a firm, sourced number yet. Even so, Rockstar could hardly pick a bigger stage for a few seconds of board time.

## Part of a sports-heavy rollout

Rockstar has a reputation for barely advertising, but the El Clásico boards are the latest in a run of sports and real-world placements over the past week:

- **NBA on NBC (Oct 20):** GTA 6's [first TV ad aired during NBA opening night](/articles/gta-6-tv-ad-nba-alternate-launch-trailer-cut/), a trimmed cut of the launch trailer. [Marketing Newsroom](https://marketingnewsroom.com/brands/creative/gta-vi-ads-el-clasico-nba-live-sport/) says the boards appeared five days after that first TV sighting.
- **Miami Heat arena:** real [Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell stands opened at the Kaseya Center](/articles/gta-6-burger-shot-cluckin-bell-kaseya-center-miami/).
- **Creator content:** GTA 6 became the [presenting sponsor of Druski's Ali G episode](/articles/gta-6-druski-ali-g-sponsorship-xbox-launch-trailer-late/) on YouTube.

According to [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-ads-el-clasico-football-match/), Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said in September that GTA 6 still needs aggressive marketing despite the hype. Beebom also mentions the Miami Heat's GTA-themed "A Night in Vice City" game against the Milwaukee Bucks on November 18. The demand looks real either way: Sensor Tower [estimated 6.45 million digital pre-orders](/articles/gta-6-pre-orders-6-45-million-sensor-tower/) earlier this month, though that's a third-party estimate, not an official number.

## What's next

- **November 12:** preloading opens

- **November 19:** GTA 6 launches on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S
