Embark Studios pushed **ARC Raiders Live Update 2.3.0** on Tuesday, October 27, and it brings the event we [previewed last week](/articles/arc-raiders-souls-offering-raider-project-october-27/) to life. Per the [official patch notes](https://arcraiders.com/news/live-update-2-3-0), the update adds the **Soul's Offering** player project, the new **Noxious Set** and a long list of balance changes and fixes. Restart your game to download it.

## Soul's Offering is now live

The new player project asks Speranza to stop and honor Toledo's fallen. In Embark's words, every Raider "who pauses in reverence shares in the Souls Offering," as the [official trailer posted by IGN](https://in.ign.com/video/275433/arc-raiders-official-souls-offering-new-player-project-trailer) puts it.

[Destructoid's breakdown](https://www.destructoid.com/all-souls-offering-level-requirements-in-arc-raiders-and-how-to-get-them/) lists four stages:

- **Vaulto's Offering:** Dead Flowers, Rock Salt, 15 ARC Alloy and an Expired Respirator.
- **Ferrovia's Offering:** more Dead Flowers, Railway Lanterns, 15 Advanced ARC Powercells and 10 Remote Raider Flares.
- **Murteta's Offering:** Fancy Plates, a Mask Fragment, Insulation Rolls and Fertilizer.
- **Honor the Fallen:** set off a firework on the roofs of the Santa Maria Houses.

Destructoid says each level pays out rewards, with a Legendary Display Table blueprint for finishing, and that the project runs **until November 16**.

## Heavy Shield and Flame Spray buffs

The biggest balance changes, per [Embark](https://arcraiders.com/news/live-update-2-3-0):

- **Heavy Shield** base charge goes from **80 to 90**. Embark says usage is "still relatively low" and promises to watch the change closely.
- **Flame Spray** damage rises to **13/s (from 10/s)**, and its durability drain is cut by 50%.
- **Firefly Burner** now ignites on impact when thrown, leaving a fire trail before it explodes about 3 seconds later.
- **Adrenaline Shot** can be used at max stamina. It no longer stacks; a second shot refreshes the timer.

## Emperor, Frigate and loot changes

- Containers near the **Emperor's** entrance now hold lower-tier loot, with better rewards deeper inside. The Frigate gets the same treatment.
- Dolabra, Bobcat, Equalizer, Jupiter, Tempest and Vulcano blueprints can now drop in any major map condition, at a slightly lower chance.
- The Emperor Key blueprint drops less often from Redirection pods.
- Furniture Design drop rates were inverted, so rarer designs now drop less often.
- The **Expedition Vault** is being disabled. Items move to your stash, and purchased slots are refunded with Bronze Statuettes.

The Grappling Hook also gets a batch of fixes for wild launches, and Pendola Pass gets more collision fixes around the fallen Emperor. There's also a workaround for a ray tracing crash on AMD RX 7000 GPUs.

## Noxious Set and a new patch rhythm

The update also adds the **Noxious Set** to the store. [Gfinity Esports reports](https://www.gfinityesports.com/article/arc-raiders-update-2-3-0-patch-notes) that it costs 1,600 Raider Tokens. Embark also says the weekly fix drops that followed Frozen Trail are ending: from now on, expect **fixes every three weeks** and **store updates weekly**.
