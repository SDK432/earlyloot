Rockstar has locked in the essentials for **Grand Theft Auto VI**: release date, platforms, price and editions. All of it is from the [official pre-order announcement](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi) and [Rockstar Support](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/4QfG4FmZCf5W1gS8jy4UVT/grand-theft-auto-vi-platform-editions-and-versions).

## Release date

**November 19, 2026.** Pre-orders opened at midnight local time on June 25, 2026, and pre-loading starts on November 12.

## Platforms

- **PlayStation 5**
- **Xbox Series X|S**

Those are the only platforms Rockstar has announced. A PC version **hasn't been announced**, and there's no version for PS4 or Xbox One.

## Price and editions

| Edition | Price (USD) |
|---|---|
| Grand Theft Auto VI | $79.99 |
| Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition | $99.99 |

Rockstar says the **Ultimate Edition** adds "an exclusive collection of premium vehicles, weapons, apparel, and action threaded across all aspects of Jason and Lucia's story."

## Pre-order bonuses

- **Vintage Vice City Pack:** included with all pre-orders and purchases before **November 20, 2026**
- **One free month of GTA+:** for digital pre-orders

## Digital vs physical

Both editions are available digitally through the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Games Store and global retailers. The **physical version contains a download code and no disc**, and Rockstar says it will be available from November 12 so buyers can pre-load.

## What is GTA VI about?

From Rockstar's official description: it's set in **Vice City, USA**, and follows **Jason and Lucia**. When "an easy score goes wrong", they end up in the middle of a conspiracy stretching across the state of **Leonida**.
