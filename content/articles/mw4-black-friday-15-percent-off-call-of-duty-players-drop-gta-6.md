**Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is already on sale.** About a month after its October 23 launch, the standard edition is 15% off for Black Friday, dropping from $69.99 to $59.49 on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4435490/), where the store page says the offer ends December 1. The timing is what has the community talking: the discount, [reported by EGW on November 24](https://egw.news/gaming/news/38675/modern-warfare-4-gets-15-off-for-black-friday-XDXMKu7oL), lands right after an analyst estimated that Call of Duty's daily player count fell off a cliff when GTA 6 arrived on November 19.

## What the deal looks like

According to [EGW](https://egw.news/gaming/news/38675/modern-warfare-4-gets-15-off-for-black-friday-XDXMKu7oL), Activision's promo tells players to "assemble the squad" and pitches:

- 20+ multiplayer maps
- DMZ, the extraction mode
- "Endless customization" for your arsenal

The banner also leans on review quotes, including GameRant's "The best COD in years" and TechRadar Gaming's "A generational Call of Duty game." Steam's $59.49 price is confirmed. On PlayStation and Xbox, the final price and end date may vary by platform and region, so check your store before you buy.

## The number that made this a story

Alinea Analytics' head of market analysis Rhys Elliott estimates that Call of Duty HQ daily active users dropped 42% when GTA 6 launched, "from an average of 7.5M DAUs to 4.4M," as quoted by [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/analyst-says-call-of-duty-lost-an-estimated-44-percent-of-its-players-when-gta-6-came-out-and-unlike-fans-of-its-live-service-competitors-theyve-been-slow-to-come-back/). [RG.org](https://rg.org/news/esports/call-of-duty-dau-fell-42-percent-after-gta-vi-alinea-estimates) adds that PlayStation, CoD's main platform, took a 44% hit.

Worse, recovery has been slow. Elliott said CoD had "barely bounced back after four days" and was still 41% below baseline, while Fortnite and EA Sports FC 27 were already climbing back, per [This Week in Video Games](https://thisweekinvideogames.com/news/analyst-claims-gta-vi-sales-hit-44-9-million-causes-engagement-drops-for-cod-and-fortnite/). Important: these are Alinea's **estimates**, not official numbers from Activision.

## Panic button or business as usual?

**The case for "panic":** MW4 was [Circana's No. 1 US game in October](/articles/mw4-best-selling-game-october-circana-misses-2026-top-10/) but still sits outside 2026's year-to-date top 10. Add an estimated 42% DAU drop, and a quick discount looks like Activision trying to win back the attention GTA 6 is eating.

**The case for "standard practice":** This has happened before. In 2023, Modern Warfare III was 15% off on the Xbox and PlayStation stores for Black Friday when it was [roughly two weeks old](https://gamingbolt.com/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-3-discounted-on-xbox-and-playstation-stores-for-black-friday). By that measure, a 15% cut a month after launch is right on schedule.

The truth is probably somewhere in between. Black Friday discounts are routine, but this year the deal lands in a very different market.

## Activision's engagement push

The sale isn't the only lure. The [Thanksgiving Duty Roster](/articles/mw4-warzone-thanksgiving-duty-roster-kill-leaderboard-points-war-chest-ends/) kill leaderboard gives out free rewards, and the Wager Match: Gauntlet 1v1 mode has doubled payouts, [GamingBible reports](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-free-rewards-558414-20261124). The same November 23 update also brought the [DMZ damage nerf on 18 guns](/articles/mw4-dmz-ttk-nerf-damage-cut-nov-23-update-every-gun/).

## What's next

- **December 1:** Steam's 15% offer ends; console end dates may differ.
- **December 4:** The Call of Duty League's 2027 season kicks off on MW4, [per RG.org](https://rg.org/news/esports/call-of-duty-dau-fell-42-percent-after-gta-vi-alinea-estimates).
- **Next DAU data:** Watch for Alinea's follow-up numbers to see whether Black Friday and the Thanksgiving events bring players back.
