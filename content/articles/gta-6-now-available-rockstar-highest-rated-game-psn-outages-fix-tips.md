Rockstar has made it official: [Grand Theft Auto VI is now available](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rockstar-games-announces-grand-theft-auto-vi-now-available) on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. In a November 19 press release, the studio says the game "launches as the highest rated game of all time." But PlayStation Network had a rough 24 hours around the release, and Sony hasn't said why.

## Rockstar's launch-day announcement

Rockstar and Take-Two say the game is sold through the PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, the Rockstar Games Store and global retailers. Founder Sam Houser said in the release that the game "represents everything we have been working towards since we first started making games."

The release also leans on critics, quoting the New York Times, The Washington Post, Bloomberg, IGN, Eurogamer, The Guardian and Polygon. IGN's line: "Unprecedented. Unbelievable. Unmissable." Rockstar adds that the Grand Theft Auto series has sold nearly 480 million units, with GTA V alone at nearly 235 million.

For the Metacritic numbers, the sales estimates and the performance-mode news, see our [launch-eve breakdown](/articles/gta-6-out-now-99-metacritic-17-million-sold-no-performance-mode/). The soundtrack is covered in our [full album tracklist](/articles/gta-6-the-album-full-tracklist-dua-lipa-lana-del-rey-playboi-carti/); Rockstar's release says it goes live at midnight on streaming services.

## PSN had two disruptions around launch

According to [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-psn-back-online-gta-6-outages/), PSN suffered two disruptions on November 18 and 19. On November 18, players reported failed sign-ins, and CT Insider counted more than 1,800 Downdetector complaints at one point. Those are user reports, not a count of affected accounts.

On November 19, outage monitor IsDown recorded roughly 24 minutes of degraded Gaming and Social services starting at 5:34 a.m. UTC. Both incidents are resolved, TechRepublic says. Sony hasn't explained the cause or tied it to GTA 6 traffic, so treat any link as unconfirmed.

## How to fix download and launch problems

If PSN is up and the game still won't start, TechRepublic relays Sony's troubleshooting guidance:

- Pause and resume a stalled download from Downloads/Uploads, or cancel and restart it from Game Library.
- Check for a system software update under Settings > System > System Software.
- Make sure you have enough storage (Sony advised reserving 212 GB, per TechRepublic) and that you're signed into the account that bought the game.
- If another account owns it, enable Console Sharing and Offline Play under Settings > Users and Accounts > Other. [Sony's support page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/gta-vi-code/) confirms this step.
- For a padlock icon, use Restore Licenses in the same menu.

## What about Xbox One?

Xbox One owners can stream the game through Xbox Cloud Gaming, [Xbox Wire confirms](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/). Streaming started at 1 a.m. ET on November 19 and uses Xbox Series S graphics settings.
