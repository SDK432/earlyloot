**Did you know GTA 6's radio is hiding a song nobody had heard before?** Players driving around Vice City have found "Kamikazi," a never-released track by the late R&B singer D'Angelo, playing on the **Worldwide FM** station. [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/dangelo-unreleased-song-kamakazi-grand-theft-auto-6-1235661667/) reported on November 20 that DJ Dojah, a longtime D'Angelo collaborator, confirmed the song is really his and that its title is "Kamikazi." It's one of more than 800 songs on the soundtrack, [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-dangelo-song-rockstar-games-radio/) notes.

## Where to hear it in GTA 6

- **"Kamikazi" (unreleased):** it plays on **Worldwide FM**. You can't pick the song, so keep the station on while you drive and wait for it to come up.
- **"Lady" (1995):** [Paste](https://www.pastemagazine.com/music/dangelo/unreleased-dangelo-song-kamikazi-featured-in-gta-vi) points out that D'Angelo's classic hit also plays on **Honey FM**. [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-lists/gta-vi-best-music-1235661734/) says that station is hosted by Chilli and T-Boz of TLC.

Don't count on YouTube for now. [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/dangelo-unreleased-song-kamakazi-grand-theft-auto-6-1235661667/) reports that Sony Music Entertainment, which signed D'Angelo to its RCA label, has been filing takedown notices against uploads of "Kamikazi." Right now the game is the only official place to hear it.

## The Red Dead Redemption 2 connection

This isn't D'Angelo's first Rockstar game. In 2018 the famously private singer recorded a new song, **"Unshaken,"** for [Red Dead Redemption 2](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/dangelo-unreleased-song-kamakazi-grand-theft-auto-6-1235661667/). [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-dangelo-song-rockstar-games-radio/) says it plays during a key story moment and has become one of the game's most iconic tracks. Polygon also says he performed it live with Rockstar at the 2021 Tribeca Film Festival.

He was a fan long before he was a contributor. Rockstar's Director of Music and Audio, Ivan Pavlovich, once told [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/dangelo-unreleased-song-kamakazi-grand-theft-auto-6-1235661667/) that D'Angelo would come to the studio's New York office "at midnight" and play the game "until four in the morning." Pavlovich added: "I have never seen someone that excited."

## A posthumous first look

D'Angelo died of pancreatic cancer in October 2025, more than a year before GTA 6 launched. [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/dangelo-unreleased-song-kamakazi-grand-theft-auto-6-1235661667/) says nobody knows yet whether "Kamikazi" was recorded for GTA 6 or came from the music he left behind. Either way, it's the first track out of his vault. His estate and collaborators have talked about a full album of unheard songs, a covers record and a B-sides collection.

Rockstar hasn't said anything about the track, according to [Paste](https://www.pastemagazine.com/music/dangelo/unreleased-dangelo-song-kamikazi-featured-in-gta-vi). That fits the way the game hides it: no announcement, just a song waiting on the radio for whoever tunes in.

## What's next

- **An official release?** [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-dangelo-song-rockstar-games-radio/) says it's unknown how RCA plans to release "Kamikazi" outside the game.
- **More from the vault:** the unreleased album, covers record and B-sides mentioned by D'Angelo's estate have no release dates yet, per [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/dangelo-unreleased-song-kamakazi-grand-theft-auto-6-1235661667/).
- **Until then:** tune to Worldwide FM and keep driving.
