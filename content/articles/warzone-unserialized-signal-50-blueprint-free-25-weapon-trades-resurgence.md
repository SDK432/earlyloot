**Warzone Season 1 is hiding a free sniper Blueprint, and you don't need a Battle Pass to get it.** [Game Rant reports](https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-cod-warzone-modern-warfare-4-unserialized-sniper-blueprint-free-how-to-get/) (November 8) that a secret challenge unlocks the **"Unserialized" Blueprint for the Signal 50** once you complete **25 trades at Weapon Trade Stations** in Resurgence. [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/call-of-duty/call-of-duty-warzones-new-zodiac-map-secret-challenges-calling-cards-weapon-blueprints-and-how-to-get-them) lists the same 25-trade requirement in its Zodiac secrets roundup. Activision hasn't announced the challenge in its patch notes, so treat it as a community-found Easter egg.

New to the season? Our [Season 1 roadmap](/articles/warzone-season-1-roadmap-hajin-zodiac-verdansk-rebirth-switch-2/) covers Hajin, Zodiac and everything else that arrived on November 6.

## How the secret challenge works

According to [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-cod-warzone-modern-warfare-4-unserialized-sniper-blueprint-free-how-to-get/), the hint is a note inside a small building just south of the **Depot** POI on Zodiac. It tells players to "keep up with the arms race by making 25 trade-ins." Reading the note is optional. The trades count either way.

- **Goal:** 25 weapon trades at Weapon Trade Stations, in total.
- **Where:** Game Rant says the stations appear on every Resurgence map, so you aren't locked to Zodiac. They're marked on the mini-map and the tac map.
- **Across matches:** progress carries over. [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/call-of-duty/call-of-duty-warzones-new-zodiac-map-secret-challenges-calling-cards-weapon-blueprints-and-how-to-get-them) notes that it doesn't matter what you trade or how many matches it takes.
- **Cooldown:** each station goes on a **30-second cooldown** after use, per Game Rant.
- **Completion:** an on-screen alert pops up when you reach 25.

## Fastest way to grind it

Game Rant's route is simple. Drop near a station, grab any floor weapon and trade it. While that station cools down, run to the next one and repeat. Resurgence respawns make this low-risk, and Casual playlists or the 20-player [Rally Point mode on Zodiac](/articles/warzone-rally-point-zodiac-buy-nuke-1-million-season-1/) should give you quieter games for farming.

## What the Weapon Trade Station does

The official [Warzone Season 1 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) list the Weapon Trade Station as a new **Resurgence** feature. It swaps your equipped weapon for "a random Weapon of higher rarity," and **Fire Sales** improve the value of each trade. So the grind also upgrades your loadout as you go. Per the [Season 1 blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1), Resurgence currently runs on Zodiac, with Rebirth Island returning later in the season.

## Why the Signal 50 unlock matters

In the official notes, the Signal 50 is a .50 Cal anti-materiel rifle that normally unlocks at **Player Level 35**. Its Apex attachment, Signal Inferno, adds incendiary rounds for extra vehicle and killstreak damage.

Game Rant reports that finishing the challenge also unlocks the Signal 50 itself in **every mode** if you don't have it yet. That includes the **Gunsmith in the DMZ FOB**, with no Weapon Manual needed. Windows Central describes the DMZ part as a one-time free craft. The Blueprint works in both Warzone and Modern Warfare 4.
