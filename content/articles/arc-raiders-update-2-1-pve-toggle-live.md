It's live. [Store Update 2.1.0](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-1-0), posted today by Embark Studios, is rolling out right now and officially starts the **PvE matchmaking toggle beta** in ARC Raiders. The Carrion Set is in the store, and the patch ships a long list of fixes for the Grappling Hook, Pendola Pass, Frigate, Reward Pass and more.

We covered the [day-before rules brief](/articles/arc-raiders-pve-toggle-goes-live-october-13/) yesterday. This is the go-live post: the build is out, and you can queue PvE today.

## PvE toggle beta is on

Embark writes that Update 2.1 "marks the start of the PvE toggle Beta Test" and asks Raiders to jump in and share feedback. Per the [October 12 brief](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-what-to-know), the window runs **October 13 at 11:00 CEST through October 20 at 11:00 CEST** (5:00 AM ET both days).

Quick refresh of the PvE rules:

- Zero player-to-player damage; stun and fire throwables don't hit other Raiders
- Lower overall loot value on PvE servers
- Mixed solo/duo/trio sessions
- No PvE option for Night Raid, Locked Gate, Hidden Bunker, Frigate or Redirection
- Eight-second grace period after being downed before ARC can re-acquire you

When the week ends, the toggle comes off. Embark says player feedback plus in-game data will decide whether it becomes permanent.

## Carrion Set and Zenith fix

Alongside the mode test, the [official notes](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-1-0) confirm the **Carrion Set** is available in the store. Players who finished their fifth Expedition also get the missing **Zenith Outfit** toggles that were previously withheld.

Equipped weapon stencils now show rarity in the weapon tooltip, and the customization screen adds a new indicator so freshly acquired stencils are easier to spot.

## Grappling Hook and Frigate changes

The Grappling Hook gets a meaningful pass. Auto-vault is more reliable on ledges and roofs (especially on Pendola Pass), the rope wraps around geometry in flight, and several detach and durability bugs are fixed — including hooks that broke instead of becoming "broken" at 0 durability, and lines that snapped on the Ballistic Gondola.

On the ARC side, Frigate hatches now inflict **burning damage over time** when they open. Frigate cargo collision and drop knockouts are smoothed, and Frigate round spawns are adjusted so you don't start too far from the action.

## Pendola Pass, Reward Pass and skills

Pendola Pass gets collision, lighting and extraction fixes — including a nasty bug where players could be eliminated while extracting inside the Gondola. Night lighting is darker with less visibility. Gondola markers on the compass and map now show out to **175 meters** (up from 120).

The Reward Pass no longer mis-counts milestones from previous rounds on the end-of-round screen, and several UI polish items land with it. Broad Shoulders, Field Repair, Fight or Flight and Sprinting on Fumes all receive skill fixes; stamina consumables can no longer bypass the Sprinting on Fumes penalty. Memory use is reduced on Spaceport, Buried City and Dam Battlegrounds, with a callout for Xbox Series S.

[MP1st](https://mp1st.com/title-updates-and-patches/arc-raiders-patch-2-1-starts-pve-toggle-beta-update-1-000-064) notes console players will see this as update **1.000.064**.

## What's next

- **Now through Oct 20, 5:00 AM ET:** try both PvE and PvPvE, then send feedback when Embark's survey lands
- **Oct 20:** the toggle is removed unless Embark extends or keeps it
- Keep grinding [Trials Season 6: Frozen Trials](/articles/arc-raiders-trials-season-6-frozen-trials/) while the PvE week runs

For the full changelog — animation, audio, quests, Outpost and Hairpin Semi-Auto Amplification — read Embark's [Store Update 2.1.0 post](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-1-0). If you sat on Frozen Trail waiting for a calmer queue, today is the day to log in.
