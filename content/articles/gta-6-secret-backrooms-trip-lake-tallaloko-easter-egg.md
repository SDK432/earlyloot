Did you spot this one? Tucked inside a shipping container on a quiet lakeside work site, GTA 6 hides one of its weirdest secrets yet: an item marked only with "???" that drops Jason into a surreal, dreamlike obstacle course. [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-where-to-find-the-backrooms-dimension/) calls it a trip through the Backrooms, and on November 22 [FinalBoss.io](https://finalboss.io/gta-6-backrooms-location) confirmed it's worth the detour: finish it and Jason walks away with **more max stamina and a new tattoo design**.

Best of all, you don't have to wait. Game Rant says you can reach it [shortly after the prologue](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-where-to-find-the-backrooms-dimension/), and [GTA6 LIFE](https://gta6-life.com/en/guides/gta-6-mushroom-trip/) points to a Postmode video from November 20 showing the whole run done in Chapter 1.

## Where the "???" box is hiding

The entrance is an open shipping container on the [east side of Lake Tallaloko in Lummox County](https://finalboss.io/gta-6-backrooms-location), north of Millport. Game Rant describes the spot as an [excavation site to the right of the lake](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-where-to-find-the-backrooms-dimension/), with the container on the right side of the site.

What you're looking for sits on a workbench inside. GTA6 LIFE describes it as a [takeaway box with an eye painted on it](https://gta6-life.com/en/guides/gta-6-mushroom-trip/), and the interaction prompt simply reads "???". (Game Rant describes the item as [a pill](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-where-to-find-the-backrooms-dimension/), so outlets don't fully agree on what's inside the box.)

## How to find it, step by step

1. **Drive north of Millport** to the little work site on the [east bank of Lake Tallaloko](https://finalboss.io/gta-6-backrooms-location), near the bend where the access road curves along the lake.
2. **Ignore the crowd outside.** By day you may see [NPCs drinking outside the container](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-where-to-find-the-backrooms-dimension/). GTA6 LIFE places them [around a barbecue beside an M7 gang operation](https://gta6-life.com/en/guides/gta-6-mushroom-trip/) and suggests slipping past them.
3. **Enter the open container** next to the wooden pallets. A lantern at the back [lights up the workbench](https://finalboss.io/gta-6-backrooms-location).
4. **Use the "???" prompt.** Heads up: the container door [closes behind you](https://finalboss.io/gta-6-backrooms-location), so make sure you have time for the whole run.

## What happens inside

Once the view starts to warp, Jason follows a container corridor into a [large pink-lit room](https://finalboss.io/gta-6-backrooms-location). From there it's pure parkour:

- **Climb.** Scale stacked containers, pallets and timber, then use the container roofs to reach a [glowing white doorway](https://finalboss.io/gta-6-backrooms-location).
- **Dive.** The corridor drops into water, where you [swim toward the jellyfish](https://finalboss.io/gta-6-backrooms-location) through flooded containers.
- **Walk the weird stuff.** GTA6 LIFE describes [walkways of fire and flowers](https://gta6-life.com/en/guides/gta-6-mushroom-trip/); FinalBoss.io has you passing through an overgrown tunnel and [between flames](https://finalboss.io/gta-6-backrooms-location).
- **Take the leap.** At the end, a dancing Jason [waves you over from below](https://gta6-life.com/en/guides/gta-6-mushroom-trip/). Hit the jump prompt to drop and end the trip.

## Your rewards

- **Max stamina boost.** You get a ["Max stamina increased" message](https://finalboss.io/gta-6-backrooms-location) when you wake up at the workbench.
- **A new tattoo.** The unlocked design is [available at tattoo parlors](https://finalboss.io/gta-6-backrooms-location).
- **Lost time.** Game Rant notes that when you step out, [a lot of time has passed](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-where-to-find-the-backrooms-dimension/) in the game world.

## Did you know?

The "Backrooms" name isn't Rockstar's. It's a nod to the [internet liminal-space horror](https://gta6wiki.eu/easter-eggs/backrooms-excavation-site-trip/) of endless, empty rooms, and that's what outlets are comparing this trip to. GTA6 LIFE calls it the [GTA 6 mushroom trip](https://gta6-life.com/en/guides/gta-6-mushroom-trip/).
