Cassio is back in Speranza next week, and this time he is bringing firepower. The official ARC Raiders account's [weekly radio brief, posted Sunday, November 15, 2026](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2122011751508263276), says the trader "returns with some fresh loot and plenty of firepower to boot." The attached graphic covers **November 17 to 23** and lists three headline Nomad offers: the **Lemons** backpack charm, the **Deadline** quick-use item and the **Anvil II** hand cannon. The week's Trials send Raiders back up to the snow of Pendola Pass.

## Nomad offers: Lemons, Deadline and Anvil II

According to the [weekly graphic](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2122011751508263276), Cassio's rotation costs:

- **Lemons (backpack charm):** 90K Epic/Legendary ARC parts + a firearm
- **Deadline (Epic quick use):** 30K Epic/Legendary ARC parts + a firearm
- **Anvil II (Rare hand cannon):** 20K Epic/Legendary ARC parts + a firearm

As usual, the graphic adds "& more," so expect a few extra items when the shop flips. Embark doesn't say what they are.

**Lemons** is the "zesty new Backpack Charm" from the post, and at 90K it is the priciest item on the list. It's purely cosmetic, and it's the one to save for if you collect charms. That follows the [Gas Mask charm from last week](/articles/arc-raiders-next-week-nov-10-16-gas-mask-angry-mask-fragment/).

**Deadline** is almost certainly the "explosive payload to cause some mayhem Topside" that Embark teases. The [community wiki](https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Deadline) describes it as an Epic mine on a fixed 6-second timer. When it goes off, it deals 1,000 damage to anything within a 10m radius, and the wiki warns that's lethal to Raiders caught without cover.

**Anvil II** is the cheapest pickup. The [wiki](https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Anvil) lists the Anvil as a single-action hand cannon with a six-round magazine and high headshot damage, but slow handling. It fires **Heavy Ammo** and has "Strong" 1.5x ARC armor penetration. That matters for this week's Trials, as you'll see below.

## This week's Trials

The same graphic lists five [Trials](/articles/arc-raiders-trials-season-6-frozen-trials/) objectives:

- **Pendola Pass:** Open Containers in Cielo Sereno Observatory
- **Destroy ARC thrusters**
- **Deal damage using Heavy Ammo weapons**
- **Destroy ground ARC**
- **Search Weapon Caches**

The post says the Trials are "sending Raiders up to the frosty peaks" of the map that arrived with [Frozen Trail](/articles/arc-raiders-frozen-trail-pendola-pass/). The observatory objective ties you to Pendola Pass, but the other four can be done on any map. Weapon caches and ground ARC are the easiest to stack on the same runs. An Anvil II from Cassio also covers the Heavy Ammo objective, and its armor penetration helps against ground ARC.

## Soul's Offering wraps up

The Nomad window that just closed was built around [Soul's Offering](/articles/arc-raiders-update-2-3-souls-offering-live-heavy-shield-flame-spray/). [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/all-souls-offering-level-requirements-in-arc-raiders-and-how-to-get-them/) reported that the event runs until November 16. This week's brief makes no mention of offerings or tribute, which fits with the event ending. Embark has not announced a follow-up event in the post.
