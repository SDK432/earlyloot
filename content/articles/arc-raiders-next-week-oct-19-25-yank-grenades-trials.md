Embark has dropped its weekly radio brief, and the next seven days look busy. In a [post on the official ARC Raiders account](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2111849794196959346) on Sunday, October 18, the studio previewed **October 19–25**: Cassio is stocking **Yank Grenades**, and this week's Trials point Raiders at Pendola Pass, the Stiletto and Rascal, and the **Bully**. The week also brings the end of the [PvE toggle test](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-what-to-know) and the last call for a U.S. Subway tie-in.

## Cassio's stall: Yank Grenades

Embark says Cassio "is stocking up on Yank Grenades" next week, [calling it](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2111849794196959346) a "crafty new addition to your arsenal." The Yank Grenade is one of the new items that came with [Frozen Trail 2.0](https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-2-0-update), alongside the Grappling Hook, Tether Launcher and cameras. We first covered it in our [Pendola Pass breakdown](/articles/arc-raiders-frozen-trail-pendola-pass/).

The [community wiki](https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Yank_Grenade) lists it as an uncommon grenade that anchors a line to whatever it hits and pulls that target toward the point of impact. Normally you need its blueprint and Explosives Station 2 to craft it. Cassio is the barter trader who [replaced Ermal in 2.0](https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-2-0-update), and his stock rotates weekly. That gives Raiders without the blueprint another way to get some next week. Embark hasn't posted the exact barter price.

## This week's Trials: loot, Stiletto, Rascal and the Bully

According to [Embark's preview](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2111849794196959346), the Oct 19–25 Trials will test:

- **Looting in Pendola Pass**, the snowy map added in Frozen Trail
- **Your skill with the Stiletto and the Rascal**
- **The Bully.** Embark asks "dare you tackle the fearsome Bully to collect its Fragmenter?"

That keeps [Trials Season 6: Frozen Trials](/articles/arc-raiders-trials-season-6-frozen-trials/) built around the new Frozen Trail enemies. The season runs until November 16, with the Maestro Outfit as its top prize. Weekly progress also feeds the separate [Reward Pass](/articles/arc-raiders-reward-pass-explained/).

## The PvE test ends Tuesday

The [PvE matchmaking toggle](/articles/arc-raiders-update-2-1-pve-toggle-live/) is still a limited test. Per [Embark's rules page](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-what-to-know), it closes on **October 20 at 11:00 CEST**, which is **5:00 a.m. ET / 2:00 a.m. PT**. On October 16 the studio [asked players](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2111079720435765667) to try the toggle over the weekend and promised "the survey coming next week." Embark hasn't said whether PvE will return after the test. If you have opinions on [griefing in PvE lobbies](/articles/arc-raiders-pve-griefing-lure-grenades-dad-gamers/) or [the mode itself](/articles/arc-raiders-pve-toggle-player-reactions/), that survey is the place for them.

The timing works for endgame fans too. In a [hands-on piece published today](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/third-person-shooter/frozen-trail-finally-puts-the-raid-in-arc-raiders/), PC Gamer's Sean Martin calls the Arc Frigate and Fallen Emperor "exactly what the game needed." He also argues there's a good case for a PvE-only option on PvE-heavy endgame events. Note that the Frigate condition is [excluded from PvE servers](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-what-to-know) during the test.

## Last call: Subway's Subterranean outfit

For U.S. players, Embark [reminded Raiders](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2111442112919339433) that the Subway promotion ends **October 26**. A qualifying meal at participating U.S. locations (Kids Meals excluded, one code per order, 13+, opt-in required) gets you a code for the **Subterranean outfit**, plus a bonus mystery Subway outfit and weekly in-game rewards. Codes can be redeemed at Embark ID until **November 10**, while supplies last.

## What's next

- **Oct 19–25:** Yank Grenades at Cassio and new Trials challenges
- **Oct 20, 11:00 CEST:** PvE toggle test ends; Embark's feedback survey follows
- **Oct 26:** Subway promotion ends (codes redeemable until Nov 10)
- **Nov 16:** Trials Season 6 ends

We'll update EarlyLoot as soon as Embark shares the PvE survey or any decision on the toggle's future.
