GTA 6 hasn't even launched yet, and it's already the game to beat at The Game Awards 2026. On **Tuesday, November 17, 2026**, The Game Awards revealed its nominees during a livestream, [Polygon reports](https://www.polygon.com/game-awards-2026-nominees-list-game-of-the-year/), and Rockstar's open-world sequel came out on top with **eight nominations**, including **Game of the Year**, according to [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/the-game-awards-2026-nominees-revealed-gta-6-up-for-8-awards). The full list is live on the [official nominees page](https://thegameawards.com/nominees), where fan voting is already open.

## All 8 GTA 6 nominations

[VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-earns-8-nominations-at-the-game-awards-and-confirms-manni-l-perez-performance/) lists the eight categories GTA 6 is up for:

- **Game of the Year**
- **Best Game Direction**
- **Best Narrative**
- **Best Art Direction**
- **Best Score and Music**
- **Best Audio Design**
- **Best Performance** (Manni L. Perez)
- **Best Action/Adventure**

That makes GTA 6 the most-nominated game of the year, per [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/the-game-awards-2026-nominees-revealed-gta-6-up-for-8-awards). The Best Score and Music nod is a natural fit for a game whose soundtrack has been dominating headlines; catch up on [GTA VI: The Album tracklist](/articles/gta-6-the-album-full-tracklist-dua-lipa-lana-del-rey-playboi-carti/).

## The Game of the Year field

Geoff Keighley confirmed six Game of the Year nominees, [Push Square reports](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/the-game-awards-2026-nominees-revealed-gta-6-up-for-8-awards):

- 007 First Light
- Control Resonant
- Grand Theft Auto 6
- Phantom Blade Zero
- Resident Evil Requiem
- The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time

[Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/game-awards-2026-nominees-list-game-of-the-year/) calls GTA 6 "immediately one of the biggest contenders" for the top prize, even though the nominations landed before release. The timing works because GTA 6 launches on **November 19** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, just inside an eligibility window that closes on **November 20 at 9 PM PT**, [VICE notes](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-earns-8-nominations-at-the-game-awards-and-confirms-manni-l-perez-performance/). If you're planning your launch night, here's [how the midnight local unlock works](/articles/gta-6-midnight-local-unlock-twitch-kick-free-code-drops/).

## Manni L. Perez gets a Best Performance nod

The most talked-about nomination is in Best Performance. [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/game-awards-2026-nominees-list-game-of-the-year/) lists the five nominees:

- Angela Sant'Albano — Resident Evil Requiem
- Liam McIntyre — Marvel's Wolverine
- **Manni L. Perez — Grand Theft Auto 6**
- Patrick Gibson — 007 First Light
- Rahul Kohli — Saros

The nomination confirms Perez has a role in GTA 6, but not which one. [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-earns-8-nominations-at-the-game-awards-and-confirms-manni-l-perez-performance/) points out that The Game Awards listed each actor alongside their game without character names. Fans have long speculated that Perez plays protagonist Lucia Caminos, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed it, so treat that as speculation for now.

## How winners are picked and how to vote

According to [GAMES.GG](https://games.gg/news/the-game-awards-2026-nominations/), a jury of around 160 media outlets around the world chose the nominees. Fans can vote now on [The Game Awards site](https://thegameawards.com/nominees), which says saved ballot picks must be finalized before **December 9 at 9 PM ET / 6 PM PT**.
