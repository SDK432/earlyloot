With Modern Warfare 4 launching on October 23 and Warzone moving into the MW4 era with Season 1 on November 6, one optional setting is worth knowing about before you drop in. [The official Call of Duty blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/09/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-operator-toggle) confirms that **Season 1 adds an Operator Skin toggle to Warzone**. Turn it on and every Operator from an older Call of Duty game shows up as a Modern Warfare 4 Operator on your screen. Here is exactly what it does, and what happens to the rest of your old collection.

## What the Operator Skin toggle actually does

Activision calls it a filter "built specifically for the Modern Warfare 4 chapter," and it only exists in Warzone. With the toggle **ON**, according to [the blog post](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/09/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-operator-toggle):

- Any Operator or Operator Skin from another Call of Duty title (Black Ops 6 is the example given) that another player has equipped is shown to you as a **Modern Warfare 4 Operator**.
- That covers **enemies, party members and squadmates**, both **in the lobby and during matches**.
- **Your own Operator does not change.** You keep seeing the one you picked, even if it comes from an older game.
- It **only affects your game**. Other players still see whatever skins they normally would.
- It is **OFF by default**, so you have to switch it on yourself.

In short, the toggle changes what you see, not what anyone owns. A teammate in a Black Ops 7 crossover skin keeps it. You just see an MW4 Operator in its place. The same post was also [published on Blizzard News](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24309307/call-of-duty-warzone-season-1-adds-a-new-operator-skin-toggle).

## Legacy weapons: playable, but locked builds

The same announcement sets the Season 1 rules for older guns. Per [Call of Duty](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/09/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-operator-toggle):

- Every weapon from **Modern Warfare II through Black Ops 7** moves to a **Legacy tab** in the Loadout menu.
- **Gunsmith won't work** on legacy weapons or legacy Blueprints, and **old Custom Loadouts aren't supported**.
- Each legacy base weapon gets a **five-attachment version that you unlock through the Armory**.
- Legacy **Blueprints keep their existing attachment setups** as fixed builds.

Activision says the aim is a weapon meta "distinctly rooted in Modern Warfare 4." Raven Software will keep balancing weapons every two weeks.

## Camos don't carry over (a correction)

The original post said legacy camos would stay usable. Activision then added a correction to the blog: "This is incorrect." Only **Operators, Operator Skins and Weapon Blueprints** from MWII, MWIII, Black Ops 6 and Black Ops 7 remain available. [Dexerto reported the reversal](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/warzone-devs-confirm-camos-wont-carry-over-after-mw4-update-3412871/) and noted that some players had already been grinding camos.

## What else comes with you

[Destructoid's carry-forward rundown](https://www.destructoid.com/all-carry-forward-content-in-call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-and-warzone/) lists **COD Points** and **Double XP Tokens** as carrying into Warzone, along with legacy Operators, weapons and Blueprints. [AllThingsHow](https://allthings.how/cod-modern-warfare-4-and-warzone-all-carry-forward-content/) adds that COD Points stay on the platform where you bought them, which matches [Activision Support](https://support.activision.com/modern-warfare-4/articles/cod-points-in-modern-warfare-4).
