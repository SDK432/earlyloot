Season 1 of Modern Warfare 4 brings four new weapons, and none of them costs money. The [official Call of Duty blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement) lists two Battle Pass guns at launch on **November 6** and two more as in-season event rewards. Because Season 1 also folds MW4 into Warzone, [GameSpot notes](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-to-unlock-all-season-1-weapons-in-modern-warfare-4-and-warzone/) that you can unlock and use all of them in battle royale too. Our [Season 1 full intel](/articles/mw4-season-1-full-intel-x01-black-site-csg-35-hajin-zodiac-november-6/) covers maps, DMZ and the Battle Pass. This post is only about the guns and how to get them.

## Quick unlock list

- **Cronen Osprey (Assault Rifle):** free, Battle Pass **tier 16**. Its BlackCell blueprint sits at tier 87.
- **CSG-35 (SMG):** free, Battle Pass **tier 36**. Its blueprint sits at tier 73.
- **JAK 12 (Shotgun):** event reward, arriving later in the season.
- **ZLR Talonhawk (Melee):** event reward, arriving later in the season.

Those tiers come straight from the [weapons detail on the Call of Duty blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement), which says the Battle Pass includes "two new (and free) Base Weapons."

## Cronen Osprey: launch AR at tier 16

The Osprey is a 5.56 rifle with **882 RPM**, a 30-round magazine and **70 weapon levels**. Activision says only the M4 matches its rate of fire, and it warns that the gun kicks up and to the right in sustained fire. Its Apex attachment, the **Cronen Helix Mk. II**, adds a double-barrel receiver that fires on both trigger pull and release. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-to-unlock-all-season-1-weapons-in-modern-warfare-4-and-warzone/) describes it as a conversion into a dual-fire weapon.

## CSG-35: the 1,200 RPM SMG at tier 36

The CSG-35 has the **highest fire rate in its class at 1,200 RPM**, 28 rounds and 42 levels. The [blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement) says it shreds targets within about 20 meters, but its recoil climbs beyond that range. Its Apex attachment, **CSG Crossfire**, turns it into akimbo SMGs.

## JAK 12 and ZLR Talonhawk: event rewards

The **JAK 12**, a full-auto shotgun returning from Modern Warfare 2019, fires at 323 RPM with 8-round magazines. Activision pitches it as a breaching tool for clearing rooms. Its Apex attachment, **JAK Barrage**, fires sticky explosives. The **ZLR Talonhawk** is a heavy combat axe with 10 levels, mastery badges and no attachments. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-to-unlock-all-season-1-weapons-in-modern-warfare-4-and-warzone/) points out that it's a melee weapon, not a throwable tomahawk. Activision lists both as "Unlocked via Event" but hasn't given dates yet.

## Bonus: new Apex attachments from the Wager Match

Season 1 opens with the **Wager Match Event**, where trophies are the currency. According to the [blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement), its rewards include the **Hyeon Auto** Apex, which turns the Hyeon Burst AR fully automatic, and the **Kastov 545** Apex, which rechambers the Kastov 762 in 5.45mm. An **M16 Burst** attachment for the M4 also arrives as an event reward. The **Joint Fire** event gives a bigger XP boost the larger your squad is. For earlier Apex changes, see our [day-one update breakdown](/articles/mw4-day-one-update-apex-attachments-weapon-tuning/).

## What's next

- **November 2:** Activision publishes the dedicated Warzone Season 1 blog and roadmap, per the [Steam announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1938090/announcements/).
- **November 6, 00:00 ET** (9 PM PT on November 5): Season 1 goes live, and the Osprey and CSG-35 enter the Battle Pass.
- **Later in Season 1:** events unlock the JAK 12 and ZLR Talonhawk. We'll post the dates once Activision confirms them.
