The first camo event of **Modern Warfare 4** is underway. The **Natural Concealment Event** went live on **Monday, November 16** and runs for one week. Infinity Ward put it simply on X: "A new camo grind starts TODAY," [as quoted by HappyGamer](https://happygamer.com/mw4-natural-concealment-event-woodfall-mastery-camo-174596/). The same day, a [new MW4 patch](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) cut **Drill Charge** damage at range by 75%.

## How the event works

There are seven weapon-class challenges. Each one can be finished in **any one** of Multiplayer, DMZ or Warzone, and clearing it unlocks the static **Forest Dusk** camo for that class. Clear all seven and you earn **Woodfall**, an animated Mastery camo that Infinity Ward says is "usable across all weapons" ([HappyGamer](https://happygamer.com/mw4-natural-concealment-event-woodfall-mastery-camo-174596/)).

Here are the requirements as listed by [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-cod-modern-warfare-4-mw4-natural-concealment-camo-challenges-rewards/) (MP / DMZ / Warzone):

- **Assault Rifles:** 100 / 300 / 30 kills
- **SMGs:** 50 / 150 / 15 hipfire kills
- **LMGs:** 50 / 150 / 20 mounted kills
- **Shotguns:** 50 / 150 / 20 point-blank kills
- **Marksman Rifles:** 30 / 75 / 15 longshot kills
- **Sniper Rifles:** 20 one-shot kills in MP, 50 headshot kills in DMZ, 10 headshot kills in Warzone
- **Melee:** 30 / 100 / 10 kills

One note on the snipers: [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/guides/natural-concealment-event-camos-mw4-warzone) lists one-shot kills in all three modes, while Game Rant says headshots for DMZ and Warzone. Check the in-game challenge text before you commit to a mode.

## Fastest route: Warzone Casual Resurgence

Both outlets point to **Warzone Casual Resurgence** as the quickest path. Warzone has by far the lowest kill counts, and [Dot Esports reports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/guides/natural-concealment-event-camos-mw4-warzone) that bot kills count there. Avoid **Rally Point**, because kills there don't count toward the challenges, according to both [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-cod-modern-warfare-4-mw4-natural-concealment-camo-challenges-rewards/) and Dot Esports. DMZ is the slowest option given its much higher totals. If you prefer Multiplayer, Shipment 24/7 (back since [last weekend](/articles/mw4-weekend-loadouts-bug-shipment-24-7-double-xp-ends-nov-16/)) suits the shotgun and melee challenges.

## Nov 16 patch: Drill Charge nerf and camo-friendly fixes

The [official MW4 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) bring several changes:

- **Drill Charge:** impact damage at range is down **75%**, while close-range damage is unchanged.
- **Kills from behind** now track at wider angles, which helps with camo challenges.
- **Joint Fire** now runs for 24 hours **every Friday** through Season 1. It follows the [5x XP Joint Fire from the Nov 13 update](/articles/mw4-nov-13-update-less-flinch-m4-range-joint-fire-5x-xp/).
- The **Crossfire** tablet strike direction is fixed, and claiming Battle Pass tiers is faster.
- **DMZ:** stacked plate traits now show 10 plates, Lieutenant Dog Tag cash goes to every squad member, and the UAV Upgrade Pro minimap zoom-out drops from 3x to 1.5x.

The loadout reset bug is **still under investigation**. The notes say loadouts can reset between matches or logins, and that it's "likely a few different bugs happening at once."

[Warzone's Nov 16 notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) contain only bug fixes. Grinding for Royalty win streaks now track, final circles on Hajin no longer close over open water, and Safe Crack Ground Ops spawn on Zodiac again. Level-up reward notifications, challenge checkmarks, the Scavenger contract icon text and stretched Operator skin thumbnails are fixed too.

## What's next

The event ends on **Monday, November 23** in the morning Pacific time. Outlets differ slightly on the hour: [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-cod-modern-warfare-4-mw4-natural-concealment-camo-challenges-rewards/) says 10 AM PT and [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/guides/natural-concealment-event-camos-mw4-warzone) says 9 AM PT. Plan to finish by Sunday night to be safe. The next 24-hour Joint Fire lands on **Friday, November 20**, and we'll update you once Infinity Ward ships a fix for the loadout resets.
