The GTA 6 leak saga just escalated. On October 10, the group known as **CyberLeek** said it will release an entire build of Grand Theft Auto VI, story mode "played to the end, credits and all," according to [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/rockstar-has-six-weeks-to-stop-gta-6-ending-from-leaking/), which cites a statement on the group's website. The threat lands just under six weeks before the game's [official November 19, 2026 launch](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026). To be clear up front: nobody has verified that this build exists, and EarlyLoot will not share or describe any leaked material.

## What CyberLeek is demanding

[Dexerto reports](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-leaker-cyberleek-threatens-to-release-playable-build-from-start-to-finish-3417786/), citing creator LegacyKillaHD's post on X, that the release is tied to one of two outcomes:

- A revival of CyberLeek's own memecoin. [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/10/10/gta-6-leaker-to-release-the-entire-game-build-if-rockstar-doesnt-meet-demands/) and DualShockers describe the trigger as the coin hitting a set market cap.
- Take-Two releasing **physical discs** for GTA 6 and issuing a **public apology**.

The group also warned it could post major story spoilers to "prove" it has a complete build.

## How we got here

According to DualShockers, clips of mission footage and map data have circulated for roughly two months. After a quiet spell following the Netflix special in August, the group resurfaced this past week with around 25 minutes of new footage. [Dexerto notes](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-leaker-cyberleek-threatens-to-release-playable-build-from-start-to-finish-3417786/) that the authenticity of that October footage, and its link to the original leaker, have not been independently verified.

## Is a full build actually out there?

That is the open question. DualShockers points out that earlier reporting described the leaked version as an **unfinished debug build** that depends on Rockstar's authentication servers to run, and that there's no confirmed evidence of a retail-equivalent copy. Offering to show any scene on request is a bold move, "but it isn't proof yet," the outlet writes.

## How Rockstar and Take-Two have responded

Per Dexerto, Rockstar previously called the leaks "heartbreaking" and asked fans to wait for the real game, and Take-Two has pursued legal action, including subpoenas, to identify those responsible. As of this writing, Take-Two hasn't publicly responded to the new ultimatum, DualShockers reports.

## Our advice: protect your playthrough

If you want to experience Jason and Lucia's story fresh, now is the time to mute keywords like "GTA 6 ending," "CyberLeek" and "GTA 6 spoilers" on X, TikTok and YouTube. Leaked debug footage also rarely reflects final quality, so it's a poor preview of the real thing.
