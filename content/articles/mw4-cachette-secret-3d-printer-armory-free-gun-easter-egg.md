**You've probably sprinted right past it.** Modern Warfare 4's Cachette multiplayer map has a working 3D printer tucked inside its Armory, and if you smash it open you walk away with a free, fully built gun for the match. The find came from Redditor [u/muggs1213 on r/modernwarfare4](https://www.reddit.com/r/modernwarfare4/comments/1xdtc6s/the_map_cachette_has_a_3d_printer_in_the_armory/), who posted it on October 24, [Dexerto reports](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-has-secret-3d-printed-guns-you-can-find-on-one-map-3421806/). Other players quickly confirmed it works.

## Where the printer is

Head upstairs to the **Armory** point of interest on Cachette. That's where [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/cod-modern-warfare-4-players-discover-a-secret-gun-printer-in-multiplayer/) places the printer, and it says a weapon sits inside it at the start of every match. Anyone in the lobby can grab it, so it's first come, first served.

It's easy to miss in the middle of a firefight. One player admitted, per [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-has-secret-3d-printed-guns-you-can-find-on-one-map-3421806/): "I have played that map 200 times and never looked up once."

## How to get the gun

1. Go to the printer in the upstairs Armory.
2. Give it a moment. [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-has-secret-3d-printed-guns-you-can-find-on-one-map-3421806/) says you "wait for a few moments" before you break it open.
3. **Melee it.** In the original clip, muggs1213 doesn't use an interact prompt. They melee the printer and break its screen, [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/cod-mw4-cachette-map-3d-printer/) explains.
4. Pick up the gun inside and use it like any other weapon.

According to [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/cod-mw4-cachette-map-3d-printer/), the printed guns wear a resin-style skin that really sells the 3D-printed look.

## What guns can drop?

The drop is random, and nobody has a confirmed full list yet. muggs1213 said they're "unsure how many guns there are" and that they saw repeats, "so the attachments aren't randomized" ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-has-secret-3d-printed-guns-you-can-find-on-one-map-3421806/)). [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/cod-modern-warfare-4-players-discover-a-secret-gun-printer-in-multiplayer/) says the builds are generally fully kitted, with five attachments.

Guns players and outlets have reported so far:

- PPSH-41 SMG and Sang 9MM pistol ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/cod-modern-warfare-4-players-discover-a-secret-gun-printer-in-multiplayer/))
- Cronen Squall assault rifle and a Mosin Nagant marksman rifle ([GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/cod-modern-warfare-4-players-discover-a-secret-gun-printer-in-multiplayer/))
- Obrez Mosin Nagant, Type 73, Sang Breach, KG-7 Vulcan, Axion Reaver and Oris 8.6 ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-has-secret-3d-printed-guns-you-can-find-on-one-map-3421806/))
- Hyeon Hyperburst ([Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/cod-mw4-cachette-map-3d-printer/))

[GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/cod-modern-warfare-4-players-discover-a-secret-gun-printer-in-multiplayer/) also points out a practical upside: it's a free loaded build for new players who don't have many guns or attachments unlocked yet.

## Why a 3D printer? Blame DMZ

It's a nod to DMZ. In MW4's extraction mode, 3D printers craft gear and upgrades, and they're part of DMZ's own [Level Unlocks](/articles/mw4-mrbeast-1-million-dmz-video-early-access/). [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/cod-mw4-cachette-map-3d-printer/) says you unlock DMZ's printer at level 2 at the FOB Station. Its gear recipes run all the way to level 55, where you can finally print disposable parachutes.

In the Reddit comments, fans compared it to MW3's Aftermarket Parts and the JAK 3D camo, [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/cod-mw4-cachette-map-3d-printer/) notes. muggs1213 also mentioned a 3D-printed camo in the game, but [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/cod-mw4-cachette-map-3d-printer/) says nobody knows how to unlock it yet.

DMZ itself has been busy lately. Catch up on the [November 23 TTK nerf to 18 guns](/articles/mw4-dmz-ttk-nerf-damage-cut-nov-23-update-every-gun/).

## Don't expect the same gun twice

Because the drop is random, [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-has-secret-3d-printed-guns-you-can-find-on-one-map-3421806/) warns you may not see the same weapon in back-to-back lobbies. That makes the Armory worth a detour every time Cachette comes up.
