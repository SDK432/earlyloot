**Infinity Ward has finally put a week on it: Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 Reloaded is coming next week, and the studio is promising more than patch notes.** In its Thanksgiving post on X on November 25, the studio wrote: "We'll be back next week with Season 1 Reloaded and some news to share," as [Timesaver quotes it](https://timesaver.gg/blog/mw4-double-xp-weekend). That's the first time Infinity Ward itself has tied Reloaded to a specific week. Last week we could only point to an [in-game countdown for December 2](/articles/mw4-warzone-season-1-reloaded-release-date-dec-2-countdown-confirmed/).

## When does Season 1 Reloaded drop?

- **Official:** "next week", so the week of November 30. Infinity Ward hasn't given a day or time yet.
- **Expected:** December 2 at 10pm PT (December 3 at 1am ET). That's when the Battle Pass bonus-rewards timer runs out, according to [Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/mw4-season-1-reloaded-release-date). [NoobFeed](https://www.noobfeed.com/news/modern-warfare-4-holiday-patch-double-xp) expects the full Reloaded blog to land on Monday or early Tuesday, before the update goes live around December 3–4.

## What "some news" could mean

That extra line is what has players talking. [NoobFeed argues](https://www.noobfeed.com/news/modern-warfare-4-holiday-patch-double-xp) that a normal mid-season blog wouldn't need a separate teaser. It guesses the news could be about matchmaking, time-to-kill, servers or netcode, all sore spots in MW4 so far. It could also be a play to win back players who left for GTA 6. Until Infinity Ward says more, those are guesses, not leaks.

## What's already confirmed for mid-season

Activision's own Season 1 materials already list these:

- **Ministry:** a fight in and around an embassy damaged during the "Global Peace Summit" ([Worthplaying, publishing Activision's announcement](https://www.worthplaying.com/article/2026/10/28/news/151296-call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-begins-next-week-all-you-need-to-know-trailer/)).
- **Overflow:** a factory surrounded by farmland, with mobile rocket launchers firing in the distance ([PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/28/modern-warfare-4-season-1-everything-you-need-to-know-for-november-6-launch/)).
- **Counter Attack:** plant bombs at the enemy Comm Station while defending your own. A new mode that twists Team Deathmatch is also on the way ([Timesaver, quoting Activision's blog](https://timesaver.gg/blog/mw4-season-1-reloaded-release-date)).
- **JAK 12 and ZLR Talonhawk:** a full-auto shotgun and a melee axe, both coming "later in the season" ([Worthplaying](https://www.worthplaying.com/article/2026/10/28/news/151296-call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-begins-next-week-all-you-need-to-know-trailer/)).
- **Warzone:** the Extreme Resurgence and Rockets and Snipers LTMs ([Call of Duty Blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1)).

## Specialist Packages: reported, not official yet

Two outlets say Specialist Packages are coming back. These let you earn extra perks on top of your loadout instead of killstreaks ([esportnow](https://esportnow.com/news/call-of-duty/cod-mw4-season-1-reloaded-release-date/), [Daily Respawn Report](https://dailyrespawnreport.com/?article=modern-warfare-4-season-1-reloaded-december-3-ministry-overflow)). We couldn't find Specialist in any Activision or Infinity Ward post about MW4 multiplayer, so treat it as **unconfirmed** for now. It would be a big swing: Charlie Intel has reported MW3 players [calling for the feature to return](https://www.charlieintel.com/call-of-duty/mw3-players-call-for-fan-favorite-feature-missing-since-mw2019-to-return-292623/) ever since MW2019. In Warzone, Specialist already exists as a loot bonus ([Warzone Season 1 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes)).

## What's next

- **Today, Nov 27:** Joint Fire is back for its 24-hour Friday run ([official patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes)). It falls inside the Double XP window.
- **Nov 30, 10am PT:** Double Level and Weapon XP ends, according to Call of Duty's post on X ([via Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/mw4-double-xp-weekend)). Battle Pass XP isn't included.
- **Around Nov 30–Dec 1:** expected Season 1 Reloaded blog, plus Infinity Ward's "news."
- **Dec 2, 10pm PT (expected):** Season 1 Reloaded goes live.
