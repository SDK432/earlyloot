Don't expect to run into a thinly veiled real-world politician in Vice City. In a wide-ranging interview with [LOVE Magazine](https://lovemagazine.net/articles/gta-vi-the-american-dream), Rockstar's Senior Vice President of Narrative **Rupert Humphries** said Grand Theft Auto VI deliberately avoids depicting specific real people, events or movements. Instead, Rockstar is building its own exaggerated versions of modern American life for the state of Leonida, just weeks before the game's **November 19, 2026** launch.

## What Rockstar said

The interview ran in LOVE's Issue 27, which [The Brief notes](https://www.thebrief.news/en/standard/article/27852/rockstar-says-gta-6-will-spoof-america-without-naming-real-politics) features Jason and Lucia on one of its covers. As quoted by [VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469254/rockstar-writer-says-gta-6-is-avoiding-direct-political-references/), Humphries explained that Rockstar wants the game to feel "very specific to the moment" of release while not mirroring "the exact world we live in," so the team doesn't want "to be too specific about any particular person, event, movement, or anything like that."

He also pointed out that national politics has rarely been the focus of past GTA games, with GTA IV's post-9/11 New York as the notable exception.

## Satire turned up to eleven

Avoiding real names doesn't mean toning things down. Humphries told LOVE that if Rockstar is going to put this much effort into recreating today's world, "it has to be more over the top, more insane, more in your face." According to [GTAVice.net's breakdown](https://www.gtavice.net/news/love-magazine-new-gta-6-details-on-jason-lucia-raul-npcs-satire-and-the-making-of-leonida), the writers start with whatever makes them laugh, then push an idea until the absurdity becomes obvious.

Key points from the interview:

- **Original targets:** Rockstar took subjects like conspiracy theories and odd public reactions to everyday events, then created "slanted, overblown versions" rather than real-world copies.
- **Political flavor stays:** VGChartz reports players will still see election signs, get campaign robocalls, and hear political ads and talk shows on the radio.
- **No chasing trends:** Because the game has been in development for years, Humphries said the team avoids jumping on short-lived trends that could feel dated by launch.
- **Shared tone:** Everyone from billionaires to people down on their luck should feel like part of the same world, with the same human weaknesses as satire targets.

## Why it matters

GTA has always been a parody of America, and this confirms the formula for Leonida: loud, grotesque and fictional. It also fits what Rockstar has said elsewhere this fall, including Rob Nelson's comments that [money will matter more to the story](/articles/gta-6-money-story-progression/) than in earlier games. And all that political noise will reach you partly through the radio, which Rockstar just previewed with its [first wave of stations](/articles/gta-6-radio-stations-revealed/).
