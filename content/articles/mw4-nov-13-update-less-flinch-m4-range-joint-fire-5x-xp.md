Infinity Ward closed the week with a big one. The **Friday, November 13, 2026** entry in the [Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) is a broad weapon buff pass built around what the studio calls the "Attacker's experience": you get knocked off target less when you're shot, and most SMGs and assault rifles hold their damage further out. On the same day, the squad-focused [Joint Fire event went live](https://gamerant.com/cod-mw4-joint-fire-event/) with up to 5x progression.

## Flinch takes a big cut

Flinch is the aim kick you feel when bullets land on you, and the [official notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) reduce it across four classes. As [GamerFuzion's class breakdown](https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-november-13-patch-notes-dmz-ttk-weapon-tuning-m4-smg-range-flinch-aim-assist-smoke/) lays out, flinch received drops by:

- **Assault Rifles:** -25.4%
- **LMGs:** -19.3%
- **Pistols:** -17.8%
- **SMGs:** -15.6%

There are only two moves in the other direction. According to [Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/mw4-november-13-weapon-buffs-flinch-m4-smg-range), the Oris 8.6 now takes **9.6% more** flinch, and launchers now receive flinch at all.

## M4, Striga and SMGs reach further

Infinity Ward says it studied "hundreds of thousands of engagements" before [pushing damage ranges out](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes). The standouts, per [GamerFuzion](https://www.gamerfuzion.com/modern-warfare-4-november-13-patch-notes-dmz-ttk-weapon-tuning-m4-smg-range-flinch-aim-assist-smoke/) and [Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/mw4-november-13-weapon-buffs-flinch-m4-smg-range):

- **M4:** max damage range goes from 27.94 m to 35.31 m, a **26%** jump, with damage values unchanged.
- **Wz.55 Striga:** mid damage range up **34%**.
- **FiNN LMG Adverse Apex:** max range grows from 15.98 m to 21.34 m.
- **CSG-35, ISO Nightshade and PPSh-41:** their final damage drop-off is gone, so they no longer fall to a lower floor at long range.

Combined with the [hitscan and ballistics changes from earlier this week](/articles/mw4-hitscan-range-3-91x-ballistics-34ms-faster-ranked-season-2/), the studio expects these guns to feel steadier and deadlier at range.

## Smoke, Wager Match and DMZ

The patch also [turns off aim assist through smoke](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) in Multiplayer, covering both Smoke Grenades and the Smoke Wall Field Upgrade, even where shots or blasts open gaps. Warzone got its own version of that rule [on November 12](/articles/warzone-no-aim-assist-through-smoke-nov-12-update-prestige-token-dmz-uav-fix/).

Other changes in the same entry:

- **Wager Match:** trophy drop rates are up in every MP mode and in DMZ. Our [Wager Match event guide](/articles/mw4-warzone-wager-match-event-live-30-free-trophies-kastov-545-vitriol/) covers what trophies unlock.
- **Resupply Run:** fixes for the DMZ event's flow.
- **DMZ TTK:** still in playtesting, with details promised later.

One caveat: the weapon tuning is listed under Multiplayer, and [Timesaver notes](https://timesaver.gg/blog/mw4-november-13-weapon-buffs-flinch-m4-smg-range) that Warzone's notes had no November 13 entry when it checked. Warzone hasn't confirmed these changes yet.

## Joint Fire event: squad up for 5x

[Game Rant reports](https://gamerant.com/cod-mw4-joint-fire-event/) that Joint Fire is live now and rewards grouping up:

- **Duos:** 3x progression.
- **Squads of three or more:** 5x progression.
- Boosts apply across **Multiplayer, DMZ and Warzone**.

The event has nine rewards unlocked with Joint Fire XP. The Move on Targets Calling Card arrives at 150,000, and the final item, a 1 Hour Double Weapon XP Token, needs 1,350,000. Claim all nine and you also get the **Squad Work Hyeon Burst Blueprint**. Game Rant doesn't give an end date, so don't leave it for later.

## What's next

Infinity Ward says it will keep "a sharp eye" on the tuning [over this weekend (Nov 14–15)](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) and expects more updates **next week**, which is also the likeliest time for news on DMZ TTK. We'll also watch Raven's notes to see whether the buffs reach Warzone.
