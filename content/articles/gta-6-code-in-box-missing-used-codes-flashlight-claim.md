GTA 6 is out on **November 19, 2026**, and some people who bought the boxed version early say their boxes came without a code, or with one that had already been used. On top of that, a claim is going around that the code can be read through a sealed PS5 case with a flashlight. Nothing here is confirmed. These are buyer reports, and Rockstar hasn't commented publicly. Sony's own [GTA VI code support page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/gta-vi-code/) does say what to do if your code fails, though.

## Why the code matters so much

There's no disc in the box. As [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-ps5-codes-potentially-being-stolen-from-physical-copies-using-flashlight-trick) points out, the physical edition is code-in-box only. Instead of a separate slip, the code is printed on the inside of the cover, according to [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2316687/some-physical-copies-of-gta-vi-can-allegedly-be-redeemed-without-opening-the-box/). So if the code is missing or already used, the box doesn't get you the game. (For a refresher on editions and prices, see our [GTA 6 price and editions guide](/articles/gta-6-price-editions-platforms/).)

## The reports: missing and already-used codes

[Kotaku reported on November 14](https://kotaku.com/some-gta-6-box-copies-reported-to-have-download-code-issues-2000756883) that photos and videos of boxes with no code have been posted on Reddit and X. Some buyers also say they were refused refunds. Kotaku thinks misprints are "well within the realm of possibility." On November 15, [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/gta-6-players-furious-as-physical-copies-reportedly-arrive-with-missing-or-already-used-codes-3428334/) reported that affected buyers bought from GameStop, Best Buy and Rockstar's own store. Some say they were sent to Rockstar support, and one says their ticket was closed without being fixed. No one knows yet how many boxes are affected.

## The flashlight claim: unproven

Here's the theory. Shine a strong light into the side of the case and you might be able to read the code without opening it. [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-ps5-codes-potentially-being-stolen-from-physical-copies-using-flashlight-trick) traces it to Reddit and ResetEra posts. It notes that US copies print a camouflage pattern behind the code, but some PEGI copies reportedly don't. People are also arguing over whether the poster insert blocks the light.

Testing so far doesn't support the claim:

- [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/we-tried-stealing-our-own-gta-6-physical-edition-codes-and-you-probably-shouldnt-worry-about-someone-getting-to-yours-first/) tried it on a US and a UK PS5 copy. In both cases the camo strip made the code unreadable. The outlet also couldn't find anyone who had confirmed a code was stolen this way.
- [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/some-gta-6-box-copies-reported-to-have-download-code-issues-2000756883) says its editor-in-chief couldn't make the trick work either, and called it "a bit of a yarn."
- [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2316687/some-physical-copies-of-gta-vi-can-allegedly-be-redeemed-without-opening-the-box/) says only a few photos are out there and the issue may only affect some international copies. It asked Take-Two for comment.

## What to do if your code is missing or used

This is based on [PlayStation's support page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/gta-vi-code/):

- **Redeem it right away.** You can use the code as soon as the box arrives and pre-load the game. Our [pre-load guide](/articles/gta-6-pre-load-live-30fps-ps5-pro-pssr-xbox-one-cloud/) covers the details.
- **Getting "Code has already been used"?** Look in your Order History to see whether the pre-order already went through, and make sure you didn't redeem it on another account. If neither explains it, contact the retailer.
- **Code missing or damaged?** Sony says to go to the retailer. Bring the box and your receipt.
- **Buy from reputable retailers** only, as Sony advises. Avoid resold boxes.

## What's next

GTA 6 launches **November 19, 2026** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar hasn't publicly confirmed a widespread problem ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/gta-6-players-furious-as-physical-copies-reportedly-arrive-with-missing-or-already-used-codes-3428334/)). Kotaku and Engadget have both asked Rockstar or Take-Two for comment. We'll update this story if either company responds or announces a replacement process.
