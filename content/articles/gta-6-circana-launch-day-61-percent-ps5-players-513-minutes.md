**GTA 6 didn't just launch. It swallowed consoles whole.** According to [Circana's new breakdown](https://www.circana.com/post/so-how-impactful-was-the-launch-of-grand-theft-auto-vi), written by industry analyst Mat Piscatella, **61% of US PlayStation 5 players** and **56% of global Xbox Series players** who were active on November 19 spent time in Grand Theft Auto VI. The next-biggest game that day? Fortnite, with just 10%.

## The launch-day numbers

All of this comes from Circana's Player Engagement Tracker, per [the firm's post](https://www.circana.com/post/so-how-impactful-was-the-launch-of-grand-theft-auto-vi):

- **61%** of active US PS5 players played GTA VI on November 19
- **56%** of active Xbox Series players worldwide did the same
- **10%**: Fortnite's share of active players across both platforms, good for second place
- **513 minutes** (about 8.5 hours): average launch-day playtime for US PS5 GTA VI players
- **494 minutes**: the worldwide average for Xbox players

Piscatella first shared the figures on Bluesky on November 20, calling both the daily-user and minutes-played engagement rates "absolutely bonkers," as quoted by [VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469713/61-of-us-ps5-players-active-on-november-19-played-gta-6-56-of-global-xbox-players/). Hard to argue. An *average* of over eight hours means plenty of people played a full workday's worth, and some went way past it.

## True or false: 61% of all US PS5 owners played GTA 6 on day one

**False.** That's how [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/61percent-of-all-us-ps5-owners-played-gta-6-on-launch-day-averaging-8-hours) headlined the story, but it's not what the data says. Circana's figure covers only PS5 players who were **active on November 19**, not every console sitting under a TV. Anyone who didn't turn on their PS5 that day isn't in the math. [VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469713/61-of-us-ps5-players-active-on-november-19-played-gta-6-56-of-global-xbox-players/) phrased it correctly: 61% of the people who played *anything* on PS5 that day picked GTA 6.

It's still a monster number. Just not the one going viral.

## It moved hardware too

The hype showed up at the register before launch. Circana's Retail Tracking Service found that in the week ending November 14, US **PS5 unit sales grew more than 2.4x** and **Xbox Series unit sales more than 2.3x** compared with the same week a year earlier, [according to Circana](https://www.circana.com/post/so-how-impactful-was-the-launch-of-grand-theft-auto-vi). That's people buying a console specifically to be ready for Vice City.

The firm also points out that GTA VI held **29% purchase intent** among US console and PC gamers in late October, a record we [covered at the time](/articles/gta-6-circana-record-purchase-intent-7-million-preorders/). Turns out that intent was real.

## Who paid the price

When most active players are in one game, everything else loses time. Alinea Analytics **estimated** that Call of Duty's daily active users dropped about 42% after GTA 6 arrived. Those are third-party estimates, not official numbers, and we broke them down in our [MW4 Black Friday story](/articles/mw4-black-friday-15-percent-off-call-of-duty-players-drop-gta-6/). Circana's 10% figure for second-place Fortnite tells a similar story from a different dataset.

## What's next

Circana's monthly US sales reports usually land several weeks after the month ends, so November's report, the first one covering GTA 6's launch month, is the next big data drop to watch. Piscatella closes by suggesting GTA VI could follow GTA V, which has [stayed on Circana's monthly best-seller charts](https://www.circana.com/post/so-how-impactful-was-the-launch-of-grand-theft-auto-vi) since 2013. Take-Two hasn't published official sell-through numbers yet.
