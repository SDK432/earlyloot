The Edge cover story we flagged [earlier this week](/articles/gta-6-edge-430-cover-story-october-29-delay-rumor-fake/) is out, and it's packed. On **October 29**, Edge 430's 16-page Grand Theft Auto VI feature went [free to read on GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/edge/gtavi-cover/). It's built on a roughly **90-minute session** in which Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson played a current build with no script. The same day, Rockstar [announced Justice's official score album](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/39ak8a12395591/grand-theft-auto-vi-original-score-by-justice).

## 12 brand-new screenshots

[GameRant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-screenshots-criminal-life-gameplay/) counts 14 Rockstar-provided images in the feature, **12 of them brand-new**. They run from Vice City's waterfront and nightlife to trailer parks, swamps and a hammerhead shark underwater. [Rockstar Report](https://rockstarreport.com/news/gta-6-12-new-screenshots-reactive-leonida) also points out a supermarket robbery getaway, Lucia firing from a passenger window and VCPD officer Méndez.

## The cops have dogs now

The headline moment: Nelson's stolen motorcycle hits a near-invisible stinger strip and the couple go flying. They limp away, but a **K9 unit** joins the chase and an Alsatian (German Shepherd) pins Jason to the ground. Per [Edge](https://www.gamesradar.com/edge/gtavi-cover/), everyone in the room urged Nelson not to shoot it.

Policing is smarter overall:

- A **six-star wanted system**, as [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6s-cops-will-send-dogs-to-get-you/) notes
- A "heat" system with on-screen icons for what police know: your description, outfit, weapon, partner and **CCTV sightings**
- Escaping means shedding clues. **Change clothes**, drop the weapon or split from your partner, and GameSpot adds you can destroy CCTV evidence

## Criminal Profile and the Jason-Lucia bond

A new **Criminal Profile** tracks behaviour from "Professional" to "Irredeemable". Incapacitating or intimidating enemies keeps you calculating; shooting first makes you violent. Rockstar says the profile shapes how the story unfolds. Jason and Lucia's closeness sits on a **four-point scale** that rises with time together, like beach walks, cycling or the gym. Nelson wouldn't confirm whether they can marry.

## Story jobs cost money

Some story jobs are gated until the pair **raise cash first**: gas station holdups, jewellery store heists, or stealing a specific car a fence wants through the WAINK app. Other systems Edge confirmed:

- **Your arsenal lives in the car's trunk**
- The **Scooter Bros** app delivers one of your cars for a small fee
- Every vehicle has a **fuel gauge and engine health**
- Car theft is risky. Some drivers are **armed and fight back**, and newer expensive cars can carry **trackers that broadcast your location**

## Justice's score gets an album

Rockstar's [Newswire](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/39ak8a12395591/grand-theft-auto-vi-original-score-by-justice) confirms **Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score** by Justice, **30 tracks** plus digital bonus tracks, out **December 11** on streaming, 2CD and 3LP vinyl. New single **"Pusher" feat. Gesaffelstein** is streaming now. [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-new-song-pusher-soundtrack-justice/) calls it "the perfect song to jam to while committing crimes". It joins "Flower in a Hurricane" on the album.

## What's next

- **November 19, 2026**: GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Nelson told Edge the game is "ready"
- **December 11, 2026**: Justice's Original Score album releases
