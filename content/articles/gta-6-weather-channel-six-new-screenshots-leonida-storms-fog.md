GTA 6 just got a weather report. On **November 16, 2026**, The Weather Channel, working with Rockstar, posted **six never-before-seen GTA 6 screenshots** framed as a "forecast for Leonida," along with comments from Rockstar's Owen Shepherd on how the game's weather was built, [GamesRadar+ reports](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/rockstar-is-so-confident-in-gta-6s-weather-system-that-it-has-the-weather-channel-itself-sharing-new-screenshots/). The channel's post says Rockstar "gave us an exclusive look at the forecast for Leonida and the dynamic weather in GTAVI."

## What the six screenshots show

The new shots lean hard into bad weather. According to [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/new-gta-6-screenshots-showcase-intense-weather-systems/), the set includes:

- **Torrential rain in the city**, with reflections across wet streets.
- **A storm rolling in over the swamp** at Pelican Valley.
- **The Vice City skyline soaked in sea fog** drifting in from the ocean.
- **A storm by a bar called the Rusty Anchor.**
- **Humid, low-lying mist** hanging over Gambit Bay.
- **Wind and rain over Key Lento.**

[GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/rockstar-is-so-confident-in-gta-6s-weather-system-that-it-has-the-weather-channel-itself-sharing-new-screenshots/) sums the gallery up as heavy rain, storm clouds and thick mist and fog, and singles out the volumetric fog as the most impressive part.

## Rockstar on rebuilding weather

Shepherd is Rockstar's art VP of light and rendering. He says the team "had to completely reimagine" its weather systems so they feel "more localized, persistent, and above all dynamic," as quoted by [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/rockstar-is-so-confident-in-gta-6s-weather-system-that-it-has-the-weather-channel-itself-sharing-new-screenshots/). He adds that conditions range from "clear blue skies to towering vertiginous cumulonimbus clouds," and that he hopes Florida locals will recognise the result.

That matches what we saw earlier this month in our [look at GTA 6's dynamic weather, storms and flight turbulence](/articles/gta-6-dynamic-weather-storms-boats-planes-turbulence/). The difference now is that we have six fresh images instead of a description. One reality check: [GamesRadar+ notes](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/rockstar-is-so-confident-in-gta-6s-weather-system-that-it-has-the-weather-channel-itself-sharing-new-screenshots/) that all this runs at a 30 FPS target on PS5, Series X and PS5 Pro, something we covered in our [pre-load guide](/articles/gta-6-pre-load-live-30fps-ps5-pro-pssr-xbox-one-cloud/).

## A second pre-launch patch is rolling out

There's also a new update. [VICE reports](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-second-day-one-patch-ps5-xbox/) that version **01.002.001** began rolling out in waves on November 16. It's about **602.5 MB on PS5** and about **1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S**, and there are no patch notes yet. [Rockstar Report](https://rockstarreport.com/news/gta-6-update-ps5-rollout), citing dataminer tracking from PlayStation Game Size, lists this PS5 wave schedule (it's not an official Rockstar timetable):

- **33%:** Nov 16, 10:00 UTC
- **66%:** Nov 16, 22:00 UTC
- **100%:** Nov 17, 10:00 UTC

This follows the first pre-launch patch, 01.002.000, which arrived on November 13 alongside the [Macca & Friends teaser](/articles/gta-6-macca-and-friends-tv-show-teaser-version-1-002-update/). If your console hasn't grabbed it yet, check for updates manually before launch night.
