Good news if you pre-ordered digitally: you can start downloading **Grand Theft Auto VI** two days sooner than planned. In a [Rockstar Newswire post](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10) published on **November 3, 2026**, Rockstar confirmed that digital pre-load now opens on **Tuesday, November 10**. The earlier date was November 12, which is what pre-order pages and our [original pre-load guide](/articles/gta-6-preload-date/) listed. Launch day hasn't moved: GTA VI still arrives on **November 19, 2026** for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## PS5 vs. Xbox: when pre-load starts

The two consoles don't unlock at the same moment, so check which rule applies to you:

- **PlayStation 5:** pre-load begins at **midnight local time on November 10**, in your own time zone. The [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/03/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-download-begins-november-10/) says the download is open to players who pre-order.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** pre-load begins at the same moment everywhere, **12 a.m. GMT on November 10**, according to [Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10). That works out to **7 p.m. ET / 4 p.m. PT on Monday, November 9** in the US.

So Xbox players in America actually get going a few hours before PS5 players, whose download waits for local midnight on the 10th.

## How much storage you need

Make space now. Rockstar says the pre-load file needs **up to 212GB**, depending on your device. Sony's blog tells PS5 owners to clear **at least 212GB**, and suggests adding extra storage if your console is nearly full. [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/rockstar-confirms-gta-6-is-212gb-pre-loading-opens-earlier-than-previously-announced-for-digital-orders/) points out that the PS5 and Xbox files may not be exactly the same size, and that the final install can be different from the pre-load download.

Pre-loading only puts the files on your console early. You still can't play until November 19.

## Physical copies still start November 12

The earlier date is for **digital** orders only. GTA VI's boxed edition is a code-in-box release with no disc. Rockstar says those **code-in-box versions** should go on sale at retailers starting **November 12**, which is the same timing as before. Once you redeem the code, you can start pre-loading.

## A $1 poster, and a lot of pre-orders

GamesRadar+ also spotted one last add-on on Rockstar's merch store: a **double-sided poster** with the cover art on one side and the map on the other. It costs **$1**, or it's free when you buy something else.

Demand is clearly strong. [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/gta-6-has-made-over-750-million-in-pre-sales/) reports that analytics firm Sensor Tower now **estimates** GTA VI pre-sales at **more than $750 million** and about **7.71 million units** as of November 3. That's up from the [6.45 million estimate we covered in October](/articles/gta-6-pre-orders-6-45-million-sensor-tower/). These are third-party estimates. Rockstar and Take-Two haven't confirmed them.
