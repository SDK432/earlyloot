Rockstar has always hidden jokes in its maps, but in GTA 6 it went full dad-joke on day one: there is a **literal Easter egg** sitting on top of Vice City. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-easter-eggs-list-and-locations/) has started a running list of what players have dug up since the game unlocked, and it is already a strong case that Leonida is Rockstar's most secret-packed world yet. Here's what's verified so far, no story spoilers.

## Did you spot the egg on the tower?

Player xGarbett posted a clip on November 18 of Jason climbing to the top of a tall tower, where a bird's nest holds eggs painted like, yes, Easter eggs. [GameSpot reports](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-a-hilarious-riff-on-easter-eggs/) that you can grab one and throw it off the ledge, and the clip shows it crashing down on a sleazy-sounding guy below. The catch: xGarbett said the climb took **about seven minutes**. GameSpot places it atop the tallest tower in Vice City and notes it works as Jason or Lucia.

## Bigfoot is back (and he smells)

GTA fans chased Bigfoot rumors in San Andreas for years. In GTA 6, he's real. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-easter-eggs-list-and-locations/) points to Gullet Marsh and says the encounter is actually tied to a mission, so most players will meet the hairy guy eventually. The outlet admits it barely counts as an Easter egg if it's unmissable, but after two decades of myth-chasing, it earned its spot.

## A UFO abduction with a hidden trophy

This one is creepy. [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175102-gta-6-players-discover-ufo-encounter-that-unlocks-a-secret-trophy.html) reports a random night encounter in Kelly County, on a dirt road east of Port Gellhorn near the Starlet Motel safehouse:

- Go at night; guides say **after 2:00 AM in-game** is the most reliable window.
- Look for a parked car with flickering lights, an open trunk and clothes on the ground.
- Follow the trail into the woods toward the glow, where a UFO is beaming someone up.
- Help her, then go back for her car. That's when the UFO comes for you.

That twist unlocks the secret bronze trophy **"The Truth Is Out There."** It works as either Jason or Lucia and isn't needed for 100%. Guides differ on timing: VGTimes says after Chapter 1, while [Beebom](https://beebom.com/gta-6-ufo-location-trophy-guide/) says Chapter 2 must be unlocked, so if it won't spawn, push the story a bit further.

## Dexter Morgan's apartment

TV fans, head to Washington Island in Vice City, near the Vice Beach Top Fun Drones shop. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-easter-eggs-list-and-locations/) says the Bay Harbor Butcher's apartment is recreated there. You can't go inside, and some players think the boat docked nearby looks a lot like Dexter's *Slice of Life*.

## Did you know? Red Dead and Liberty City are in here too

- **True or false: Red Dead and GTA share one universe?** True, and GTA 6 rubs it in. GameSpot spotted a Western novel about a cowboy living in New Austin, the Red Dead Redemption region, and you can actually read excerpts.
- On Jason's table early in Chapter 1 sits a memoir from a former Liberty City mobster that references events from 2008, the year GTA 4 is set.
