The wait is over. Grand Theft Auto VI's official release date is **Thursday, November 19, 2026** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, as the [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/18/grand-theft-auto-vi-launches-tomorrow-november-19/) confirms, but the game unlocks at midnight local time, so the rollout began on Wednesday. Players in the Asia-Pacific region had already been playing for hours before the UK unlocked at 00:00 GMT, [BBC News reports](https://www.bbc.com/news/live/c60rkg21dl22t). If you're still waiting for your region, here's [how the midnight local unlock works](/articles/gta-6-midnight-local-unlock-twitch-kick-free-code-drops/).

## A 99 on Metacritic

Reviews went live on November 18, and the scores are historic. GTA 6 holds a **99 Metascore** on PS5 based on 150 critic reviews, with every review counted as positive, according to [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/). Game Informer, The Guardian and IGN France are among the outlets that gave it a perfect 100.

[NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/grand-theft-auto-six-release-rockstar-games-what-to-know-rcna609644) notes that the score ties The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest-rated game ever. [BBC News](https://www.bbc.com/news/live/c60rkg21dl22t) points out that Ocarina earned its 99 from just 22 critic reviews, while GTA 6 reached the same mark with well over a hundred. The praise lands one day after the game picked up [8 nominations at The Game Awards 2026](/articles/gta-6-game-awards-2026-8-nominations-game-of-the-year-manni-perez/).

## Sales estimates and Wall Street's reaction

Rockstar hasn't shared official sales numbers yet, but third-party trackers are already counting:

- Sensor Tower estimated **nearly 16 million copies** and more than **$1.5 billion** in pre-launch sales as of Tuesday, [NBC News reports](https://www.nbcnews.com/pop-culture/pop-culture-news/grand-theft-auto-six-release-rockstar-games-what-to-know-rcna609644).
- By early Wednesday, Sensor Tower's tracker put the figure at roughly **17.1 million units**, according to [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/gta-vi-publisher-taketwo-jumps-as-gamers-relish-blockbuster-release-5036414).
- Take-Two shares jumped **more than 6%** in premarket trading on Wednesday, [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/gta-vi-publisher-taketwo-jumps-as-gamers-relish-blockbuster-release-5036414) reports.
- Wells Fargo now expects about 65 million units sold by the end of March, and Oppenheimer estimates around $1.85 billion in gross revenue over the first three days, per the [same Reuters report](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/gta-vi-publisher-taketwo-jumps-as-gamers-relish-blockbuster-release-5036414).

These are analyst estimates, not official figures from Take-Two.

## No performance mode, even on PS5 Pro

If you were hoping for a 60fps patch, don't hold your breath. Rockstar told Kotaku that GTA 6 won't get a performance mode on current consoles, PS5 Pro included, [Polygon reports](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-30-fps-locked-ps5-pro-performance/). Technology production director Marc Chambers explained that the PS5 Pro's upgraded GPU and PSSR improve visual fidelity and add ray-traced shadows, but that its CPU "is not powerful enough to run the game's complex simulation at higher frame rates." That matches the 30fps target Rockstar confirmed [when pre-loading opened](/articles/gta-6-pre-load-live-30fps-ps5-pro-pssr-xbox-one-cloud/).

## What about PC?

Rockstar has **not** announced a PC version. [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-30-fps-locked-ps5-pro-performance/) says rumours point to a port as early as 2027, Wells Fargo's forecast assumes a "potential" PC release early in 2027 according to [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/gta-vi-publisher-taketwo-jumps-as-gamers-relish-blockbuster-release-5036414), and [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2320788/gta-vi-launch-day-live-blog/) also points PC players to 2027. Treat that as speculation until Rockstar confirms it.
