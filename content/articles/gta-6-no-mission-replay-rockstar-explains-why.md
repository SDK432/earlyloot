Finished a jaw-dropping GTA 6 mission and want to run it back? You can't. There's no mission replay menu in Grand Theft Auto 6, and Rockstar says it cut the feature on purpose. Senior design director **Scott Butchard** explained the call to [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-ditched-mission-replay-because-it-felt-at-odds-with-the-story-2001000335) in an email before launch, and it's already one of the most argued-about design choices in the game.

## What Rockstar actually said

Butchard says the studio "certainly" looked at mission replay "early on in development," but "we do not currently support it," [per Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-ditched-mission-replay-because-it-felt-at-odds-with-the-story-2001000335). As the game came together, he said, the feature "felt at odds with the story we were trying to tell" and "a bit alien and disconnected."

His main point: stepping outside the game to replay missions "really diminished the choices you had made." [Gamenguide](https://www.gamenguide.com/articles/109238/20261127/gta-6-does-not-let-players-replay-missions-because-it-conflicts-story-rockstar-says.htm) notes Butchard framed it as a creative decision, not a technical limit.

## Why your choices make replays messy

GTA 6 missions aren't fixed set pieces. [IGN points out](https://sea.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi-rumored-title/252449/gta-6-doesnt-let-you-replay-missions-and-rockstar-says-theres-a-good-reason-for-that) that some characters can be killed or spared, objectives can be done in different ways, and Jason and Lucia's relationship can change based on what you do. That can unlock entirely different dialogue and cutscenes.

It's the same idea behind the game's [permanent "Irredeemable" Criminal Profile tier](/articles/gta-6-criminal-profile-irredeemable-permanent-psycho-explained/): your decisions are meant to stick. Kotaku compares the missions to quests in RPGs like The Witcher 3, where the outcome you get matters more than a perfect run.

## A GTA staple, gone

This is a real break from tradition. Rockstar first added mission replay with GTA 4's Ballad of Gay Tony expansion, and it came back in GTA 5 with a medal system that had players grinding missions for top ratings, [Kotaku recalls](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-ditched-mission-replay-because-it-felt-at-odds-with-the-story-2001000335). [GameSpot adds](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/why-gta-6-doesnt-let-you-replay-missions-it-felt-alien-and-disconnected/) that Red Dead Redemption 2 let you replay missions too.

[IGN says](https://sea.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi-rumored-title/252449/gta-6-doesnt-let-you-replay-missions-and-rockstar-says-theres-a-good-reason-for-that) the missing option "has been a point of contention for fans." With the campaign taking around 50 to 100 hours, few players will restart just to see one favorite mission again. It's also worse news for anyone already stuck on bugs like the [Missing Persons softlock](/articles/gta-6-missing-persons-softlock-jason-greyed-out-after-first-patch/).

## The workaround: manual saves

You can still go back, just not easily:

- **Reload an older save.** [GameSpot confirms](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/why-gta-6-doesnt-let-you-replay-missions-it-felt-alien-and-disconnected/) loading an earlier save lets you play a mission again.
- **Use all 15 slots.** GTA 6 has 15 manual save slots, [according to Gamenguide](https://www.gamenguide.com/articles/109238/20261127/gta-6-does-not-let-players-replay-missions-because-it-conflicts-story-rockstar-says.htm). Save before big missions or key decisions. Loading an old file means losing the progress you made after it.
- **Mid-mission bailout.** If a mission is going badly, the pause menu still offers retry, quit or restart, [per GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/why-gta-6-doesnt-let-you-replay-missions-it-felt-alien-and-disconnected/).

## What's next

Butchard's "currently" leaves the door slightly open, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed any plan to add mission replay in an update, [IGN notes](https://sea.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi-rumored-title/252449/gta-6-doesnt-let-you-replay-missions-and-rockstar-says-theres-a-good-reason-for-that). Kotaku and IGN both expect modders to add it whenever GTA 6 comes to PC. Until then, keep those save slots full.
