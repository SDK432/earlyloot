**Grand Theft Auto VI** doesn't launch until **November 19**, but it may already be a billion-dollar game. On **November 11, 2026**, analytics firm Sensor Tower updated its GTA VI sales tracker and now estimates **more than 10 million pre-orders** across PS5 and Xbox, putting revenue **above $1 billion net of returns**, [The Washington Post reports](https://www.washingtonpost.com/entertainment/video-games/2026/11/11/grand-theft-auto-vi-reportedly-passes-1b-sales-week-before-release/). A day earlier, Xbox CEO Asha Sharma used the game to announce a new **10-year partnership with Take-Two**.

As always with these numbers: they are **third-party estimates**. Rockstar Games and Take-Two have not published any pre-order figures.

## The new Sensor Tower numbers

According to [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-sold-almost-8-million-copies-so-far-and-ps5-is-dominating-the-split-of-course/), the updated tracker shows:

- **10.31 million** pre-purchased units, worth about **$1 billion**
- **PS5:** 7.73 million units and about $751 million (75%)
- **Xbox Series X|S:** 2.58 million units and about $250 million (25%)
- A projection of up to **25 million** pre-purchases by launch

[TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114626/gta-6-pre-orders-break-dollars1-billion-with-10-million-sales-analyst-firm-estimates/index.html) notes that the totals already exclude expected refunds and cancellations, and that Sensor Tower doesn't disclose its exact data sources or method.

## A big jump from October

When we covered Sensor Tower's tracker on October 24, it estimated [about 6.45 million digital pre-orders worth roughly $630 million](/articles/gta-6-pre-orders-6-45-million-sensor-tower/). The new estimate is nearly **4 million copies and about $370 million higher** in less than three weeks, with the PS5/Xbox split barely moving (74/26 then, 75/25 now).

If the figure is accurate, GameSpot points out it would [top Cyberpunk 2077's reported 8 million pre-orders](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-sold-almost-8-million-copies-so-far-and-ps5-is-dominating-the-split-of-course/), the previous high-water mark.

Take-Two hasn't given a number of its own. CEO Strauss Zelnick called pre-orders "far in excess of expectations," while also stressing that pre-orders aren't the same as final results, [TweakTown reports](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114626/gta-6-pre-orders-break-dollars1-billion-with-10-million-sales-analyst-firm-estimates/index.html). That matches what the company told investors this week, when it said [development is complete and pre-orders are far above expectations](/articles/gta-6-take-two-earnings-development-complete-pre-orders-far-above/).

## Xbox announces a 10-year Take-Two partnership

On November 10, Xbox CEO Asha Sharma posted on X that the day marked "the first step in our new 10-year strategic partnership with Take-Two," [IGN reports](https://in.ign.com/xbox/276686/xbox-ceo-asha-sharma-confirms-a-10-year-strategic-partnership-with-take-two-interactive-as-gta-6-pre). In the same post she said GTA VI is "already the most pre-ordered game ever on Microsoft Store" and confirmed it will be playable on **Xbox One through cloud gaming** at launch.

What the partnership actually covers is still unknown. [IGN](https://in.ign.com/xbox/276686/xbox-ceo-asha-sharma-confirms-a-10-year-strategic-partnership-with-take-two-interactive-as-gta-6-pre) notes Sharma gave no specifics. The first visible piece is cloud: GTA VI streams on day one on Xbox One and Series X|S using Series S graphics settings, and [Xbox says](https://in.ign.com/xbox/276686/xbox-ceo-asha-sharma-confirms-a-10-year-strategic-partnership-with-take-two-interactive-as-gta-6-pre) GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 are also coming to Xbox Cloud Gaming. Our [pre-load and Xbox cloud breakdown](/articles/gta-6-pre-load-live-30fps-ps5-pro-pssr-xbox-one-cloud/) has the details.

## What's next

- **Now:** pre-loading is already underway, [GameSpot notes](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-sold-almost-8-million-copies-so-far-and-ps5-is-dominating-the-split-of-course/), and Sensor Tower's tracker will keep updating as the 25 million projection is put to the test.
- **November 19:** GTA VI launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, unlocking at midnight local time, plus Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One.
- **After launch:** official sales figures, if Take-Two shares any, will show how close Sensor Tower's model came. Any detail on the Xbox–Take-Two deal will be the next thing to watch.
