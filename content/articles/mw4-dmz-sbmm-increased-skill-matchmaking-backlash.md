**The skill-based matchmaking war has a new front.** On November 18, CharlieIntel reported that Infinity Ward "recently updated matchmaking in MW4 DMZ to increase consideration of skill factor in match selection," citing "sources familiar with the change," [as Dexerto reports](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-increased-sbmm-in-dmz-despite-multiplayer-backlash-report-3429314/). Infinity Ward hasn't announced anything officially, but if the report holds, the extraction mode just inherited multiplayer's most hated argument.

## What reportedly changed

According to CharlieIntel's sources, skill now carries more weight when DMZ lobbies are built ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-increased-sbmm-in-dmz-despite-multiplayer-backlash-report-3429314/)). In plain terms: more squads around your own level, fewer mismatches. What nobody knows is the size of the dial turn. As Dexerto points out, [the developers never said how much skill counted at launch](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-increased-sbmm-in-dmz-despite-multiplayer-backlash-report-3429314/), so there's no baseline to compare against.

There may already be a side effect. Streamer JGOD posted that he waited [more than six minutes for a DMZ match](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-increased-sbmm-in-dmz-despite-multiplayer-backlash-report-3429314/), writing that he'd "never waited this long." One long queue isn't proof, but it's exactly the trade-off critics warned about.

## Why DMZ players care more

In regular multiplayer, a sweaty lobby costs you a K/D. In DMZ, it costs you your loot. You fight over the best zones and have to survive exfil to keep anything, so a tighter skill band means [veteran squads see other veteran squads far more often](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-increased-sbmm-in-dmz-despite-multiplayer-backlash-report-3429314/). Newer teams get fewer stomps from stacked squads. Good players argue they're being punished for being good.

## The multiplayer fight never ended

This all lands on top of a launch-long fight. Infinity Ward brought [Classic matchmaking over from Black Ops 7](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-boss-responds-to-sbmm-backlash-as-players-call-it-miserable-3421274/), citing data on player retention, queue times and quit rates compared with Open matchmaking. Multiplayer creative director Joe Cecot told IGN, ["I don't know if there's perfecting it,"](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-boss-responds-to-sbmm-backlash-as-players-call-it-miserable-3421274/) and said "our end goal is that every match is a good match." Former Call of Duty producer Mark Rubin argued the other side, saying [public lobbies are more fun with a wider skill mix](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-increased-sbmm-in-dmz-despite-multiplayer-backlash-report-3429314/).

Players aren't buying the studio line. One post quoted by Dexerto called SBMM ["the most miserable experience to date"](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-boss-responds-to-sbmm-backlash-as-players-call-it-miserable-3421274/). Daily Respawn Report says MW4 sits around [70% positive on Steam overall](https://dailyrespawnreport.com/?article=two-weeks-into-warzone-season-1-hajin-is-a-keeper) in a mid-November snapshot of about 6,200 English reviews, with the post-launch window down at 58%. That's a rough gap for a game that was [October's best-selling title in the US](/articles/mw4-best-selling-game-october-circana-misses-2026-top-10/).

## How to dodge the sweatiest lobbies (in multiplayer)

YouTuber TheXclusiveAce found that playlists in the **Popular** section use [more open matchmaking and persistent lobbies](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-boss-responds-to-sbmm-backlash-as-players-call-it-miserable-3421274/) than Quick Play:

- Queue Team Deathmatch or Search and Destroy from **Popular**, not Quick Play.
- Stay in the lobby after a match to keep persistent lobbies working for you.

DMZ has no equivalent workaround yet.

## What's next

- **War Chest DMZ event:** live since [November 19](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes). Open containers (5 points) and safes (50 points) to climb 3-hour leaderboards that reset every 4 hours, with rewards like Fulton Exfils and Tempered Armor Vests.
- **Season 1 Reloaded:** expected [December 2](/articles/mw4-warzone-season-1-reloaded-release-date-dec-2-countdown-confirmed/). That's the next big moment for Infinity Ward to address matchmaking, but nothing has been promised.
- **No official word:** Infinity Ward still hasn't publicly confirmed the DMZ change or [said whether Open matchmaking will return](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/modern-warfare-4-boss-responds-to-sbmm-backlash-as-players-call-it-miserable-3421274/).
