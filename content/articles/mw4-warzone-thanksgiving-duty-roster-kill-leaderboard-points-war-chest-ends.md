Thanksgiving week in Modern Warfare 4 starts with a kill race. A new all-modes leaderboard event called the **Thanksgiving Duty Roster** went live on Monday, November 23, and the scoring is lopsided: according to [COD Central's Events-menu screenshots](https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-events/), a Warzone kill is worth **20 points**, a Multiplayer kill **10** and a DMZ kill just **2**. [DMZMap lists the same values](https://dmzmap.com/updates). In other words, one Warzone elimination counts as much as ten DMZ kills.

Activision hasn't put the event in its [Season 1 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) yet. Until it does, the numbers below come from those two community trackers. The reward list hasn't been published either.

## How the Thanksgiving Duty Roster works

- **Every mode counts.** Kills in Warzone, Multiplayer and DMZ all score, [per DMZMap](https://dmzmap.com/updates).
- **Points per kill:** 20 in Warzone, 10 in Multiplayer, 2 in DMZ ([COD Central](https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-events/)).
- **Short cycles.** COD Central says it runs as [a series of short leaderboards like War Chest](https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-events/), and you can only earn each reward once.

## Where to grind it

If you only care about the leaderboard, play Warzone. One kill there is worth two in Multiplayer and ten in DMZ. Multiplayer is the steadier option, though. A Warzone squad can go a whole circle without a fight, while a fast Multiplayer match gives you a kill chance every few seconds.

DMZ is the slow lane. [COD Central notes](https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-events/) that DMZ players will climb "much slower" than everyone else. Since rewards can't be earned twice, you don't need to grind past a tier you've already cleared in an earlier cycle.

Whatever you play, kills still drop trophies for the [Wager Match event](/articles/mw4-warzone-wager-match-event-live-30-free-trophies-kastov-545-vitriol/), which [runs until December 2](https://dmzmap.com/updates). So the same kills count toward both events.

## What ends today (Nov 23)

- **War Chest (DMZ).** Containers are worth 5 points and safes 50, on leaderboards that [last 3 hours and reset every 4 hours](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes), per Activision. Both [COD Central](https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-events/) and [DMZMap](https://dmzmap.com/updates) list November 23 as the last day. First place gets two Fulton Drop Markers, and the top 30 still get two Night Vision Goggles.
- **Natural Concealment.** The camo event also [ends November 23](https://dmzmap.com/updates). Finish the Forest Dusk challenges in all seven weapon classes to unlock Woodfall. Our [Natural Concealment breakdown](/articles/mw4-natural-concealment-event-woodfall-camo-drill-charge-nerf/) covers the details.

## What's next

- **November 24: Threat Briefing: The Captain (DMZ only).** You enter X01, take down The Captain and [extract Prototype Helmets](https://codcentral.net/blog/mw4-dmz-events/). The more helmet sections you bring out across your deployments, [the better the reward](https://dmzmap.com/updates).
- **December 2: Wager Match ends** ([DMZMap](https://dmzmap.com/updates)).
- **December 2: Season 1 Reloaded.** That's where the in-game countdown points, as we covered in our [Season 1 Reloaded release date guide](/articles/mw4-warzone-season-1-reloaded-release-date-dec-2-countdown-confirmed/). Activision has said [more camo and DMZ events will arrive](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) throughout Season 1 and Reloaded.

If you've been saving your Warzone sessions for the long weekend, now's the time to play them. We'll update this post once Activision publishes the official Duty Roster rewards.
