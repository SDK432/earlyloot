Did you spot this one? Rockstar hid a nod to Nintendo's most famous plumbers in the backwoods of Leonida, and the trigger is pure GTA. On November 23, [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2026/11/grand-theft-auto-vi-super-mario-bros-hats/) reported that taking a hit from a bong inside a set of ruins in Lummox County reveals a giant floating, see-through cube. Jump into it from below and you walk away with a **red "J" cap and a green "L" cap**, an obvious wink at Mario and Luigi, for Jason and Lucia.

[GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-a-super-mario-bros-easter-egg-and-its-a-trip/) covered it the same day under a headline that sums it up nicely: "it's a trip."

## Where the Mario block is hiding

Outlets describe the same spot in slightly different ways, but they all point to one place:

- **Consequence** places the ruins near the [Water Wheel Meditation spot in Lummox County](https://consequence.net/2026/11/grand-theft-auto-vi-super-mario-bros-hats/).
- **Dexerto** names the landmark: the [Lawton Co. Paper Mill in Moreau, just north of Mount Kalaga National Park](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/super-mario-bros/).
- **GameSpot** says to head north from Mount Kalaga to the [end of a twisty road that seemingly goes nowhere](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-a-super-mario-bros-easter-egg-and-its-a-trip/).
- **Mobalytics** adds a handy tell: you're in the right place when the [meditation spot prompt pops up nearby](https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-super-mario-easter-egg).

## How to get the J and L hats, step by step

1. **Drive to the paper mill ruins** in Moreau, Lummox County. Dexerto also suggests checking the lock box next to the bong for [$400 in cash](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/super-mario-bros/).
2. **Find the bong on the purple tarp**, as both [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-a-super-mario-bros-easter-egg-and-its-a-trip/) and [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/super-mario-bros/) describe it, and use it.
3. **Look up.** Once the high kicks in, a [white plume of smoke appears, followed by a floating cube](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/super-mario-bros/). GameSpot says the smoke rises from one of the columns, and standing in it makes the [invisible block show up above your head](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-a-super-mario-bros-easter-egg-and-its-a-trip/).
4. **Climb the ruins** and stand right under the block. Mobalytics notes you'll need to [parkour up the broken walls](https://mobalytics.gg/gamebase/guides/grand-theft-auto-6-super-mario-easter-egg) to get there.
5. **Jump.** Dexerto warns that lining it up is [fiddly, so keep trying](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/super-mario-bros/). When you connect, a jingle plays, the block breaks and two hats fall to the ground.
6. **Pick up both caps.**

## What you actually get

No gold coins, sadly. GameSpot jokes that you [won't get any for your trouble](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-a-super-mario-bros-easter-egg-and-its-a-trip/), but you do get a nostalgic tune and two embroidered caps. Dexerto lists them as a ["J" Baseball Cap for Jason and an "L" Baseball Cap for Lucia](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/super-mario-bros/). Mario's cap carries an "M", so Rockstar swapped in Jason's initial, and Lucia's "L" happens to match Luigi's exactly.

As far as anyone has reported, the reward is cosmetic only. No outlet mentions stat boosts or unlocks tied to it.
