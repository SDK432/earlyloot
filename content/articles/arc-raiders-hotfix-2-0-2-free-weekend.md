One day after the Frozen Trail update went live, Embark Studios is rolling out **Hotfix 2.0.2** for ARC Raiders on **October 9, 2026**. It goes after the most annoying launch-day bugs: weapons turning partly invisible, server crashes and a grate on Pendola Pass that could swallow players whole. According to [ConsolePCGaming's write-up of Embark's Steam announcement](https://consolepcgaming.com/arc-raiders-hotfix-2-0-2-targets-weapon-bugs-and-server-crashes/), you only need to **restart the game** to download it.

The timing matters. Frozen Trail arrived alongside a [free-to-play window that runs from October 8 to 12](https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-is-live), so a lot of brand-new Raiders are hitting the Rust Belt right now. Stable servers this weekend are a big deal.

## What Hotfix 2.0.2 fixes

Here is the full list of fixes, as [reported by ConsolePCGaming](https://consolepcgaming.com/arc-raiders-hotfix-2-0-2-targets-weapon-bugs-and-server-crashes/):

- Weapon meshes no longer turn partially invisible when you equip attachments. This was already listed as a known issue in the Frozen Trail 2.0 patch notes.
- Weapon models show up in the inventory again.
- Several causes of server crashes are fixed.
- A grate on Pendola Pass no longer lets players fall through it and get stuck.
- An error that could appear when buying DLC bundles is fixed.

## Still broken: Reward Pass progression

The hotfix does **not** fix everything. Embark says it knows the Reward Pass is not progressing for some players and is "working on a solution". No timing has been given for that fix yet. If your pass looks stuck, the problem is on Embark's side, so don't spend hours grinding to fix it. For a refresher on what each tier gives you, see our [ARC Raiders Reward Pass guide](/articles/arc-raiders-reward-pass-explained/).

## Free weekend and 50% off

If you haven't tried ARC Raiders yet, this is the cheapest moment to start. Embark's [official announcement](https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-is-live) confirms:

- **Free to play from October 8 to 12** on all platforms. On PS5 in Germany, a €0.25 fee applies.
- **50% off on all platforms** while the promotion runs. [GamingBible](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/arc-raiders-free-to-play-frozen-trail-update-645100-20261009) notes that the free access ends on October 12, after which you will need to buy the game to keep playing.

## A quick reminder of what Frozen Trail added

Frozen Trail is the biggest ARC Raiders update so far. As [GamingBible summarizes](https://www.gamingbible.com/news/arc-raiders-free-to-play-frozen-trail-update-645100-20261009), it adds:

- **Pendola Pass**, a frozen map with an observatory, an underground train depot and new dynamic loot zones
- three new enemies, the Bully, Hydra and Skulker, plus the hulking Frigate
- the **Bantam** and **Stiletto** weapons and a new upgrade tier for Amplified Weapons
- a redesigned Raider skill tree
- a bigger stash, now up to **328 slots**

[Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/erikkain/2026/10/09/arc-raiders-frozen-trail-patch-notes-new-map-weapons/) also has a breakdown of the patch notes. For our map rundown, read [Everything New in Pendola Pass](/articles/arc-raiders-frozen-trail-pendola-pass/).

## What's next

- **Now:** restart ARC Raiders to grab Hotfix 2.0.2.
- **October 12:** the free-to-play window closes.
- **TBA:** a fix for Reward Pass progression. Embark has not given a date.
