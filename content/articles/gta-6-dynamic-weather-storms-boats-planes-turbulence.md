Weather in **Grand Theft Auto VI** won't just be a backdrop. In Edge magazine's GTA 6 cover story, Rockstar co-studio head **Aaron Garbut** described a dynamic weather system that pushes boats around, shakes aircraft and changes how Leonida's residents behave. As [GamesRadar+ reported on November 4, 2026](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6s-weather-will-rock-your-boat-damage-your-plane-and-send-vice-city-denizens-seeking-shelter/), Garbut called the system "much more granular" than what most games offer, where in his words it's usually "either rainy or sunny or whatever."

## Wind that actually does something

According to [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6s-weather-will-rock-your-boat-damage-your-plane-and-send-vice-city-denizens-seeking-shelter/), Garbut explained that wind moves through Vice City, bending trees and whipping up waves on the water. The more interesting part is that the world gets in the way of it. Islands and buildings block the wind much as they would in reality, which changes how a boat anchored near the shore bobs and sways.

Even where you stand on deck counts. "Depending on where you are in your boat," Garbut told Edge, "you'll skip over that stuff or get really rocked around by it." For a game set in a Florida-inspired state full of canals, bays and open ocean, that could make a simple boat ride feel very different from one day to the next.

## Flying into a storm is a bad idea

Pilots should keep an eye on the sky. Per [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6s-weather-will-rock-your-boat-damage-your-plane-and-send-vice-city-denizens-seeking-shelter/), flying a plane or helicopter through severe weather causes turbulence, and staying inside a storm for too long can lead to light damage to your aircraft. So that shortcut through a thunderhead may end up costing you some repairs.

## Leonida's citizens head for cover

The weather also affects the people around you. When severe weather rolls in, citizens of Leonida will seek shelter, [GamesRadar+ reports](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6s-weather-will-rock-your-boat-damage-your-plane-and-send-vice-city-denizens-seeking-shelter/). Garbut summed up the approach simply: "Everything comes down to these granular levels, and then it just interacts."

It fits the picture Rockstar has been painting of a dense, reactive world. We've already covered how big that world is in our [GTA 6 map size breakdown](/articles/gta-6-map-size-gta-4-gta-5-rdr2-combined-600000-npc-animations/), and the official [launch trailer](/articles/gta-6-launch-trailer-flower-in-a-hurricane/) leaned hard into storm imagery too.

## More Red Dead DNA

Weather isn't the only system with Red Dead Redemption 2 roots. [Game Rant reports](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-gameplay-features-rdr2-focus/) that Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson showed IGN a feature called Focus, which he described as "very similar to 'Eagle Eye' in Red Dead Redemption 2." In the demo, it highlighted a nearby police officer.
