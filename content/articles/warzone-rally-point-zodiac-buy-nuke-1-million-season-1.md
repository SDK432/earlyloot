**Warzone Season 1's wildest flex is not on Hajin.** In the new **Rally Point** mode on Zodiac, squads bank Cash, hit a Buy Station, and buy a **Tactical Nuke for $1,000,000** — then evacuate as the map goes up. [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/new-warzone-mode-lets-you-buy-nuke-for-1000000-to-end-the-game-3425358/) broke down the buyable Nuke on launch day; [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/news/mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) flagged the same purchasable Nuke summarizing Raven's Season 1 notes on November 5.

For Hajin BR, Zodiac Resurgence, Tac Sprint, and update 1.111, see our [Season 1 is live digest](/articles/warzone-season-1-live-hajin-zodiac-patch-notes-november-6/). This post is the Rally Point / $1M Nuke angle.

## What Rally Point actually is

Per [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/news/mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes), Rally Point is a **20-player sandbox mode on Zodiac** with a different loop from classic BR or Resurgence:

- **No Gas Circle** — the zone does not shrink to force an endgame.
- **Unlimited Resurgence** — you keep coming back.
- **Drop-in / drop-out** — join or leave mid-match without a permanent wipe.
- **Objectives over pure circle play** — complete Contracts, defeat Hostile Soldiers, and stack Cash.
- **Form squads of up to four** during a match.

[Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/new-warzone-mode-lets-you-buy-nuke-for-1000000-to-end-the-game-3425358/) adds that designated **neutral / safe areas** let players change loadouts, take Cash payouts, and team up — fitting the sandbox framing over pure deathmatch.

## Special events on Zodiac

Rally Point is not just Contracts on a quiet map. [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/news/mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) lists rotating pressure events from the Season 1 notes: **Hostile Choppers**, **Bombing Drones**, **JLTV deliveries**, and **Elite reinforcements**. Those keep the lobby busy while squads farm toward the Buy Station Nuke.

## Buy the Nuke for $1,000,000 — first come, first served

This is the headline. [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/new-warzone-mode-lets-you-buy-nuke-for-1000000-to-end-the-game-3425358/) reports you can buy a **Tactical Nuke for $1,000,000 from a Buy Station**. Once purchased, a **countdown** starts, then a **cutscene** plays of your squad evacuating as the map is destroyed — an intentional win condition for a mode with no gas.

Cash comes from **contracts, eliminations, and map activities**, per Dexerto. [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/news/mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) stresses the Nuke is **purchasable on a first-come, first-served basis** — only the fastest squad to hit $1M secures it that match.

Important scope note from [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/new-warzone-mode-lets-you-buy-nuke-for-1000000-to-end-the-game-3425358/) at time of writing: this accessible Buy Station Nuke is **not** in full Battle Royale on Hajin or in regular Resurgence. Older Champion's Quest Nukes are different; Rally Point is the straightforward "save up and buy it" version.

Official detail lives in the [Call of Duty Season 1 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) and the [Season 1 blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1); schedule confirmation is also on the [Activision Support seasons page](https://support.activision.com/modern-warfare-4/articles/modern-warfare-4-warzone-seasons).
