The switch flips tomorrow. In a [new brief posted October 12](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-what-to-know), Embark Studios confirmed the exact window for the ARC Raiders **PvE matchmaking toggle test** and spelled out the rules that apply once you queue into a PvE server. The test starts **Tuesday, October 13 at 11:00 CEST** and ends **Tuesday, October 20 at 11:00 CEST**.

We covered the original announcement and Embark's comments on a possible permanent split in our [earlier PvE test article](/articles/arc-raiders-pve-mode-test-october-13/). This is the practical, day-before version: what actually changes when you hit that toggle.

## Exact start and end times

The [official post](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-what-to-know) lists 11:00 CEST for both the start and the end. For players in the US, that converts to:

- **Eastern:** 5:00 AM ET, Oct 13 to 5:00 AM ET, Oct 20
- **Central:** 4:00 AM CT
- **Pacific:** 2:00 AM PT

That gives you a full seven days of PvE matchmaking, including next weekend.

## The PvE rules, one by one

Embark says the toggle lets you move between the standard PvPvE mode and the new PvE servers. Inside PvE, [according to the studio](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-what-to-know):

- **No player-to-player damage at all.** Other Raiders can't hurt you, and you can't hurt them.
- **Throwables won't touch other players.** Stun and fire items have no effect on fellow Raiders.
- **Barricades and ziplines still work normally.** Other physical interaction items are unaffected.
- **Loot is worth less.** Overall loot value on PvE servers is lowered.
- **Mixed squad sizes meet more often.** Squad separation isn't prioritized, so a solo can run into a trio.

## Downed? Count to eight

This is the rule worth remembering. After you go down, there's an **eight-second grace period** where ARC enemies won't acquire you. Once that window ends, ARC "may attack if they catch you moving," Embark writes. In short: stay still and wait for your squad, especially in the first seconds.

## Maps and conditions without PvE

Some major map conditions won't offer PvE as an option. The new list names **Night Raid, Locked Gate, Hidden Bunker, Frigate and Redirection**. Redirection wasn't in the [original announcement](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-beta-test), so expect to queue in regular PvPvE if you want to play it this week.

## Matchmaking and queue times

Embark warns that queue times may be longer in **low-population regions and during quiet hours**, and says it will watch this closely. Regular PvPvE sessions keep standard matchmaking during the test. The studio says it wants clean data on how a PvE option affects PvPvE before it makes "any sweeping changes."

## What's next

- **Oct 13, 5:00 AM ET:** the PvE toggle goes live.
- **Oct 13–20:** play both modes and send feedback. Embark wants input from PvE fans and from players who stick to PvPvE.
- **Oct 20, 5:00 AM ET:** the test ends.
- **After the test:** Embark will send out a survey to collect detailed feedback and opinions on where PvE should go next.

Embark also recommends reading its [earlier explainer](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-beta-test) on why it's running the test now. While you wait, check our [Trials Season 6 guide](/articles/arc-raiders-trials-season-6-frozen-trials/) for this week's challenges.
