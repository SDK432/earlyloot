ARC Raiders' Chinese version, developed by Embark Studios with Tencent, has a date for its next big test: the **second closed beta starts on December 1, 2026**, on **PC only**. [Beebom reports](https://beebom.com/arc-raiders-china-december-beta-test-announced/) that the announcement landed on TapTap on November 12 together with a new **10-minute gameplay trailer**, and the [official recruitment page](https://arc.qq.com/cp/a20261103ques/index.html) confirms that sign-ups close on **November 19**. On the same week, Sony confirmed that its new PS5 upscaler is heading to the global game too.

## The China beta: dates and rules

In China the game is called 弧光猎人, and this test is branded the "同频测试", roughly the "Sync Test". Here's what the [official TapTap post](https://www.taptap.cn/app/754064/topic) and the [recruitment page](https://arc.qq.com/cp/a20261103ques/index.html) lay out:

- **Sign-ups:** November 3 to November 19, 23:59 (China time), via a recruitment questionnaire.
- **Selection:** results are sent out from **November 23**, by SMS and the official WeChat assistant, according to the [recruitment page](https://arc.qq.com/cp/a20261103ques/index.html).
- **Format:** limited access with a **progress wipe** after the test, PC only, as [Beebom notes](https://beebom.com/arc-raiders-china-december-beta-test-announced/).
- **Login:** WeChat or QQ accounts.

Filling in the form doesn't guarantee a spot. [Beebom points out](https://beebom.com/arc-raiders-china-december-beta-test-announced/) that only a limited number of players will get in.

## Data Hunt: a China-exclusive PvP event

The headline addition is **Data Hunt** (数据猎取), a PvP event exclusive to the Chinese client. The [official TapTap description](https://www.taptap.cn/app/754064/topic) has Raiders collecting signal data in a zone where communications have gone dark, then hauling it back to Speranza for rewards. The catch, according to [Beebom](https://beebom.com/arc-raiders-china-december-beta-test-announced/), is that other Raiders can steal that data before you extract. In other words, it only counts if you get it home.

Beebom adds that the PvE-leaning **Security Protocol** condition seen in earlier Chinese tests hasn't been confirmed for December, and that there's no word on whether Data Hunt will ever reach global servers.

## QSSR is coming to ARC Raiders on PS5

Over on PlayStation, Sony's [PlayStation Blog post from November 13](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/13/qssr-arrives-in-marvels-spider-man-2-control-resonant-the-finals-arc-raiders-and-hell-let-loose-vietnam/) names ARC Raiders in the next wave of games getting **QSSR (Quick Spectral Super Resolution)**, the AI-guided upscaler for the base PS5 that is built on the same foundation as PS5 Pro's PSSR. Embark's Technical Art Director Mikael Linderholm says QSSR is "coming soon" to ARC Raiders and The Finals, promising "increased sharpness, better environmental clarity" for PlayStation players.

The same wave covers Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (live now), Control Resonant (December), Hell Let Loose: Vietnam and No Man's Sky, as [PSU](https://www.psu.com/news/sony-confirms-qssr-is-coming-to-arc-raiders-control-resonant-marvels-spider-man-2-more/) and [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/ps5-qssr-support-added-to-marvels-spider-man-2-control-resonant-arc-raiders-the-finals-no-mans-sky-and-hell-let-loose-vietnam) also report. **No release date was given for ARC Raiders' QSSR update.** Sony only says the updates roll out "over the coming weeks".
