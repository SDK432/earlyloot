**Season 1's third straight fix day just landed.** On Saturday, November 7, Raven Software added a new entry to the official [Warzone Season 1 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) that lowers the volume of teammate footsteps, adjusts XP across all modes and fixes the bug that blocked a second Elite Perk purchase. The same day, Infinity Ward posted a matching round of [MW4 Season 1 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) for Multiplayer and DMZ.

If you missed the launch itself, our [Warzone Season 1 Is Live](/articles/warzone-season-1-live-hajin-zodiac-patch-notes-november-6/) digest covers Hajin, Zodiac and the full launch notes.

## Warzone: the changes you'll actually notice

The headline change is audio. Raven says it "lowered the volume of teammate footsteps so enemy movement is easier to hear during combat," per the [Warzone notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes). With [Tactical Sprint back as default movement](/articles/warzone-tactical-sprint-default-season-1/), squads make a lot more noise, so this should help you pick out the footsteps that matter.

The rest of the Warzone list:

- **XP:** gains were "adjusted across all modes to match intended values." Raven didn't say whether that means up or down.
- **Elite Perks:** you can now buy a second Elite Perk after already owning one.
- **Grinding for Royalty:** the event no longer shows the Daily Challenge timer by mistake, and Personal Bests for E/D, Damage, Contracts and Win Streaks now update properly.
- **Drone Intercept:** the crate icon no longer appears on the map when there's no crate.
- **Loadout Drop:** the stray "LOADOUT DROP" text above the crate is gone.
- **Hajin:** map collision was fixed in areas where players could clip through geometry.

## MW4 Multiplayer fixes

Infinity Ward's [November 7 notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) fix an issue where players could be kicked out of the **Gunship** killstreak when their Operator was eliminated. Battle Pass progress on a tier now rolls over to the next tier when you use a token, and players should no longer get stuck on the scoreboard after Hardcore matches.

Map voting also now supports playlists with three map and mode combinations, like the [SHRANSIT playlist](/articles/mw4-shransit-playlist-shipment-shoot-house-transit-213/). On Lithium, Field Upgrade equipment no longer disappears on certain surfaces.

## DMZ and X01

For DMZ, the [patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) list stability improvements in X01 and a fix for player kills in X01 raising your Bounty Level "more than intended." Other fixes:

- The Operation Fallen Ember story mission no longer soft-locks if you destroy the boat too early.
- Paid Light Helo infils are no longer shot down by SAM Sites at Chang-san Military Base right after spawning.
- The Captain and Colossus can no longer eliminate players in Last Stand who could still be revived.
- Wheelson Commander turrets stop rotating once disabled by DDoS or the Shock Stick.

Still on the free trial? Our [DMZ Free Trial guide](/articles/mw4-dmz-free-trial-through-november-11-x01-hajin/) explains what's included through November 11.

## Still broken

Infinity Ward's known issues list still includes Platinum camo challenges not tracking for the Cronen Osprey and CSG-35, the Warfighter skins being temporarily disabled, and a crash at launch on Steam. [Insider Gaming's tracker](https://insider-gaming.com/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-known-issues/) also flags a DMZ bug where the transition from Hajin into X01 can fail and wipe your Operator, Loadout and Backpack.

## What's next

More hotfixes are likely as Season 1 settles. According to the [known issues list](https://insider-gaming.com/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-known-issues/), a Firing Range fix and other range improvements are scheduled for **November 10**.
