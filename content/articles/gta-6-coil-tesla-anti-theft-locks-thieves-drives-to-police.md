Did you know the worst car to boost in GTA 6 might be the shiny electric one? Players have discovered that Coil vehicles, Rockstar's long-running Tesla parody, fight back. Try to steal one and it can lock you inside, then either drive itself to the nearest police station or keep you there until officers show up to arrest you, [IGN reported on November 23](https://me.ign.com/en/grand-theft-auto-vi/249973/why-you-probably-shouldnt-steal-a-tesla-in-gta-6). The clip that kicked it all off was posted on X on November 22 by Lucky Chamu, captioned "Do NOT steal is Tesla in GTA 6," as [Consequence noted](https://consequence.net/2026/11/gta-6-steal-tesla-coil-car/).

## How the Coil "Autonomous Security System" works

According to [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2026/11/gta-6-steal-tesla-coil-car/), each Coil carries an "Autonomous Security System." Once it triggers, the doors lock with you in the driver's seat, and the car takes over. Two outcomes have been reported:

- **The ride of shame:** the Coil drives itself, with you trapped inside, to the nearest police station.
- **The hold:** the car stays put and keeps you locked in until the cops arrive and arrest you.

IGN also says players found that Coil owners get "preferential treatment from the police." In other words, steal from the wrong driver and Leonida's cops show up fast.

## It's only the Coils

IGN's Rhiannon Bevan tested it herself and found that [none of the other Tesla-like EVs did the same thing](https://me.ign.com/en/grand-theft-auto-vi/249973/why-you-probably-shouldnt-steal-a-tesla-in-gta-6). Those cars still need a key cloner to steal, but you can hop into one that's already running and drive off. [VGTimes](https://vgtimes.com/gaming-news/175559-gta-6s-coil-cars-can-trap-carjackers-and-drive-them-to-the-police.html) adds some context from player testing:

- The Key Cloner, which bypasses electronic locks, unlocks in **Chapter 3**.
- Coils have been found in **Capri**, including spaces near a jewelry store in the town center.
- The trap doesn't seem to fire every time. Testers triggered it as Lucia but not as Jason, and some stole a second Coil afterward. That suggests it may be a scripted encounter rather than a rule for every theft.

Treat those details as early player findings, not confirmed rules.

## How to escape

There is one way out, but the car doesn't come with you. According to [IGN](https://me.ign.com/en/grand-theft-auto-vi/249973/why-you-probably-shouldnt-steal-a-tesla-in-gta-6):

1. **Smash a window** before the police get there.
2. **Climb out** and run.
3. If you wait, the cops will drag you out. IGN notes you'll likely be surrounded and "very much in tasering range" by then.

Is a Coil with its security switched off out there? IGN says it's unclear, and players are still looking.

## Why it matters: the Tesla satire

Rockstar has [parodied Tesla with Coil since GTA 5](https://me.ign.com/en/grand-theft-auto-vi/249973/why-you-probably-shouldnt-steal-a-tesla-in-gta-6). GTA 6 turns that joke into a gameplay system. Self-driving, always-online cars that report thieves and lock their doors are a pointed dig at real-world smart cars, and they fit right into a game where [real US police departments are already warning players](/articles/gta-6-police-departments-warn-players-officers-not-npcs/) about the line between fiction and reality. VGTimes also reports that Elon Musk shared a video of the in-game car on X with a laughing emoji.

It's another sign of how deep GTA 6's details go, right after players [found a portal back to Vice City](/articles/gta-6-vice-city-portal-easter-egg-tommy-vercetti-shirt-bike/).
