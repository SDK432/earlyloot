**Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 won October in the US, and it still can't crack 2026's top 10.** Circana's Mat Piscatella [confirmed on November 19](https://bsky.app/profile/matpiscatella.bsky.social/post/3n2oaaej4es2i) that MW4 was the best-selling game of October, the 19th consecutive year a Call of Duty release has topped its debut month. But on the year-to-date chart, MW4 only shows up in Piscatella's [list of ranks 11 to 15](https://bsky.app/profile/matpiscatella.bsky.social/post/3n2og3tnkk22i). That split is now the hottest debate in the CoD community.

## The streak survives

Piscatella notes the run [began in November 2008](https://bsky.app/profile/matpiscatella.bsky.social/post/3n2oaaiw43u2i), when World at War beat Guitar Hero: World Tour and Gears of War 2. MW4 extended it ahead of two other new releases, Gears of War: E-Day (2nd) and Star Wars: Galactic Racer (3rd). Circana ranks games by dollar sales and, as [Nintendo Life points out](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-tops-best-selling-games-in-october-us), shared no unit figures.

## The YTD problem

The [2026 year-to-date top 10](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/call-of-dutys-influence-is-fading-fast-as-modern-warfare-4-fails-to-make-us-top-10) is led by NBA 2K27, followed by Resident Evil: Requiem and 007 First Light. The sting for Activision: the only Call of Duty in it is **Black Ops 2**, at No. 6, thanks to the PS4/PS5 port that launched in July. [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/call-of-dutys-influence-is-fading-fast-as-modern-warfare-4-fails-to-make-us-top-10) calls the absence "unthinkable just a few short years ago" for a new CoD and wonders whether the Black Ops 7 backlash hurt MW4.

Before you write the obituary, here's the context:

- **Nine days on sale.** MW4 launched October 23 and Circana's period ended October 31, [Game Rant notes](https://gamerant.com/best-selling-games-2026-call-of-duty-mw4/). November is its first full month.
- **It's a tight race.** Piscatella says [less than $10M separates 8th from 13th](https://bsky.app/profile/matpiscatella.bsky.social/post/3n2ofxcfcfk2i) on the YTD chart.
- **No day-one Game Pass.** [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/best-selling-games-2026-call-of-duty-mw4/) points out Black Ops 7 hit Game Pass at launch, while Activision says MW4 won't join until holiday 2027.

The wider market isn't helping. Total US spending [fell 12% year over year to $4.2 billion](https://bsky.app/profile/matpiscatella.bsky.social/post/3n2oaafl3c22i) in October, and console content spending [dropped 18%](https://bsky.app/profile/matpiscatella.bsky.social/post/3n2oaaiw34m2i). Circana says moving CoD into October couldn't match last October's Battlefield 6 and Pokémon Legends: Z-A. November won't be easy either: [GTA 6 launched November 19](/articles/gta-6-now-available-rockstar-highest-rated-game-psn-outages-fix-tips/).

## Meanwhile, Infinity Ward is buffing guns

The same day, Infinity Ward's [November 19 patch](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes) kept pushing "range and lethality on full auto weapons", with LMGs included, in multiplayer:

- **Cronen Squall:** max damage range 27.94m to 31.24m, min damage 22 to 24.
- **Oden:** max damage range 33.02m to 36.83m, min damage 32 to 34.
- **PPSh-41:** max damage 18 to 19, plus a torso multiplier.
- **Type 73:** damage 22/19 to 23/20.
- **FiNN LMG:** max damage 30 to 31, with a slightly shorter max range.
- **Mosin-Nagant:** close-range damage cut from 95 to 91 and mid damage from 93 to 86. Infinity Ward called it out by name as over-performing.

On the Warzone side, Raven's [November 19 fixes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) mean you can use and cook lethals inside vehicles again. Raven also fixed sniper glint on the ZLR Swordfish optic and added the missing glint drawback to the Locus PX3 Canted Optic in Gunsmith. This comes on top of last week's [hitscan overhaul](/articles/mw4-hitscan-range-3-91x-ballistics-34ms-faster-ranked-season-2/).

## What's next

Circana's November report, the first full month for MW4 and the GTA 6 launch month, should land in December. Season 1 Reloaded is [expected around December 2](/articles/mw4-warzone-season-1-reloaded-release-date-dec-2-countdown-confirmed/). If MW4 jumps into the YTD top 10 after a full month, the "CoD is fading" talk gets a lot quieter. If it doesn't, expect it to get louder.
