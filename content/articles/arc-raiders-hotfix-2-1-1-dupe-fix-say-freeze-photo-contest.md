Embark Studios pushed out **ARC Raiders Hotfix 2.1.1** early on Friday, October 16. The [official Steam announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1808500/view/1846651706680764) went up at about 5:35 AM ET, and its goal is narrow but important: it closes "several instances" that let players **get into locked rooms and duplicate items**. One day earlier, the studio also announced [Say Freeze!](https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-photo-competition), a community photo contest built around the new camera gadget. Entries open today.

## What Hotfix 2.1.1 fixes

The patch note is short. According to [Embark's post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1808500/view/1846651706680764), the hotfix:

- **Blocks several ways into locked rooms** without the proper key.
- **Closes several item duplication exploits.**

Embark didn't say which methods were patched, and that's on purpose. The studio thanked players who reported the bugs privately through its **Discord support inbox or @ScrappyMail**, saying this lets the team act "without spreading the method publicly." As [ConsolePCGaming notes](https://consolepcgaming.com/arc-raiders-hotfix-2-1-1-is-deploying-to-fix-locked-room-access-and-item-duplication/), the fix arrives just three days after [Store Update 2.1.0](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-1-0), which launched the PvE toggle beta (we covered it [here](/articles/arc-raiders-update-2-1-pve-toggle-live/)).

## Why it matters

In an extraction shooter, locked rooms and duped gear hit the economy hard. Keys are supposed to be a gamble that pays off with high-value loot. When someone glitches in first, the player who actually used a key can find the room already stripped. This isn't the first round of the fight, either. In late 2025, [GameSpot reported](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/arc-raiders-introduces-hilarious-new-fix-for-locked-room-exploits-burn-everything/1100-6536614/) that Embark had started filling glitched-into rooms with flames to punish the exploit.

Timing matters too. Key rooms are already a flashpoint in the [PvE toggle test](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-what-to-know), where players have been [camping locked loot rooms](/articles/arc-raiders-pve-griefing-lure-grenades-dad-gamers/). Duped items also drag down the value of everything else in your stash.

## Say Freeze! photo contest: how to enter

The [Frozen Trail update](/articles/arc-raiders-frozen-trail-pendola-pass/) added a [camera gadget](https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-content-preview) with zoom, aperture presets, manual and auto focus, and a selfie mode with emotes. Now Embark wants to see what you've shot with it. Per the [official announcement](https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-photo-competition):

- **Submission window:** October 16 to 26.
- **Where to enter:** share your shots in the official ARC Raiders Discord.
- **Subjects:** Frozen Trail scenes such as the Frigate, the inside of the Emperor or Pendola Pass snowstorms. Selfies are "encouraged."
- **Prizes:** Embark developers will pick **10 favourites**. Each winner gets **1,150 Raider Tokens**, enough for cosmetics or the new Premium [Reward Pass](/articles/arc-raiders-reward-pass-explained/), plus an exclusive **Instant Film Camera backpack charm**.

Read the contest's terms and conditions, linked from the [announcement](https://arcraiders.com/news/frozen-trail-photo-competition), before you post.

## What's next

- **October 20, 11:00 CEST (5:00 AM ET):** the [PvE toggle beta ends](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-what-to-know). Embark says [feedback and in-game data](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-1-0) will decide whether it becomes permanent.
- **October 26:** Say Freeze! submissions close. Winners will be the developers' 10 favourite shots.
- **Exploits:** if you find a new one, Embark asks you to report it through the Discord support inbox or @ScrappyMail instead of posting it publicly.

We'll update EarlyLoot as soon as Embark shares PvE test results or more patch notes.
