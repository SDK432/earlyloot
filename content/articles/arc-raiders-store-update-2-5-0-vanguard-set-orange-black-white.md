Embark Studios rolled out **Store Update 2.5.0** for ARC Raiders on Tuesday, November 10, and it's another quick cosmetic drop. According to the [official update post](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-5-0), the patch adds **two new colour variants for the Vanguard Set: Orange and Black/White**. As with last week's store refresh, Embark says **no restart is required**, so the new looks appear without kicking anyone out of a raid.

## What's in Store Update 2.5.0

The [2.5.0 notes](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-5-0) are short, and the community-run [ARC Raiders Wiki](https://arcraiders.wiki/wiki/Update:2.5.0) logs the same single change:

- **Vanguard Set, Orange:** a bright new colourway for an outfit that's been in the store for months.
- **Vanguard Set, Black/White:** a high-contrast monochrome option.
- **No restart:** the content goes live server-side.

That's the whole list. There are no weapon, ARC, loot or map changes in this one, so your loadout and your Speranza stash are untouched.

## How much does Vanguard cost?

Embark's post doesn't list prices, but community trackers do. [ARCTracker](https://arctracker.io/cosmetics/outfit-vanguard) and [MetaForge](https://metaforge.app/arc-raiders/skins/outfits/vanguard) both show the **Vanguard Outfit at 1,300 Raider Tokens** on its own, or **1,600 Raider Tokens as part of the full Vanguard Set bundle**. Both trackers now list Orange and Black/White among the outfit's colour options. Treat those figures as a guide and check the in-game store before you spend, since Embark hasn't said anything about pricing for the new variants specifically, or whether they're time-limited.

## The weekly store rhythm is holding

2.5.0 is the second straight Tuesday store drop since Embark said it was going back to its normal schedule. [Store Update 2.4.0](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-4-0) on November 3 brought the Dead Eye Set and a Raider Tool skin fix ([our coverage](/articles/arc-raiders-store-update-2-4-0-dead-eye-set-raider-tool-fix/)), and in that post the studio confirmed **weekly store updates** and **gameplay fixes roughly every three weeks**. That cadence was first laid out in the [Live Update 2.3.0 notes](https://arcraiders.com/news/live-update-2-3-0) on October 27.

The most recent fix patch was [Hotfix 2.4.1](/articles/arc-raiders-hotfix-2-4-1-seed-vault-gondola-invisibility-xbox-crashes/) on November 5, which closed off the Seed Vault exploit and fixed Ballistic Gondola invisibility and several Xbox crashes.

## Bigger picture

The cosmetic drop lands a day after Nexon's Q3 report confirmed ARC Raiders has **passed 17 million copies sold**, while warning that Q4 revenue will cool off after the launch-year peak ([our breakdown](/articles/arc-raiders-17-million-copies-sold-nexon-q3-frozen-trail/)). Steady store refreshes like this are part of how Embark keeps players engaged between big content updates. For this week's Trials and Nomad trades, including the Gas Mask, see our [Nov 10–16 schedule](/articles/arc-raiders-next-week-nov-10-16-gas-mask-angry-mask-fragment/).

## What's next

- **Next store update:** if the weekly rhythm holds, expect the next one around **Tuesday, November 17**. Embark hasn't confirmed a date.
- **Next fix drop:** three weeks after Hotfix 2.4.1 would put the next gameplay fixes in **late November**. That's our estimate, not an announcement.
- **PvE decision:** Embark has said it will share more about PvE matchmaking in November; see our [year-two explainer](/articles/arc-raiders-year-two-plan-expeditions-early-2027-pve-november/) and CEO Patrick Söderlund's [latest comments](/articles/arc-raiders-ceo-could-just-add-pve-test-data-50-percent/).
