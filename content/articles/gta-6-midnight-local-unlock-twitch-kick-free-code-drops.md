Three days to go. **GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on November 19, 2026**, and Rockstar has put that in writing: its [support page on regional release times](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/29jV3Cv2cCgvwM1XgtMO5o/grand-theft-auto-vi-regional-pre-load-and-release-times-on-playstation-5-and-xbox-series-x-or-s) says the game releases "midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions" on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. And if you haven't bought it yet, Twitch and Kick are both handing out free Ultimate Edition codes to stream viewers.

## When GTA 6 unlocks where you live

Rockstar's wording is short. It doesn't include a table of time zones, so here's what the platform holders add:

- **PS5:** the [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/) describes a "rolling midnight launch by country." It starts at 12:00AM NZDT in New Zealand and moves west as each country reaches midnight.
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming:** [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/) says streaming starts at **1am ET on November 19**, on Xbox One and Series X|S.
- **Big countries:** [Digital Foundry](https://www.digitalfoundry.net/news/2026/11/rockstar-officially-confirms-gta-6-regional-release-times-and-theyre-not-what-we-thought) flagged that Sony's "by country" wording and Rockstar's "local time" wording can be read differently in countries with several time zones, like the US and Canada, and asked Rockstar to clarify. Until there's an answer, check the release time shown on your own store page.

Make sure the game is already installed. Pre-load has been live since November 10 ([our pre-load guide](/articles/gta-6-pre-load-live-30fps-ps5-pro-pssr-xbox-one-cloud/)). If you bought a boxed copy, redeem the code now. If anything goes wrong with it, read our [code-in-box explainer](/articles/gta-6-code-in-box-missing-used-codes-flashlight-claim/).

## Twitch: surprise daily code drops until November 30

Twitch revealed its GTA plans at the TwitchCon San Diego opening ceremony. In its [official recap](https://blog.twitch.tv/en/2026/11/13/twitchcon-san-diego-2026/), Twitch says it will do "surprise daily drops of GTA 6 game codes from launch through the end of November." [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151356/twitch-gta-6-ultimate-edition-code-drop-twitchcon-2026) reports that CEO Dan Clancy described them as Ultimate Edition copies. The same Twitch post lists more rewards that run from launch to November 30:

- GTA-themed Sub Emotes and Badges, plus an evolving badge that levels up as you watch GTA streams. Max it out for a **Golden Iguana** chat badge.
- **Heist Trains:** GTA-themed Hype Trains with six exclusive emotes made by streamer LumpyTouch.
- Gift Subs dropped into channels that publish GTA 6 Stream Recaps.

What Twitch hasn't shared yet: how many codes there are, how much watch time you need, and which channels take part. [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/kick-and-twitch-are-giving-away-thousands-of-gta-6-codes-to-viewers-before-launch-3428151/) noted the same gaps.

## Kick: the Sunset Reward Case

Kick's giveaway [started on November 12](https://about.kick.com/news-and-press/13-sunset-reward-case). It's simple: watch any stream for three hours to unlock a case, then open it from beside your avatar. According to Kick's [help page](https://help.kick.com/en/articles/18357299-sunset-reward-case-claim-a-free-copy-of-gta-vi-on-kick):

- The watch requirement resets every day, so you get a new chance daily. Winning is not guaranteed.
- Xbox codes are global. PS5 codes must match the country on your PSN account.
- Codes **won't be issued before November 19**, and some may arrive later after eligibility checks.

Dexerto says Kick's terms list **10,000 codes**, with the campaign running until December 7. Kick also says it isn't affiliated with Rockstar or Take-Two.

## What's next

- **November 18:** launch eve. The Heat play [as "Vice City" in Miami](/articles/gta-6-tv-ad-nba-alternate-launch-trailer-cut/), and YouTube's [24-hour launch stream](/articles/gta-6-24-hour-youtube-launch-livestream-30-creators-vice-city-shorts-live-gifts/) pre-show starts at 11 PM ET.
- **November 19:** midnight local unlock. Twitch daily drops begin, and Kick codes can start going out.
- **November 30:** last day of the Twitch GTA campaign and code drops.
- **December 7:** Kick's Sunset Reward Case ends, per Dexerto.
