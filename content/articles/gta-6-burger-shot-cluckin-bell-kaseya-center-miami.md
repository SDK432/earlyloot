You can now order a Burger Shot meal without booting up a console. As of this week, Miami Heat fans at the **Kaseya Center** can buy food from real-life versions of GTA's two most famous fast-food chains, **Burger Shot** and **Cluckin' Bell**, [GameSpot reported on October 22, 2026](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/you-can-now-buy-gta-6-burgers-and-fried-chicken-irl-but-its-not-cheap/). It's the latest stunt in Rockstar's ongoing push to turn Miami into Vice City before Grand Theft Auto VI launches on [November 19](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026).

One caveat up front: Rockstar hasn't announced the stands on its Newswire. Everything below comes from fans at the arena and the outlets that collected their reports.

## What's actually on the tray

In the games, Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell are parodies of Burger King and KFC. The real versions are less of a custom menu and more of a costume change:

- **Burger Shot:** according to fans who went to a game, the burgers are the same **Skinny Louie's** burgers the arena already sold, now in GTA 6 packaging. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/you-can-now-buy-gta-6-burgers-and-fried-chicken-irl-but-its-not-cheap/) says staff wear Burger Shot shirts and hats. [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/rockstar-is-quietly-transforming-the-real-miami-into-gta-6s-vice-city/) also describes the stand as a rebranded Skinny Louie's.
- **Cluckin' Bell:** a second stand sells fried chicken under the Cluckin' Bell name.

For now, the stands appear to exist only inside the Kaseya Center.

## How much a GTA 6 burger costs

Not cheap. GameSpot says the cheapest combo, a cheeseburger with fries and a drink, costs **$28.50 before tax**, and the **applewood bacon burger combo costs $31**. That's steep, but it's roughly what you'd expect to pay for food at a pro sports arena in 2026.

## The rest of the arena takeover

The food stands aren't the only GTA touches at the Heat's home court. GameSpot reports that the **elevators** now carry ads for in-game brands, including the radio station **Vice City FM**, **The Vault Nightclub** and the TV show **Macca & Friends**. Rockstar had already put the Vice City logo on top of the building.

The arena plan leads up to **"A Night In Vice City"** on **November 18**, the night before launch. The Heat will play as "Vice City" in themed jerseys on a GTA-inspired court. We covered the [first GTA 6 TV ad during NBA opening night](/articles/gta-6-tv-ad-nba-alternate-launch-trailer-cut/) earlier this week.

## Miami keeps turning into Vice City

The arena is just one piece of a citywide campaign. [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/rockstar-is-quietly-transforming-the-real-miami-into-gta-6s-vice-city/) rounds up sightings from across Miami:

- A GTA 6 blimp, murals, and Vice City branding on **buses and taxis**
- Pink and blue **Vice City umbrellas and loungers** on Miami Beach
- A real **Coquette** in a Vice City wrap and a **Lamborghini in Vice Beach Police livery**
- Ads for the in-game beers **Pißwasser** and **Patriot** outside Miami bars

On the beer, VGC says a label filing by Florida's **Tripping Animals Brewing** *suggests* real versions could go on sale. Nobody has confirmed that yet. For more on the cars and the local pushback, read our earlier story on [real GTA 6 cars hitting Miami's streets](/articles/gta-6-miami-real-cars-vice-city-pushback/).

## What's next

- **November 18:** "A Night In Vice City" Heat game at the Kaseya Center, with themed jerseys and court.
- **November 19:** [Grand Theft Auto VI launches](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/ak3ak31a49a221/grand-theft-auto-vi-is-now-set-to-launch-november-19-2026) on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

If you're in Miami for a Heat game before then, bring an appetite and about $30 for the full Burger Shot experience.
