Critics call it the best game ever made. Metacritic users can't agree on whether it's even good. At the time of writing, Grand Theft Auto VI's PS5 page on [Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/) shows a **99 Metascore from 152 critic reviews, all 152 positive**, next to a **user score of 5.7 from 105,906 ratings**. And on November 23 the fight spilled onto other games: [VGC reports](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/as-gta-6-gets-review-bombed-on-metacritic-gta-fans-have-started-review-bombing-ocarina-of-time-in-response/) that GTA fans are now dumping zeros on The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time in revenge.

## The numbers behind the 5.7

Metacritic's own breakdown of user ratings, at the time of writing ([source](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/)):

- **Positive:** 50% (52,626 ratings)
- **Mixed:** 7% (7,590)
- **Negative:** 43% (45,690)

So more users like it than hate it, and the score still lands in "Mixed or Average." That's what happens when the votes cluster at the extremes. The slide started early. [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/gta-6-metacritic-user-score-review-bombed/) counted a 5.5 from 34,804 ratings on the morning of November 21. In roughly two days the vote count tripled and the score barely moved.

## Bombed before launch, bombed again after

This started before anyone could play. [VGC reported](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/metacritic-has-already-had-to-remove-fake-gta-6-user-reviews/) on November 17 that fake GTA 6 user reviews had shown up days before release. Metacritic deleted them and closed the page. Normally the site doesn't take user reviews until a game has been out for at least 36 hours.

The wait didn't help much. Once the window closed, [VGC says](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/as-gta-6-gets-review-bombed-on-metacritic-gta-fans-have-started-review-bombing-ocarina-of-time-in-response/) the zeros came right back. So why are people angry? According to outlets that dug through the reviews, these are the most common complaints:

- **"Relationship simulator":** VGC says many zero scores attack the focus on keeping Lucia happy, and some come with culture-war comments.
- **30fps cap and pacing:** [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/articles/gta-6-review-bombed-on-metacritic) lists the 30fps target, a slow Chapter 1 spent fixing up Jason's house, bugs and "too woke" complaints. It also mentions the gripe that you can't steal cars in the starting neighborhood.

## Ocarina of Time takes the hit

Here's where it gets petty. GTA 6's 99 ties it with Ocarina of Time for the highest critic score on Metacritic, and some GTA fans are convinced Zelda fans are behind the zeros. [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/as-gta-6-gets-review-bombed-on-metacritic-gta-fans-have-started-review-bombing-ocarina-of-time-in-response/) found 0/10 reviews aimed at the Ocarina of Time remake, which still holds an 8.3 user score, and at the 1998 N64 original. SoulCalibur, No. 3 on the all-time list, got hit too. [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/articles/gta-6-review-bombed-on-metacritic) describes the same fight in both directions.

## True or False: you need to own a game to rate it on Metacritic?

**False.** [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/articles/gta-6-review-bombed-on-metacritic) notes that Metacritic lets anyone post a user rating without proving they own or played the game. [GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/gta-6-metacritic-user-score-review-bombed/), citing Push Square, says the picture looks very different on the PlayStation Store. Only buyers can rate there, and GTA 6 had 4.84 out of 5 from about 170,000 ratings.

## Meanwhile, Rockstar is patching

Rockstar's first post-launch update, **01.002.002**, started rolling out November 23. It's 603.8 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-first-patch-is-rolling-out/) lists fixes for crashes, weather that wouldn't change, broken autosaves, phone calls that didn't trigger story missions, Wanted Levels you couldn't lose, the gym deadlift bar and partners not handing over bags for fences.

## What's next

Metacritic has removed review-bombing waves before, [GTABase notes](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/articles/gta-6-review-bombed-on-metacritic), so the user score could still move.
