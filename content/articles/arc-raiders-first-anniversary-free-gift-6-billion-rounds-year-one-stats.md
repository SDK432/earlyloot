ARC Raiders is officially one year old. On Friday, October 30, 2026, exactly a year after launch, Embark Studios published a [thank-you post on the official site](https://arcraiders.com/news/one-year-anniversary). It comes with two things worth knowing about: a **free gift that's already waiting in your in-game inbox**, and a [year-one infographic](https://assets.arcraiders.com/media/ArcRaiders_FirstAnniversary_Infographic_16x9-Approved-1024x576.jpg) packed with some properly ridiculous numbers.

## How to claim the free anniversary gift

You don't need a code. Embark calls it "a small token of our appreciation" and says you only have to log in and open your inbox to [collect it](https://arcraiders.com/news/one-year-anniversary). The studio hasn't said what the item is or how long you have to claim it, and [Digg](https://digg.com/gaming/ua0pna8a) notes that the official X post doesn't name it either. So check your inbox today rather than waiting.

Per the same [Digg roundup](https://digg.com/gaming/ua0pna8a), the official account also lined up an anniversary Twitch stream featuring Ossen, Raz and guest developers.

## Year one in numbers

Embark's ["Your First Year in Numbers" graphic](https://assets.arcraiders.com/media/ArcRaiders_FirstAnniversary_Infographic_16x9-Approved-1024x576.jpg) covers 2025 to 2026. The highlights:

- **6 billion** rounds played
- **Over 14.8 billion** ARC destroyed
- **3,920,595** Queens and **2,919,521** Matriarchs taken down
- **The Anvil** was the Rust Belt's favourite weapon
- **6,800 tonnes** of food fed to Scrappy, whose favourite snack is **apricots**
- **5,643** Raiders healed by a single Raider, and a record of **18** strangers healed in one raid
- **3,625** Raiders taken out by a volcanic rock

That last one is our favourite. Embark doesn't explain how it counted any of these, so treat them as the studio's own figures.

## A huge first year

The anniversary caps a big run. In its [press release for the anniversary gear collection](https://corsair-newsroom.prgloo.com/news/corsair-and-embark-studios-mark-one-year-of-arc-raiders-by-sending-exclusive-raider-grade-tech-topside), CORSAIR says ARC Raiders has sold **more than 16 million copies** worldwide and won Best Multiplayer Game at The Game Awards 2025. CORSAIR also says orders from that collection start shipping on October 30. We covered the [full list of in-game rewards and prices here](/articles/arc-raiders-corsair-collection-live-in-game-rewards-azimuth-outfit/).

October alone brought the [Frozen Trail update and Pendola Pass](/articles/arc-raiders-frozen-trail-pendola-pass/), a week-long [PvE matchmaking test](/articles/arc-raiders-pve-mode-test-october-13/), and the [Soul's Offering Raider Project in Update 2.3](/articles/arc-raiders-update-2-3-souls-offering-live-heavy-shield-flame-spray/).

## What's next

Embark isn't sharing a roadmap yet. The anniversary post only says the team is "already hard at work on what comes next" and has "lots planned for the future." Here's what's on the calendar:

- **Today, October 30:** log in and claim the anniversary gift from your inbox
- **Through November 1:** the current weekly rotation, with the Cauldron and Cultist backpack ([our week guide](/articles/arc-raiders-next-week-oct-26-nov-1-cauldron-cultist-nomad-trades/))
- **November:** Embark has said it will share more on the PvE test results ([background here](/articles/arc-raiders-pve-mode-future-embark-grondal-interview/))

Here's to year two, Raiders. See you Topside.
