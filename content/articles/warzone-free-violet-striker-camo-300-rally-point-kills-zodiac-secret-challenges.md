**There's a free purple camo waiting in Warzone's new Rally Point mode, and you can farm it off bots.** According to [Game Rant's guide](https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-cod-modern-warfare-4-mw4-warzone-free-violet-striker-camo-how-to-get/) (November 9), the **Violet Striker** camo unlocks after **300 kills of players or hostiles in Rally Point**, and it then works on Modern Warfare 4 weapons too. The [official Season 1 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) confirm that Rally Point awards "one exclusive Weapon Blueprint and two Weapon Camos", but they don't name the camos or list how to earn them. The requirements below come from outlets and content creators, not from Activision.

## What Rally Point is

The [patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) describe Rally Point as a **20-player mode on Zodiac** with no gas circle, unlimited Resurgence and drop-in/drop-out play. You fight Hostile Soldiers, complete Contracts and build up Cash. A single Nuke can be bought each match, as we covered in our [Rally Point nuke explainer](/articles/warzone-rally-point-zodiac-buy-nuke-1-million-season-1/).

## How to unlock Violet Striker

- **Goal:** 300 total kills in Rally Point, per [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-cod-modern-warfare-4-mw4-warzone-free-violet-striker-camo-how-to-get/).
- **Bots count:** Game Rant says AI kills and player kills both add to the total. [NeoTeo](https://www.neoteo.com/en/warzone-zodiac-secret-challenges-reported-rewards) cites creator CovertMF saying the same thing.
- **Farming tip:** Game Rant had the most luck on the **outskirts of Zodiac**, where fresh soldiers keep dropping in as others are eliminated.
- **Out of targets?** Game Rant suggests starting a **Supply Run Contract**, which marks a nearby Buy Station and spawns tougher enemies to farm.
- **Where it works:** Game Rant reports that it unlocks as a universal camo, sits in the Special Camo tab, and can be equipped on MW4 weapons in Multiplayer and DMZ.

## Every reported Zodiac secret challenge

[NeoTeo](https://www.neoteo.com/en/warzone-zodiac-secret-challenges-reported-rewards) put together nine Zodiac objectives from creator walkthroughs and a Windows Central guide. The outlet itself stresses that they are reported requirements, not an official Activision list:

- **Tourist Trap** calling card: visit every Zodiac point of interest.
- **Fame** animated calling card: show up on Zodiac's smart displays eight times.
- **Classified** camo: 10 eliminations with a Classified weapon. The [patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes) do confirm that a hidden Classified Weapon challenge unlocks that weapon's camo.
- **Violet Striker** camo: 300 Rally Point kills.
- **Neutral Ground** camo: down a supply drone, grab its camo case and hold it for three minutes without dying.
- **EXE** weapon charm: pick up the EXE phone in six different matches.
- **Zodiac Railway** loading screen: play five matches on Zodiac.
- **Hot Range** sticker: finish all eight firing-range tasks at Sentinel.
- **Renaissance** animated camo: win four Resurgence or Casual Resurgence matches.

NeoTeo also notes that some viewers have reported tracking problems with the Classified objective, so don't panic if progress seems to stall.

## Stack them for a faster grind

Several of these overlap. Grab the EXE phone in each Zodiac match while you work toward Zodiac Railway's five games, and tick off points of interest for Tourist Trap along the way. If you'd rather chase a free Blueprint, our guide to the [Unserialized Signal 50 challenge](/articles/warzone-unserialized-signal-50-blueprint-free-25-weapon-trades-resurgence/) explains the 25-trade route in Resurgence.

## What's next

Season 1 kicked off on November 6, according to the [official Call of Duty blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1). Activision hasn't said whether these Zodiac rewards will expire, so grinding them during Season 1 is the safe bet. We'll update this post if Call of Duty publishes official requirements or a mid-season update changes Rally Point.
