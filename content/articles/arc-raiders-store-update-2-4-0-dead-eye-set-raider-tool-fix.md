Embark Studios pushed **Store Update 2.4.0** to ARC Raiders on Tuesday, November 3, and it's a small one. According to the [official patch post](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-4-0), the update adds the **Dead Eye Set** to the in-game store and fixes a bug where the **Raider Tool showed up as the default look** even when players had a different skin equipped. Best of all, Embark says **no restart is required**, so you can keep raiding while it rolls out.

## What's in Store Update 2.4.0

The [2.4.0 notes](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-4-0) list just two changes:

- **Dead Eye Set:** a new cosmetic set is now available in the store. Embark's post doesn't list its pieces or price, so check the store in-game for details.
- **Raider Tool skin fix:** the Raider Tool no longer falls back to its default appearance when you have a different skin equipped. If you paid for a custom look and kept seeing the stock tool, that should be sorted now.

Because it's a live update with no client restart, this isn't the kind of patch that touches weapons, ARC behavior or loot. Balance changes still come through the bigger fix drops.

## Back to the regular schedule

The more useful part of the post is the reminder about timing. Embark says it is "back to our regular schedule" of **weekly store updates** and **fixes every three weeks**, and that the team is watching for critical issues ([ARC Raiders](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-4-0)).

That matches what the studio laid out in the [Live Update 2.3.0 notes](https://arcraiders.com/news/live-update-2-3-0) on October 27, which brought back the roughly three-week fix cycle alongside weekly store refreshes. It also sits inside the bigger plan from May, when Embark [moved to two major updates a year](https://arcraiders.com/news/development-update) with a live team handling fixes, store updates and events in between. Our [Update 2.3 breakdown](/articles/arc-raiders-update-2-3-souls-offering-live-heavy-shield-flame-spray/) and [year-two explainer](/articles/arc-raiders-year-two-plan-expeditions-early-2027-pve-november/) cover that cadence in more detail.

For this week's Trials and Nomad trades, see our [Nov 2–8 schedule](/articles/arc-raiders-next-week-nov-2-8-snap-hook-hullcracker-vintage-mic/).

## Meanwhile: Embark on AI voices

The store drop lands a day after [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/third-person-shooter/there-was-some-really-fair-criticism-arc-raiders-devs-understand-why-players-were-so-upset-about-its-ai-use-but-admit-that-it-was-too-impractical-to-record-every-voice-line/) highlighted comments from Embark's audio team in the studio's "Evolution of ARC Raiders" video series. Executive producer Aleksander Grøndal said there was "some really fair criticism" of the game's AI-assisted voice lines, while audio director Bence Pajor argued it was "too impractical to record every possible version" of the ping dialogue as the game kept changing. PC Gamer notes Embark has since re-recorded lines to add more real human voices. We covered Grøndal's PvE comments [here](/articles/arc-raiders-pve-mode-future-embark-grondal-interview/).

- **PvE news:** Embark has said it expects to share more on PvE this month; see our [year-two explainer](/articles/arc-raiders-year-two-plan-expeditions-early-2027-pve-november/).
