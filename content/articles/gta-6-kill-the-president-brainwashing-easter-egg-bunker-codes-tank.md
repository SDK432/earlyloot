Did you know there's a full-blown conspiracy hiding in plain sight in Downtown Vice City? GTA 6 has a secret side quest chain, nicknamed **"Kill the President,"** that drags you through two hidden government bunkers, hands you a **tank** and a unique assault rifle, then straps you into a chair for a trippy brainwashing sequence. [Polygon mapped out the whole thing](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-brainwashing-president-easter-egg-walkthrough/) on Nov 27, 2026, and here's what you need to know.

## How it starts: a guy at a statue

Head to the **Joe Russo monument in Downtown Vice City** during the day and talk to **José Bermúdez**, the man sitting in front of it. When he starts talking, pick **"What really happened?"** He'll send you to his apartment, a salmon-colored building with Cuban flags on **NW 4th Avenue in Little Cuba**, [per Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-brainwashing-president-easter-egg-walkthrough/).

Inside you'll find a bunker blueprint on the wall, some recordings, and a photo of President Russo on **Sharktooth Island**. Listen to the recording: Russo basically reads you the next code himself.

## Bunker #1: Sharktooth Island

Sharktooth Island sits on the south side of Little Hardin Island in Leonard County. Head northwest on the island to find the bunker keypad. The code:

- **Bunker door: 010317**

Flip the lights on at the electrical box, then find the presidential desk next to a big safe. Search the drawer for a **"Protocol"** file with three number sequences. A recording in an earlier room mentions **"5297"** and **"protocol two,"** and moving the last digit to the front gives you:

- **Safe: 2975**

Inside: **Russo's 1922 pistol** and a photo with **"OBSESS"** written on the back, [Polygon notes](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-brainwashing-president-easter-egg-walkthrough/).

## Bunker #2: the IAA Black Site (and the tank)

The photo points to a second bunker in **Grassrivers, Mariana County**, southwest of Richardson Hunting Supplies. The main entrance is shut, so look for a hatch to the northeast. Read "OBSESS" as numbers and you get:

- **Hatch: 085355**

Turn on the power, find the control room and hit the **two red buttons**. The garage opens up with a **tank**, the **Cold War 556** unique assault rifle, ammo and grenades. Polygon says the main entrance stays open after that, so you can come back anytime.

## The brainwashing trip

You're not done. A room near the entrance has a chair facing a TV that's now on. Sit down and the game flips to **first person**, trapping you in a looping corridor in the past. From there it's a puzzle of ringing phones, a clock that time-travels you between past and future, and three "candidates" you have to track down. [Polygon's walkthrough](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-brainwashing-president-easter-egg-walkthrough/) has every step and code if you get stuck.

Finish it and you're rewarded with the **"Brainwashed" award tattoo** and the **Brainwasher 1922** unique pistol next to the TV.

## Why it's worth your time

It's one of GTA 6's deepest secrets so far, right up there with the [Vice City portal easter egg](/articles/gta-6-vice-city-portal-easter-egg-tommy-vercetti-shirt-bike/) and the [hidden Backrooms trip](/articles/gta-6-secret-backrooms-trip-lake-tallaloko-easter-egg/). A free tank alone makes the detour worth it.
