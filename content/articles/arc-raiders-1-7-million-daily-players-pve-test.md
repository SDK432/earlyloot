ARC Raiders just had its busiest stretch in more than six months. According to [new estimates from Alinea Analytics](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/two-october-2025-games-popped-off), published October 16, Embark's extraction shooter peaked at **about 1.7 million daily active players across all platforms on Wednesday, October 14**. That was one day after the [PvE toggle beta](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-what-to-know) went live, and two days after the Frozen Trail free weekend ended. These are third-party estimates, not official numbers from Embark, but they line up with the Steam spike we saw [after Frozen Trail launched](/articles/arc-raiders-player-count-7-month-high-frozen-trail/).

## The numbers, day by day

Here's how Alinea's analyst Rhys Elliott [breaks it down](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/two-october-2025-games-popped-off):

- **Before the update:** the game was still drawing roughly 500,000 daily players. [TechPowerUp, citing Alinea](https://www.techpowerup.com/353802/arc-raiders-launches-to-6-month-high-after-free-weekend-pve-only-test-and-frozen-trail-update), puts the day before at about 599,000.
- **October 8 (Frozen Trail + free weekend):** about 944,000 players jumped in.
- **Free weekend (Oct 8–12):** daily players climbed to around 1.6 million, and the free period brought in roughly 150,000 new players.
- **After the free weekend:** instead of dropping, daily players held near 1.6 million and **peaked at 1.7 million on October 14**. Alinea says that's about 10% of everyone who has bought the game.

On Steam alone, [TechPowerUp notes](https://www.techpowerup.com/353802/arc-raiders-launches-to-6-month-high-after-free-weekend-pve-only-test-and-frozen-trail-update) that SteamDB's peak concurrent count rose from 152,547 on October 12 to 164,228 on October 13, the day the PvE test started. [Insider Gaming points out](https://insider-gaming.com/arc-raiders-pve-study-asked-1100-players-what-they-think/) that the Frozen Trail launch peak was even higher, at 185,443.

## Sales and spending are up too

The free weekend worked as a sales pitch. Alinea [estimates](https://alineaanalytics.substack.com/p/two-october-2025-games-popped-off) that **150,000 new copies sold in the three days after it ended**, worth nearly $3 million. The game was 50% off at $19.99, its deepest discount yet, until October 16 on Steam and Xbox (Alinea said it was still active on PS5). The firm also estimates **another $2 million** this month from cosmetic collector sets on Steam, about three-quarters of it from the new Frozen Trail Collector Set. It puts lifetime revenue at around **$710 million**.

## Is PvE the reason?

It's a big part of the story, though not all of it. Alinea credits the new content *and* the PvE test for keeping players around. It says its Steam review sentiment analysis shows most players "loving the mode," while calling it "polarising." That matches what we've seen: some players [love the chill raids](/articles/arc-raiders-pve-toggle-player-reactions/), while others complain about [griefers and loot vultures](/articles/arc-raiders-pve-griefing-lure-grenades-dad-gamers/). Insider Gaming's Grant Taylor-Hill [collected 1,100+ comments](https://insider-gaming.com/arc-raiders-pve-study-asked-1100-players-what-they-think/) on a video about the toggle and found overwhelming support. He also admits YouTube's algorithm may have skewed the sample toward PvE fans.

## What's next

- **October 20, 11:00 CEST (5:00 AM ET):** the [PvE toggle beta ends](https://arcraiders.com/news/pve-toggle-what-to-know). Embark plans to send a survey afterward.
- **The big decision:** Embark says player feedback and in-game data [will decide](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-1-0) whether the toggle becomes permanent. A 1.7 million daily peak is the kind of data point that will be hard to ignore.
- **Patches:** Embark is still shipping fixes, most recently [Hotfix 2.1.1](/articles/arc-raiders-hotfix-2-1-1-dupe-fix-say-freeze-photo-contest/) against dupes and locked-room exploits.
