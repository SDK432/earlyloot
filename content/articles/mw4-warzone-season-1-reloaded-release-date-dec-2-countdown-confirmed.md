**Modern Warfare 4 and Warzone Season 1 Reloaded looks set for the night of December 2, but Activision hasn't confirmed it yet.** [Dot Esports reports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/guides/mw4-warzone-season-1-reloaded-release-date) that the in-game countdown for the Season 1 Battle Pass Bonus Rewards, which unlock at mid-season, ends on **December 2 at 10pm PT** (December 3 at 1am ET / 6am GMT). Activision's own [Warzone Season 1 blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1) still only says more is coming "in the Mid-Season", so treat that date as expected, not official.

## When does Season 1 Reloaded come out?

Here's where things stand on November 18:

- **Official date:** none. [Timesaver notes](https://timesaver.gg/blog/mw4-season-1-reloaded-release-date) that Activision's Season 1 posts only promise Reloaded intel "closer to the Mid-Season update."
- **Expected date:** December 2 at 10pm PT, based on the Bonus Rewards timer ([Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/guides/mw4-warzone-season-1-reloaded-release-date)).
- **Different hour elsewhere:** [Pro-Gamer](https://pro-gamer.gg/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-reloaded-release-date-content-guide/) reads the same countdown as **December 3 at 9am PT**.

Either way, that's about four weeks after the November 6 launch, which [Fenix Bazaar points out](https://fenixbazaar.com/2026/11/18/mw4-season-1-reloaded-release-date/) is in line with earlier Call of Duty mid-season updates. The exact time could still change until Activision publishes its Reloaded blog.

## New Multiplayer maps and modes

The [PlayStation Blog's Season 1 post](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/28/modern-warfare-4-season-1-everything-you-need-to-know-for-november-6-launch/) already names what's coming to Multiplayer at mid-season:

- **Ministry:** an all-new map built around a damaged embassy during a diplomatic summit.
- **Overflow:** a factory surrounded by farmland.
- **Counter Attack:** a new mode where you detonate bombs at the enemy Comm Station while defending your own.
- **More maps and modes:** [Timesaver quotes](https://timesaver.gg/blog/mw4-season-1-reloaded-release-date) Activision promising additional [CLASSIFIED] maps, plus a new mode that puts a twist on Team Deathmatch.

## Warzone: two new LTMs

The [official Warzone blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1) confirms that **Extreme Resurgence** and **Rockets and Snipers** arrive at mid-season. Rules haven't been revealed yet.

Verdansk is coming back, but the same blog only lists it as "In-Season" and says it "returns later in Season 1", with no date. It may or may not land with Reloaded. Our [Warzone Season 1 roadmap](/articles/warzone-season-1-roadmap-hajin-zodiac-verdansk-rebirth-switch-2/) has the full picture.

## JAK 12, Talonhawk and Battle Pass bonus skins

- **JAK 12:** a full-auto shotgun, unlocked through an in-season event ([Timesaver](https://timesaver.gg/blog/mw4-season-1-reloaded-release-date)). Activision hasn't said whether that event starts on Reloaded day.
- **ZLR Talonhawk:** a combat axe, also earned through an event ([Fenix Bazaar](https://fenixbazaar.com/2026/11/18/mw4-season-1-reloaded-release-date/)). We break down both unlocks in our [Season 1 weapons guide](/articles/mw4-warzone-season-1-weapons-unlock-tiers-osprey-csg-35-jak-12-talonhawk/).
- **Bonus Rewards:** [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/guides/mw4-warzone-season-1-reloaded-release-date) and [Pro-Gamer](https://pro-gamer.gg/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-reloaded-release-date-content-guide/) both report **eight extra Operator skins** for players who finish 100% of the Battle Pass. You can unlock them in any order, one at a time.

## What about Ranked Play?

Ranked isn't part of any Reloaded plans announced so far, as [Fenix Bazaar notes](https://fenixbazaar.com/2026/11/18/mw4-season-1-reloaded-release-date/). Infinity Ward has instead pointed to Season 2, as we [reported earlier this month](/articles/mw4-hitscan-range-3-91x-ballistics-34ms-faster-ranked-season-2/).

## What's next

- **Now through November 23:** the [Natural Concealment event](/articles/mw4-natural-concealment-event-woodfall-camo-drill-charge-nerf/) is still live, so grab the Woodfall camo before Reloaded arrives.
- **Before December 2:** finish your Battle Pass if you want to start on the bonus skins right away.
- **In the coming days:** Activision's Season 1 Reloaded blog should confirm the date, time and patch notes. We'll update you as soon as it's posted.
