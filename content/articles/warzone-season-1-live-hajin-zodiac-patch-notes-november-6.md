**Warzone and Modern Warfare 4 Season 1 are live.** Servers flipped at midnight ET on November 6 (9 PM PT November 5), matching the [Activision Support seasons page](https://support.activision.com/modern-warfare-4/articles/modern-warfare-4-warzone-seasons) and the [official Call of Duty Season 1 blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1). Client builds show as **MW4 / Warzone 1.111** with **Multiplayer Pack 1.003**, per [MP1st's Update 1.111 write-up](https://mp1st.com/title-updates-and-patches/modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-update-ver-1-111-brings-flood-of-new-conten).

We already covered the [release-time window](/articles/mw4-warzone-season-1-release-time-november-6-midnight-et/), the [Season 1 roadmap](/articles/warzone-season-1-roadmap-hajin-zodiac-verdansk-rebirth-switch-2/), and the [full intel package](/articles/mw4-season-1-full-intel-x01-black-site-csg-35-hajin-zodiac-november-6/). This post is the launch-day patch digest: what shipped, what changed, and what to close before you queue.

## Warzone: Hajin, Zodiac, and full MW4 integration

Season 1 completes the Warzone / MW4 merge. Per [Activision Support](https://support.activision.com/modern-warfare-4/articles/modern-warfare-4-warzone-seasons) and the [official patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes):

- **Hajin** — new Battle Royale map set in Korea, with **13 POIs** and dynamic weather.
- **Zodiac** — new Resurgence map built around the DRC Advanced Systems drone HQ.
- **Level reset / Prestige** across Multiplayer, DMZ, and Warzone.
- **30+ MW4 weapons** carry into Warzone, plus Season 1 Battle Pass guns.
- Launch modes: **BR**, **Resurgence** (including Casual), **Rally Point** on Zodiac (20 players), **Massive Resurgence**, and **Loaded Resurgence**.

New kit includes a **Zipline Launcher**, a **Grabber Drone**, and interactive **fuel barrels** / pushable cover. For the weapon track itself, see our [Season 1 unlock tiers guide](/articles/mw4-warzone-season-1-weapons-unlock-tiers-osprey-csg-35-jak-12-talonhawk/).

## Default Tactical Sprint and QoL combat changes

[Activision Support](https://support.activision.com/modern-warfare-4/articles/modern-warfare-4-warzone-seasons) confirms **Tactical Sprint is now the default** for every Operator, with a slightly shortened duration. You can also **drag allies while reviving**, and Activision lists improved visibility plus footstep audio tuning. We flagged the Tac Sprint shift earlier in [Warzone Tactical Sprint default for Season 1](/articles/warzone-tactical-sprint-default-season-1/).

Battle Pass launch weapons are the **Cronen Osprey** assault rifle and the **CSG-35** SMG — both live on the Season 1 track today.

## Last-gen Warzone ends; Warzone Agent blocked

With Season 1, **PS4 and Xbox One Warzone support ends**. Progression carries over on a linked Activision account, per [Activision Support](https://support.activision.com/modern-warfare-4/articles/modern-warfare-4-warzone-seasons).

Anti-cheat also hardens: starting Season 1, the lobby-shopping third-party app **Warzone Agent** is blocked. Close it or lose Multiplayer access; circumvention can lead to bans, according to [MP1st](https://mp1st.com/title-updates-and-patches/modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-update-ver-1-111-brings-flood-of-new-conten) and the [official patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-mw4-warzone-season-1-patch-notes).

## MW4 Multiplayer, DMZ, and Performance Mode

On the MW4 side, Season 1 adds maps **Backstab**, **Canyon**, and a reimagined **Shoot House**, plus modes **Grind** and **Drop Zone**. DMZ opens the **X01** black site. [MP1st](https://mp1st.com/title-updates-and-patches/modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-update-ver-1-111-brings-flood-of-new-conten) also notes **Performance Mode 120Hz** is now available on all PS5 and Xbox Series X|S consoles with 120Hz displays, with or without VRR.

## What's next

Season 1 is live now — download Update **1.111**, close Warzone Agent if you still have it running, and pick Hajin BR or Zodiac Resurgence. Mid-season still holds Verdansk and Rebirth Island returns; we'll cover those as Activision locks dates.
