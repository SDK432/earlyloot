**Modern Warfare 4's DMZ mode is free to play through November 11, 2026 at 10 AM PT.** The [Xbox store listing](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-dmz-free-trial/9npw26k5sx07) and matching [PlayStation Store trial](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP0002-PPSA01649_00-CODMW4TRIAL00001) opened with Season 1 on November 6, giving full DMZ access on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC (Steam / Battle.net) without owning the game.

[Call of Duty](https://x.com/CallofDuty/status/2118823701433188466) announced the window on November 6. [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-dmz-mode-free-trial-on-all-platforms/) and [GamerHeadlines](https://gamerheadlines.com/modern-warfare-4-dmz-free-trial-november-11/) confirmed the same end date and platform coverage. For the Season 1 launch overview — Hajin, Zodiac, and patch notes — see [Warzone Season 1 Is Live](/articles/warzone-season-1-live-hajin-zodiac-patch-notes-november-6/).

## What the free trial unlocks

The trial is the **full DMZ experience**, not a stripped demo. You drop into **Hajin**, solo or in a squad, and run the same loop as paying owners: loot, fight, complete objectives, and extract.

Season 1 also put the new **X01 weapons facility** into the mode — a tunnel maze with shifting hazards, new equipment, and **DMZ Orders** inside the facility. Threat Briefings and Resupply Runs sit in the same Season 1 package. Extraction points can close as a run progresses, so finishing the required steps before exits vanish is part of the loop. Full Season 1 facility and Black Site intel is in our [MW4 Season 1 Full Intel](/articles/mw4-season-1-full-intel-x01-black-site-csg-35-hajin-zodiac-november-6/) guide.

## What DMZ normally costs

DMZ has never been free the way Warzone is. Since launch, players needed to own Modern Warfare 4 to queue extraction. Warzone remains the free battle royale, and Season 1 added Hajin there as a BR map — but DMZ itself stayed behind the purchase. This five-day window is the first chance since launch to try the mode without buying or pre-ordering MW4.

## Fine print

A few requirements still apply:

- **Console online** needs PlayStation Plus or Xbox Game Pass / online multiplayer access.
- An **Activision account** may need a linked phone number before you can play.
- The trial covers **DMZ only** — not Campaign or Multiplayer.
- In-game purchases are optional. Progression sits on the linked Activision account and carries over when you sign in on a supported platform.

After **November 11 at 10 AM PT**, DMZ returns to requiring Modern Warfare 4 ownership.

## Season 1 stealth fix worth knowing

While you are in the trial, note a related Season 1 stealth fix from the [MW4 Season 1 patch notes](https://www.callofduty.com/patchnotes/2026/11/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-season-1-patch-notes): **Thermal Padding**, **Thermal Padding Pro**, and the **Sedative Inhaler** now correctly hide you from standard UAVs and UAV Upgrade towers. Advanced UAV Fire Support can still detect them — so treat AUAV coverage as the remaining counter while you learn X01 routes.
