ARC Raiders has now sold **more than 17 million units**. Nexon disclosed the figure on **November 10, 2026**, in its [third-quarter 2026 earnings release](https://pdf.irpocket.com/C3659/xoA3/DnNg/UwmY/FseY.pdf), a little over a year after Embark Studios' extraction shooter launched in October 2025. The publisher gives much of the credit for the latest stretch to [Frozen Trail](/articles/arc-raiders-frozen-trail-pendola-pass/), the October 8 update it calls the largest since launch.

## What Nexon actually said

The [earnings release](https://pdf.irpocket.com/C3659/xoA3/DnNg/UwmY/FseY.pdf) keeps it short, but the key points are clear:

- **17 million+ units sold** to date.
- **Frozen Trail (October 8)** was "successful in re-engaging lapsed players and driving incremental unit sales."
- **Q3 revenue** for ARC Raiders "came in within the outlook."
- Nexon CEO Junghun Lee named "new games like ARC Raiders" as one reason the company now relies less on any single franchise or market.

On the [earnings call](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-nexon-beats-q3-outlook-as-maplestory-surges-shares-slip-93CH-5014475), Executive Chairman Patrick Söderlund said Frozen Trail grew the community with players "who bought the game a full year after its release." [PCGamesN quotes Nexon](https://www.pcgamesn.com/arc-raiders/17-million-sales) as saying: "This is a performance we expect to see from a franchise, and why we built Arc Raiders for the long term."

## Why Frozen Trail mattered

Frozen Trail brought the [new Pendola Pass map](/articles/arc-raiders-frozen-trail-pendola-pass/), new ARC enemies, new weapons and a skill tree refresh, [per PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/arc-raiders/17-million-sales). The boost showed up outside the sales figures too: we covered the [seven-month player-count high](/articles/arc-raiders-player-count-7-month-high-frozen-trail/) that followed the update.

Söderlund was also frank about the comedown. [On the call](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-nexon-beats-q3-outlook-as-maplestory-surges-shares-slip-93CH-5014475), he said that after the strong October, "both in-game and unit sales have slowly normalized." He added that Nexon is committed to major ARC Raiders updates "at least two times a year."

## The catch: Q4 won't match launch

Nexon's outlook is cautious. The [release](https://pdf.irpocket.com/C3659/xoA3/DnNg/UwmY/FseY.pdf) says ARC Raiders' Q4 revenue should **fall year over year**, since it is up against the 2025 launch quarter, and stay **broadly flat** compared with Q3. The reasons it gives:

- Lower revenue per unit because of **promotional discounts**.
- **Less revenue recognized** from earlier deferrals, and more pushed into future periods.

Partly offsetting that, Nexon expects more unit sales plus income from the **Premium Reward Pass** (see our [Reward Pass explainer](/articles/arc-raiders-reward-pass-explained/)) and the **Collector Set DLC** package. The release also repeats that Nexon and Chernin Entertainment are developing **film and TV adaptations** of ARC Raiders and THE FINALS.

## Also today in ARC Raiders

- **Store Update 2.5.0** is live and adds two new colour variants for the Vanguard Set, **Orange** and **Black/White**. It needs no restart, [per Embark](https://arcraiders.com/news/store-update-2-5-0).
- **PvE is still the big question.** Söderlund's comment to [The Game Business](https://www.thegamebusiness.com/p/why-embark-had-to-pull-the-handbrake) that a permanent PvE mode wouldn't be technically hard is back in circulation. We broke down that interview and the PvE test data in [our earlier report](/articles/arc-raiders-ceo-could-just-add-pve-test-data-50-percent/). A permanent mode is still unconfirmed.

## What's next

- **Now through Q4 2026:** the Premium Reward Pass and Collector Set DLC carry monetization, with Nexon forecasting flat revenue quarter over quarter.
- **Next major update:** Söderlund's "at least two times a year" commitment points to the next big content drop following Frozen Trail. Embark's [year-two plan](/articles/arc-raiders-year-two-plan-expeditions-early-2027-pve-november/) already mentions Expeditions in early 2027.
- **Next financial update:** Nexon's Q4 and full-year 2026 results, which will show whether the post-Frozen Trail plateau held.
