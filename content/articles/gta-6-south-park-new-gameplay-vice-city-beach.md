Rockstar's newest look at Grand Theft Auto VI didn't land on YouTube or the Newswire. It turned up in a cartoon. South Park's **October 28** episode, "Guinea Pig on a Stick", cut to a short clip of genuine, previously unseen GTA 6 gameplay, and [IGN](https://in.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/275633/gta-6-dog-poop-gameplay-premieres-in-the-latest-episode-of-south-park) confirms the footage is real. The highlight: Jason picks up a bag of dog poop on a Vice City beach and hits someone with it.

## Real footage, credited to Rockstar

This isn't an animated parody. According to [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/South-Park-airs-GTA-6-gameplay-with-flying-dog-poop-on-packed-Vice-City-beach.1436981.0.html), the end credits read "Grand Theft Auto VI footage courtesy of Rockstar Games, Inc." [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/new-gta-6-gameplay-revealed-in-south-park-shows-a-disgusting-feature/) also says the credits confirm Rockstar supplied it. That means Rockstar handed the footage over itself. It wasn't a leak.

Nobody agrees on exactly how long it runs. [IGN](https://in.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/275633/gta-6-dog-poop-gameplay-premieres-in-the-latest-episode-of-south-park) calls it "just shy of 20 seconds" and [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/news/gta-6s-latest-gameplay-reveal-is-filthy-and-south-park-is-helping-rockstar-sell-it) says around 20. [Radio Times](https://www.radiotimes.com/technology/gaming/south-park-season-29-gta-footage-newsupdate/) times it at 17 seconds, while [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/grand-theft-auto-6-south-park-america-dog-poop-video-2000750987) counts about 25. Either way, it's brief.

## What the clip shows

In the episode, the boys get a "secret" GTA 6 presentation from the woman running the steakhouse that's buying up the town's beef, [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/the-latest-episode-of-south-park-features-exclusive-gta-6-footage-where-a-man-is-hit-in-the-head-with-dog-poo/) explains. Then the show switches to real game footage:

- **Jason and Lucia** on a packed Vice City beach, full of sunbathers, swimmers and umbrellas
- A **dog doing its business** on the sand while its owner bags it up
- Jason **throws something at the dog walker** to start a fight, then **grabs the poop bag and smacks him in the face** with it, and the bag bursts

[Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/South-Park-airs-GTA-6-gameplay-with-flying-dog-poop-on-packed-Vice-City-beach.1436981.0.html) points out the crowd density, the NPCs who react with dialogue and the footprints left in the sand. The clip also uses the normal third-person gameplay camera. In practice, it confirms that items NPCs drop out in the open world can be picked up and used as weapons outside missions. As [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/10/south-park-parodies-gta-vi-dog-poop-gameplay-1237207932/) quotes the episode's pitch: "That is what's coming in November."

## Part of a playful launch campaign

South Park has joked about GTA 6 all season, so the clip plays as a punchline more than a paid ad, [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/news/gta-6s-latest-gameplay-reveal-is-filthy-and-south-park-is-helping-rockstar-sell-it) argues. It's also the latest in a string of unusual marketing moves. Rockstar recently [turned part of Miami Beach into Vice Beach](/articles/gta-6-y-hotel-vice-beach-miami-beach-takeover/) and ran [GTA 6's first TV ad during NBA opening night](/articles/gta-6-tv-ad-nba-alternate-launch-trailer-cut/). Just a day after the episode aired, Edge's cover story brought [12 new screenshots and K9 police units](/articles/gta-6-edge-preview-12-screenshots-k9-police-criminal-profile-justice-score/).

Rockstar hasn't posted the clip on its own channels or released a statement about it, so for now the episode is the only official source.
