Three days after the biggest entertainment launch ever, GTA 6 has a new problem, and it isn't [PSN outages](/articles/gta-6-now-available-rockstar-highest-rated-game-psn-outages-fix-tips/). On November 22, [The National reported](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/22/jordan-proposes-gta-6-ban-over-child-safety-fears-as-game-breaks-sales-records/) that Jordan's Telecommunications Regulatory Commission (TRC) is working with other authorities to ban the game over child safety concerns. The same day, the UAE's National Drug Enforcement Authority [warned parents](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/11/22/gta-6-prompts-uae-warning-over-portrayal-of-drug-use/) about the game's drug content. One important detail up front: **Jordan's ban is only proposed. It has [not been formally implemented yet](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/22/jordan-proposes-gta-6-ban-over-child-safety-fears-as-game-breaks-sales-records/).**

## Jordan: a ban in the works, not in force

According to [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/22/jordan-proposes-gta-6-ban-over-child-safety-fears-as-game-breaks-sales-records/), which cites the Jordanian broadcaster Roya News, the TRC is acting after members of the public called for tighter controls on the game. Here's what TRC chairwoman Lara Al-Khatib said:

- **Online chat is the main worry.** Modern games are now interactive spaces where players can [talk to strangers through voice and text chat](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/22/jordan-proposes-gta-6-ban-over-child-safety-fears-as-game-breaks-sales-records/), she said, and that can expose kids to behavioural and security risks.
- **Age ratings aren't enough.** GTA 6 carries an adult-only rating, but Al-Khatib said ratings alone [don't stop children from getting games through digital platforms](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/22/jordan-proposes-gta-6-ban-over-child-safety-fears-as-game-breaks-sales-records/).
- **A blocking system is coming.** The regulator is building an [integrated monitoring and blocking system](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/22/jordan-proposes-gta-6-ban-over-child-safety-fears-as-game-breaks-sales-records/) to enforce the restrictions. It's also urging parents to supervise their kids' gaming.

Jordanian lawmakers are getting involved too. Per [Ammon News](https://www.ammonnews.net/article/1046957), MP Dima Tahboub sent the government 12 questions after the TRC's move. She asked who legally monitors and rates video games in Jordan, whether there is a binding national age-rating system, and what is being done about the risks of in-game voice and text chat. She also asked why a national game-rating system recommended by Jordan's National Center for Human Rights [hasn't been approved](https://www.ammonnews.net/article/1046957).

## UAE: no ban, but a warning about drugs

The UAE isn't banning anything. GTA 6 is on sale there with a [21+ rating, the highest the National Media Authority gives](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/11/22/gta-6-prompts-uae-warning-over-portrayal-of-drug-use/). But the NDEA posted a statement on social media saying the game's scenes of drug use and other illegal activity [could negatively affect children and teens](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/11/22/gta-6-prompts-uae-warning-over-portrayal-of-drug-use/), and that adult-themed content may "help normalise dangerous behaviours."

"Protecting our children from the dangers of drugs is a shared responsibility," the authority said, adding: ["Your children: befriend them, keep an eye on them, keep them occupied."](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/11/22/gta-6-prompts-uae-warning-over-portrayal-of-drug-use/) The National notes that in-game drug use, drug dealing and meth labs are [part of the open-world activities](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/11/22/gta-6-prompts-uae-warning-over-portrayal-of-drug-use/).

## Record sales vs. pushback

The pushback is landing in the middle of a historic launch. Take-Two says GTA 6 made [$3.79 billion in day-one retail sales](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/grand-theft-auto-vi-shatters-entertainment-box-office-records/) and sold 32.5 million units before release. (Sensor Tower's [earlier $2.36 billion estimate](/articles/gta-6-2-36-billion-day-one-sensor-tower-vs-gta-5-spider-man/) came in well below that.) The region isn't moving in one direction, either. [The National points out](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/22/jordan-proposes-gta-6-ban-over-child-safety-fears-as-game-breaks-sales-records/) that Saudi Arabia lifted its long-running ban on the series in 2025, so GTA 6 is sold there with a 21+ rating.

How does the US compare? Nobody is talking about a ban. GTA 6 is [rated M for Mature by the ESRB](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/grand-theft-auto-vi-shatters-entertainment-box-office-records/), and the pushback so far has come from [police departments telling players "officers are not NPCs"](/articles/gta-6-police-departments-warn-players-officers-not-npcs/) and from TV commentators, not regulators.

## What's next

- **Jordan:** The TRC still has to finish its monitoring and blocking system [before any ban can be enforced](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/11/22/jordan-proposes-gta-6-ban-over-child-safety-fears-as-game-breaks-sales-records/). No date has been announced.
- **Parliament:** The government will need to answer [Tahboub's 12 questions](https://www.ammonnews.net/article/1046957). Those answers could lead to broader rules for games in Jordan.
- **UAE:** For now, the [21+ rating and parental supervision](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/11/22/gta-6-prompts-uae-warning-over-portrayal-of-drug-use/) are the official response.

We'll update this story if Jordan formally confirms the ban.
