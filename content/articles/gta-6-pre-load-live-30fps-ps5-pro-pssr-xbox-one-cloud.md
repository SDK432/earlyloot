The download button is finally live. On **November 10, 2026**, Rockstar confirmed in a [Newswire post](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/89834o94713o21/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-now) that **Grand Theft Auto VI** pre-loading is open to everyone who pre-ordered digitally, and code-in-box buyers can start as soon as they activate their code. The same post finally answers the big performance question: GTA VI is aiming for **30fps** on consoles. Launch is still **November 19** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## 30fps target, with PS5 Pro extras

Rockstar says the game was built from the ground up for current-generation consoles and is targeting 30 frames per second, as [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/1030749/rockstar-gta-6-30fps-ps5-pro-pssr-ray-tracing) reports. No 60fps mode was announced.

PS5 Pro owners get the same frame-rate target, plus two visual upgrades the [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/) lists for the Pro:

- **PSSR** (PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution) for sharper image clarity
- **Ray-traced shadows**

On every PS5, Sony says the game uses the DualSense speaker, haptics and adaptive triggers (adjustable in accessibility settings), Tempest 3D Audio and the console's fast SSD for quicker loading. Sony's line is that this makes GTA VI "play best on PlayStation 5 consoles."

## Xbox One can play via the cloud

The surprise came from [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/): GTA VI will be playable on day one through **Xbox Cloud Gaming** on **Xbox One** and Xbox Series X|S, starting at **1am ET on November 19**. Streamed sessions use **Xbox Series S graphics settings**.

- **Series X and Series S:** play natively; pre-install is available now.
- **Xbox One:** streaming only, not a native version.
- **No Game Pass?** Starting **November 11**, players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime to stream supported games without a subscription. You still have to buy GTA VI separately.

Microsoft also says more Rockstar games, including GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2, are coming to Xbox Cloud Gaming "in the coming months."

## Record pre-orders and a rolling PS5 launch

Both platform holders say demand is the biggest they've seen. Sony calls GTA VI [the most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store history](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/pre-load-grand-theft-auto-vi-on-ps5-starting-today/), and Microsoft calls it [the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/). That fits what Take-Two told investors this week, when it said [development is complete and pre-orders are far above expectations](/articles/gta-6-take-two-earnings-development-complete-pre-orders-far-above/).

PlayStation is also moving to a **rolling midnight launch by country**. It starts at **12:00AM NZDT** in New Zealand and moves around the world as each country reaches midnight on November 19.

## A new 29-second "Pre-Load Now" trailer

Rockstar released a short **Pre-Load Now** trailer set to Tove Lo's "Blood in Your Eyes" from *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*. [IGN](https://in.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/276612/gta-6-preload-now-trailer-includes-nearly-30-seconds-of-new-footage-swat-team-nightclub-dancing-and) counts 29 seconds with never-before-seen footage, including:
