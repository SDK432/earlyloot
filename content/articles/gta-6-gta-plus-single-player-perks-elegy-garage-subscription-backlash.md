**True or false: GTA 6 locks content behind a subscription?** Mostly true. GTA 6 has no online mode yet, but Rockstar's [GTA+ page](https://www.rockstargames.com/gta-plus) now has a GTA VI section. It lists a members-only car, a garage and in-game discounts, all inside the single-player story. Rockstar calls them ["optional benefits in GTAVI,"](https://www.gta6everything.com/news/gta-plus-gta-6-benefits-venturas-blvd-elegy-rockstar-page) according to GTA 6 Everything, which read the page on November 19. In the US, GTA+ costs [$7.99 a month](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-plus-membership-benefits-exclusive-car-garage/).

## What $7.99 a month gets you in Leonida

Here's what Rockstar's page lists, as quoted by [GTA 6 Everything](https://www.gta6everything.com/news/gta-plus-gta-6-benefits-venturas-blvd-elegy-rockstar-page) and confirmed by [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-plus-membership-benefits-exclusive-car-garage/):

- **The '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy**, a widebody sports car, plus **a garage in the Crosstown District**. Both are "Available to claim from Chapter 3." Polygon says the single-car garage has a weapon locker and lets you fence stolen items.
- **Transit Service Bonuses:** Scooter Bros Premium vehicle care, premium RydeMe rideshare membership, free scooter and bike rentals, and "Leonida metro benefits." [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-plus-membership-benefits-exclusive-car-garage/) adds free Seashark rentals and free public transit rides.
- **Store Discounts:** "a 10% discount from select businesses across Leonida," including weapons and clothes shops, hairdressers, tattooists and vehicle mod shops.
- **Increased Sell Value:** "a 10% increase on owned vehicle sales."

## The fine print

According to [GTA 6 Everything](https://www.gta6everything.com/news/gta-plus-gta-6-benefits-venturas-blvd-elegy-rockstar-page), the page says you need **an active membership and an internet connection** to access and claim the perks. Stop paying and they're gone: [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-plus-membership-benefits-exclusive-car-garage/) reports that canceling removes the car, the garage and everything else.

You can switch them off. Polygon says to open the pause menu, go to the **Account** tab and toggle **GTA Plus Boosts** off. Watch out if you claimed the free month that came with digital pre-orders: [Rockstar Report](https://rockstarreport.com/news/gta-6-gta-plus-story-mode-benefits) says it renews at the regular price unless you cancel. (We covered the pre-order bonus in our [GTA 6 price and editions guide](/articles/gta-6-price-editions-platforms/).)

## Why players are mad

According to [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-plus-membership-benefits-exclusive-car-garage/), a Reddit post about the perks drew thousands of upvotes. Top comments argue the car and garage belong in the base game. Others point out that even buyers of the pricier editions still need GTA+ to get them. A third group worries this is a preview of how much Rockstar will lock behind the subscription once GTA Online arrives.

The timing makes it worse. Rockstar says GTA VI made [$3.79 billion in retail sales on day one](https://www.theguardian.com/games/2026/nov/21/a-new-record-for-entertainment-grand-theft-auto-vi-sales-hit-38bn-on-first-day-of-release-developer-says) and sold 32.5 million units before release, The Guardian reports. That's well past the [Sensor Tower estimate we covered](/articles/gta-6-2-36-billion-day-one-sensor-tower-vs-gta-5-spider-man/). [PPRGaming](https://pprgaming.com/news/gta-6-gta-plus-7-99-single-player-perks-elegy-garage-discounts/) put it bluntly: "They made their money…and they found a way to keep making it." PPRGaming also notes Rockstar hasn't commented on the reaction.

## The verdict

**Is it a paywall?** Partly. None of the perks are needed to finish the story, and you can turn them off. But a car and a garage you can only use while you keep paying, in a game that already costs full price, is subscription content in a single-player game. Nothing changes that.

## What's next

- **Chapter 3** is where the Elegy and the Crosstown garage unlock, per [Rockstar's page](https://www.gta6everything.com/news/gta-plus-gta-6-benefits-venturas-blvd-elegy-rockstar-page).
- **GTA Online for GTA 6** hasn't been dated. Players will be watching how GTA+ shapes it.
- **Rockstar's response:** none so far. We'll update this post if the publisher addresses the backlash.
