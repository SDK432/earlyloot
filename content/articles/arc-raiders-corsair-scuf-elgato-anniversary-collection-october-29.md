ARC Raiders is getting a birthday present for your desk. On Tuesday, October 27, [CORSAIR announced on X](https://x.com/CORSAIR/status/2115186879330918588) that **CORSAIR, SCUF and Elgato "go Topside together" on October 29** with "a collection built to celebrate ARC Raiders' 1-year anniversary." The official [ARC Raiders account reposted it](https://x.com/ARCRaidersGame/status/2115385494292881808) on Wednesday morning, so this is an approved collab, not a fan project. The drop lands exactly one day before the game turns one on October 30.

## What the teaser shows

CORSAIR's announcement comes with a 15-second teaser video. It doesn't list products, but here is what we could see in it:

- A **headset** with the game's multicolor stripe on the earcup
- A **SCUF controller** with Xbox-style face buttons and the same stripe accents
- A lightweight, honeycomb-style **gaming mouse**
- A **keyboard** with green, yellow and red keycaps and a striped "ARC" spacebar
- A full desk setup with a large **desk mat**, plus a close-up of a camera lens, which fits Elgato's streaming lineup

None of this is new to anyone who visited Gamescom. In August, CORSAIR [showed an ARC Raiders collab at its booth](https://x.com/CORSAIR/status/2093685201255698823), and [players on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/ArcRaiders/comments/1w2re4m/special_arc_raiders_peripherals_at_gamescom_2026/) spotted a themed keyboard, headset, mouse and mouse pad. What's new is the date and the anniversary branding.

## What we don't know yet

CORSAIR hasn't published **prices, a full product list, regions or a launch time**. It also hasn't said whether the collection includes in-game items such as a code, stencil or cosmetic. Some fans replying to the post asked about a PlayStation controller, and the teaser only shows the Xbox-style pad, so don't assume a PS5 version until CORSAIR confirms one. We'll update this story when the store page goes live.

## A busy anniversary week

The hardware arrives in the middle of a packed stretch for Embark:

- **Soul's Offering** is live. [Update 2.3.0](https://arcraiders.com/news/live-update-2-3-0) launched the new Raider Project on October 27, along with the Noxious Set and Heavy Shield and Flame Spray buffs. Our [full 2.3 breakdown](/articles/arc-raiders-update-2-3-souls-offering-live-heavy-shield-flame-spray/) has the details.
- **Fixes slow down.** Embark says weekly fix patches end with 2.3.0. From now on, expect fixes every three weeks and store updates every week.
- **Best of the Best 2026** voting closes today, October 28. The top 10 clips win 1,150 Raider Tokens each ([our guide](/articles/arc-raiders-best-of-the-best-2026-community-vote/)).
- **No in-game anniversary event announced.** Embark tied October's free weekend to the anniversary, but its [news page](https://arcraiders.com/news) lists nothing for October 30 itself.

## What's next

- **October 29:** CORSAIR, SCUF and Elgato ARC Raiders collection launches
- **October 30:** ARC Raiders' first anniversary (the game launched October 30, 2025)
- **November:** Embark's follow-up on the PvE toggle test, which [PC Gamer reports](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/third-person-shooter/over-30-million-rounds-of-pve-were-played-over-the-last-week-in-arc-raiders-but-embark-still-needs-a-bit-of-time-to-understand-what-the-data-is-telling-us/) logged more than 30 million rounds
- **November 16:** Soul's Offering ends

If Embark has a surprise planned for October 30, we'll cover it here.
