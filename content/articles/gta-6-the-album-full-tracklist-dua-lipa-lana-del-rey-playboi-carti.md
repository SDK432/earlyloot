Rockstar didn't get to reveal this one itself. On **Saturday, November 14, 2026**, a video of a record store employee handling an early vinyl copy of **Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album** went around social media, and the back sleeve showed all **34 songs**. Within hours, sources close to the project confirmed the list to both [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/gta-6-album-tracklist-grand-theft-auto-vi-soundtrack-1236382244/) and [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/lana-del-rey-dua-lipa-zara-larsson-gta-vi-album-track-list-1235658416/), and [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-soundtrack-leak-confirmed-accurate-with-new-songs-from-lana-del-rey-playboi-carti-and-keith-richards/) followed today.

To be clear: Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records have **not** posted the full tracklist themselves. Everything below is the leaked list as confirmed by those outlets' sources, not an official announcement.

## The big new names

Several singles were already out, so the surprise is the stuff nobody had announced. According to the sources who spoke to Billboard and Rolling Stone, the album includes:

- **Dua Lipa** – "Go Easy" (track 7)
- **Playboi Carti** – "Ahh Yea"
- **Lana Del Rey** – "Crazy for You". [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/lana-del-rey-dua-lipa-zara-larsson-gta-vi-album-track-list-1235658416/) points out that the title matches a song fans have known as an unreleased Del Rey track for more than a decade.
- **Jennie** – "Power Trip"
- **Burna Boy**, **Gunna**, **Young Thug**, **Camila Cabello** and **Zara Larsson**
- **Justice feat. Michael McDonald** – "Starting From Zero", which opens the record

Those join the cuts that were already streaming. That list includes Travis Scott's "RHYNO", Cardi B's "Don't Chart", Tove Lo's "Blood in Your Eyes", Morgan Wallen's "Last Thing You Need", Yung Lean's "That's It" with Future and Metro Boomin, and Keith Richards' "Bright Lights, Big City". Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard is on there too. [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Full-GTA-6-album-tracklist-leaks-with-Dua-Lipa-Playboi-Carti-and-Lana-Del-Rey-among-34-songs.1450848.0.html) notes that the leaked running order matches the track numbers Rockstar's pre-save listing had already given to the first singles. That's a good sign the sleeve is real.

## Album vs. radio vs. score

The album is separate from the game's in-world radio. [Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/gta-6-album-tracklist-grand-theft-auto-vi-soundtrack-1236382244/) recaps that the stations are hosted by artists such as Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey, Morgan Wallen, Trina and Trick Daddy, Burna Boy and Robyn. Our [GTA 6 radio stations breakdown](/articles/gta-6-radio-stations-revealed/) has the details. Justice also composed the game's dynamic score, which gets its own release later (see below).

Billboard adds that each retailer, including Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon, Target and Urban Outfitters, will sell its own exclusive vinyl pressing with a unique design and poster.

## When can you listen?

The release date depends on who you ask. Billboard still lists **November 19**, the same day the game launches. [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-news/lana-del-rey-dua-lipa-zara-larsson-gta-vi-album-track-list-1235658416/), however, says the album arrives on **Friday, November 20**, the day after GTA VI. Until Rockstar or Atlantic confirms it, treat November 20 as the reported date.
