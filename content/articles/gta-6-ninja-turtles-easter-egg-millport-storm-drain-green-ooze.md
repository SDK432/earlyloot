Did you spot this one? Somewhere in the hills of Leonida, four baby turtles are hanging out next to a pile of pizza boxes, a rat and a crack in a wall leaking **glowing green ooze**. If that sounds like the opening scene of a 1980s cartoon, that's the point. On November 24, [Complex](https://www.complex.com/pop-culture/a/markelibert/gta-6-teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-easter-egg) reported that GTA 6 players had found an apparent Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles easter egg at the end of a storm drain, less than a week after the game's November 19 launch.

## How it went viral

The find blew up before anyone knew where it was. According to [Complex](https://www.complex.com/pop-culture/a/markelibert/gta-6-teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-easter-egg), film account DiscussingFilm posted it on X on November 24 without a location, and the post pulled in **more than 30,000 likes and 1,700 reposts within hours**. Guide writers then tracked it down, and by November 25 both [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-tmnt-easter-egg-location/) and [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles/) had published step-by-step locations.

One caveat: Rockstar hasn't confirmed it, as [Complex notes](https://www.complex.com/pop-culture/a/markelibert/gta-6-teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles-easter-egg), and the game never names the franchise. But four turtles, a rat, pizza and mutagen-green slime in a sewer? That's about as subtle as a nunchuck to the face.

## Where to find the Ninja Turtles in GTA 6

The outlets describe the same patch of East Lummox County in slightly different words:

- **Dexerto** puts it at a [water treatment facility north of Millport](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles/).
- **VICE** places it in the East Lummox County sewers, [just south of Lake Tallaloko and below the M7 Weed Grow illegal operation](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-tmnt-easter-egg-location/).

Put together: set a waypoint on Millport, head north toward Lake Tallaloko and look for a sewer opening with green water flowing out.

## How to see the turtles, step by step

1. **Go at night.** VICE recommends arriving [after 10 PM in-game](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-tmnt-easter-egg-location/), and Dexerto says it [has to be after dark](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles/) for the scene to appear.
2. **Hop the guardrail** along the road and walk down the embankment to the sewer opening, per [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-tmnt-easter-egg-location/).
3. **Check the east side of the opening.** That's where VICE found the four turtles and the rat next to two empty pizza boxes.
4. **Keep your distance.** Get too close and "Splinter" bolts. Dexerto suggests using [binoculars](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles/) to get a good look first.

## Is there a reward?

Not a big one. Dexerto says there's [no direct reward](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/easter-eggs/teenage-mutant-ninja-turtles/), but scanning the crack in the wall ticks off "Intriguing Substance" in the Curiosities collection, and you can grab the Ratster collectible card nearby. VICE says to open the [Waink Me app on your phone and scan the ooze](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-tmnt-easter-egg-location/) just above where the turtles sit. So if you're chasing 100%, this stop counts.
