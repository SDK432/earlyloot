Season 1's first big event has started. **The Wager Match event is now live** in Modern Warfare 4, Warzone and DMZ, [Infinity Ward announced](https://x.com/InfinityWard/status/2120681101517820410) late on **November 11, 2026**. You earn Trophies in any mode and spend them on rewards, including Apex attachments, Blueprints and camos. To get everyone started, the studio is **giving every player 30 bonus Trophies**. If you were already in the game when it went live, restart to claim them.

## How the Wager Match event works

Trophies are the currency for the whole event. According to [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-cod-modern-warfare-4-mw4-wager-match-event-rewards-how-to-get-trophies/), they drop from eliminations in every mode and can also be found in Supply Crates in Warzone. You can then risk them:

- **Multiplayer:** stake Trophies in designated wager modes. The headline mode is **Gauntlet 1v1**, a Gunfight variant. The [official Season 1 blog](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/10/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-warzone-season-1-announcement) says the winner stays on to face the next opponent, and you have to win several duels in a row to claim the reward you wagered.
- **DMZ and Warzone:** complete Wager Challenges for bigger payouts. Game Rant says some DMZ challenges pay up to 70 Trophies, and Warzone Resurgence challenges pay 60 or more.

The event runs until **December 2**, according to both Game Rant and [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/call-of-duty/guides/wager-match-event-mw4-warzone). Wager Match is free, so you don't need the Battle Pass.

## The rewards worth chasing

Activision's blog lists three new weapon attachments tied to the event:

- **Hyeon Auto (Apex):** converts the Hyeon Burst assault rifle to fully automatic fire. You trade precision for a much higher sustained fire rate.
- **Kastov 545 (Apex):** turns the Kastov 762 into a 5.45x39mm rifle with "smooth handling and rapid follow-up shots."
- **M16 Burst:** swaps the M4's full-auto fire for controlled bursts.

Game Rant's breakdown of the reward track adds:

- **Kastov 545:** unlocks once you've claimed 10 Wager Match rewards.
- **Vitriol Operator skin:** unlocks at 15 rewards.
- **Venom Pile camo:** costs 1,000 Trophies.
- **Chainsaw Grip stock (FINN LMG) and TT Iron Guard Kit (.50 GS):** 100 Trophies each.
- **Six more rewards** are due to be revealed on **November 18**.

Game Rant also says the event's Apex attachments will move to the Armory after the event ends, so missing them now doesn't lock you out for good. The cosmetics are a different story.

## Launch-day hiccups

The start wasn't entirely smooth. On November 11, [Call of Duty Updates](https://x.com/CODUpdates/status/2120646400929309038) **temporarily disabled the Wager option in Warzone** while the team investigated an issue. Players could still earn Trophies and challenge progress in the meantime. Game Rant also flagged a bug that stopped Trophies dropping from eliminations, and suggested Warzone Supply Crates as a workaround. Separately, the team [is investigating](https://x.com/CODUpdates/status/2120620885866684555) intermittent delays when firing semi-auto weapons in MW4 and Warzone. When we checked, neither issue had been officially marked as fixed.
