You can play the **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4** campaign **a full week early**. Activision has [confirmed Campaign Early Access](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/06/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-campaign-early-access) starting **Friday, October 16, 2026**, ahead of the worldwide launch on **Friday, October 23, 2026**.

## When does Campaign Early Access start?

**Friday, October 16, 2026.** [Activision Support](https://support.activision.com/modern-warfare-4/articles/modern-warfare-4-editions) lists the start as October 16 (PT). Activision also notes that actual play time is subject to possible outages and time zone differences.

## Who gets early access?

Anyone who **digitally pre-orders or pre-purchases** Modern Warfare 4. According to Activision Support, that covers **any digital edition on any platform**, including the Standard Digital Edition and the Vault Edition.

Physical editions aren't mentioned in the early access offer, so if early access matters to you, pre-order digitally.

## Which platforms get early access?

Campaign Early Access covers:

- PlayStation 5
- Xbox Series X|S and Xbox on PC
- PC via Battle.net and Steam
- Nintendo Switch 2 (pre-orders opened August 26, per Activision Support)

There's no version for **PlayStation 4 or Xbox One**. Activision has [confirmed](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/05/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-announcement) the game won't be available on last-gen consoles.

## What does the campaign cover?

From Activision's official description: you play as **Private Park**, a young South Korean soldier facing live combat for the first time after North Korea launches a full-scale invasion. Meanwhile, **Captain Price**, now an "operator-turned-outlaw", is on a hunt for revenge outside the system he once served.

The campaign moves from trench warfare in Korea to close-quarters combat in New York, chases through Paris and SAS night raids in Mumbai.
