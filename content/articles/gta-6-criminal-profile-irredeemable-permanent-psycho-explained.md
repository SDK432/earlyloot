Think GTA 6 has a halo-or-horns morality meter like Red Dead Redemption 2's Honor? Not quite. The **Criminal Profile** doesn't judge whether Jason and Lucia are "good." It tracks **how violent** your crimes are. And if you push it too far, [PC Gamer reports](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-explained/) you can land in a tier you never get out of: **Irredeemable**.

## Myth: it's a good vs. evil meter

Busted. When Rockstar first detailed the system in August, [GameSpot noted](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-a-red-dead-2-honor-style-system-tracking-how-violent-you-are/) that it isn't designed to turn the duo into do-gooders. It measures how violently and maliciously you go about your crimes. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told IGN, as quoted by GameSpot: "This is a story about a couple on the run." Commit a crime, get away before the cops arrive and don't start shooting, and "you're generally going to be fine."

Another detail people miss: Jason and Lucia **each have their own profile**, [per PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-explained/). How violent and lethal you are, and how you treat people *and* animals, all feed your rating.

## The five tiers (and what they give you)

[PC Gamer's breakdown](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-explained/) shows each tier as a horned-face icon on screen:

- **Professional (yellow):** use only the violence you need, incapacitate instead of killing and stay undetected. *Bonus:* non-lethal incapacitations and thoughtful acts toward citizens fill your focus (bullet-time) meter.
- **Hardened (orange):** you kill enemies, kill citizens only in self-defense and don't care about detection. *Bonus:* the same as Professional.
- **Violent (purple):** you skip non-lethal options and kill enemies, police and citizens indiscriminately. *Bonus:* headshots fill focus.
- **Psycho (light gray):** you kill lots of cops, helpless citizens and domestic or defenseless animals (hunting doesn't count). *Bonus:* headshots fill focus, but **you can't surrender to the cops**.
- **Irredeemable (dark gray):** see below.

## Irredeemable is permanent

You can bounce between the first four tiers all game long. But stay Psycho for too long and you're marked **Irredeemable**, and [PC Gamer says](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-explained/) that label is permanent. Rockstar says only that being Irredeemable "will have a lasting impact on Jason and Lucia." What that impact actually is hasn't been documented yet, so treat any specific claims you see online with caution.

Nelson, speaking to Eurogamer as [reported by PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/aspects-of-gta-6-are-inspired-by-red-dead-redemption-2s-honor-system-because-its-player-agency-was-a-hard-thing-to-ignore-and-walk-away-from/), says you can "smash your Criminal Profile until it breaks." Irredeemable, he explains, basically says "I reject your criminal profile system!" He also admits RDR2's player choice "was a hard thing to ignore and walk away from."

## Did you know? You can kill 321 enemies and still be "Professional"

PC Gamer's Christopher Livingston [finished the main story as Professional](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-explained/) even though his stats included **321 enemies killed** and **58 cops killed**. His secret: aiming at knees and elbows with focus, which added up to **461 enemies incapacitated**. His 98 animal kills? He blames mostly the birds, which he says just don't move out of the way of vehicles. "You are going to run over a lot of birds," he warns.
