Did you spot this one? GTA 6 has only been out since November 19, and players have already cracked what might be its wildest secret: a hidden portal that throws you straight back into the 2002 PS2 classic, GTA: Vice City. As [VGC reported on November 23](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/one-of-gta-6s-most-elaborate-easter-eggs-has-been-solved/), YouTuber **Memeio** (@MemeioYT) solved the chain about four days after launch, and the payoff is Tommy Vercetti's own shirt and motorbike.

## What the Vice City easter egg actually is

It starts with a familiar musical chime from Vice City and turns into a cross-map scavenger hunt along Leonida's power grid. Memeio summed up the grind bluntly to VGC: "I literally spent three days following powerlines in game to find the substations." The finale is a portal into a stylized take on the original game, complete with a [cameo from Danny Dyer reprising Kent Paul](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/one-of-gta-6s-most-elaborate-easter-eggs-has-been-solved/). [Pure Xbox](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/11/gta-6s-most-spectacular-easter-egg-so-far-has-just-been-discovered) says the portal lands you inside a nightclub from the 1986-set PS2 game.

## How to open the portal, step by step

Here's the route as laid out in [Push Square's walkthrough](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/gta-6-vice-city-easter-egg-walkthrough). Visit the substations **in order** and don't fast travel. Some are staffed, so expect trespassing heat.

1. **Badger R&D radio tower, Key Lento** (near Jason's home): climb to the top room, flip the electrical box lever to ON, then press the button on the equipment opposite to hear the Vice City sound. Pure Xbox notes a TV there then shows the Malibu logo.
2. Clock the posters for **LPL** (the power company) and JoySystem's "Lose Track of Time." That's a nod to Vice City's "Life Is Passing Me By" cheat, which sped up the clock.
3. Hit the 12 LPL substations in this order: Downtown Vice City, Balsero, Key Lento, Penmarch Isle (Washington Beach), Crosstown, Port Gellhorn, Hamlet, Gloriana Key, Hamlet again, the bottom of Port Gellhorn, Hamlet a third time and North Mariana County. Each one shows letters and a button prompt.
4. The letters spell **"LIFE IS PASSING ME BY."** Finish and you get a text: "U-235 REACTOR 19-06."
5. Go to the **T.H. (Turtle Head) Power Station** between 7pm and 6am in-game. It's heavily guarded. Reactor 2 holds the portal. Pull the lever next to it and you're in pixelated Vice City.

## The rewards: Tommy's shirt and the American Glory

Push Square says you walk away with **Tommy Vercetti's blue Hawaiian shirt** and the **"American Glory" motorcycle**. Register the bike at a Pay 'n' Spray or park it in a garage so you don't lose it. Both Jason and Lucia can claim the rewards.

## What players are reporting

- Most footage shows Jason, but Pure Xbox points to X user @ajrodz saying "You have to go solo, Lucia can do it."
- [Many players say the portal simply isn't spawning](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/11/gta-6s-most-spectacular-easter-egg-so-far-has-just-been-discovered) for them, and nobody knows why yet.
- Some report a "flashback" pop-up afterwards, which has fans guessing there could be **four more flashback sequences** to find. That's **player speculation only**. Rockstar hasn't confirmed anything.
