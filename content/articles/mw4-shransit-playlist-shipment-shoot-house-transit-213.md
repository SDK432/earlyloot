**SHRANSIT is live in Modern Warfare 4 Season 1.** On November 6, [Infinity Ward](https://x.com/InfinityWard/status/2118793596921106910) posted that fresh playlists are up in #MW4, including **SHRANSIT** — a close-quarters rotation of **Shipment**, **Shoot House**, and **Transit 213**. A follow-up confirms **Hardcore SHRANSIT** is also available via the Hardcore playlist filter.

Season 1 launched November 6. For the full launch checklist — Hajin, Zodiac, and patch notes — see our [Warzone Season 1 Is Live](/articles/warzone-season-1-live-hajin-zodiac-patch-notes-november-6/) digest. This post is the weekend CQB playlist drop.

## What SHRANSIT includes

[Infinity Ward](https://x.com/InfinityWard/status/2118793596921106910) framed the weekend drop simply: Season 1 is here, and the new featured playlists include SHRANSIT with three classic-feeling small maps:

- **Shipment** — the ultra-tight container yard that returned earlier this year (our [Shipment returns](/articles/mw4-shipment-returns-today-october-27-what-to-expect/) guide covers what to expect).
- **Shoot House** — the three-lane CQB staple for fast Spawns and mid-range fights.
- **Transit 213** — the third map in the rotation, rounding out the SHRANSIT loop.

If you want the sweaty filter, [Infinity Ward](https://x.com/InfinityWard/status/2118795877825532331) says **Hardcore SHRANSIT** is live too — switch your Playlist Filter to Hardcore and jump in.

## Other featured playlists this weekend

[GameRant](https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-mw4-playlist-update-today-shransit/) confirmed the SHRANSIT refresh on November 7 and listed additional featured playlists that rolled out alongside it:

- **Kill Block 24/7**
- **Season 1 Moshpit**
- **Kill Block 6v6 Gunfight**
- **Possession**

GameRant also notes that some fans are complaining that a dedicated **Shipment 24/7** playlist appears removed from featured playlists. That is outlet reporting on community reaction, not an official Infinity Ward statement — treat it as context while you queue SHRANSIT or Kill Block instead.

## Also this weekend: DMZ Free Trial

Separately, [Call of Duty](https://x.com/CallofDuty/status/2118823701433188466) announced a **DMZ Free Trial** running through November 11. It is not the SHRANSIT lead, but it is another Season 1 weekend hook if you want extraction alongside the CQB grind.

SHRANSIT slots into the day-one Season 1 playlist refresh after launch.
