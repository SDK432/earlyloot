**Grand Theft Auto VI** now has age ratings in Japan and South Korea, and both are as high as they go. As of **October 17, 2026**, the [Japanese PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/ja-jp/product/JP0230-PPSA29660_00-GTAVISTANDARD001) lists the game as **CERO Z**, the "18 and over only" tier. One day earlier, South Korea's private ratings board gave it an **adults-only (19+)** rating, [Inven reports](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27299/gta-6-rated-adults-only-in-south-korea-following-private-board-review). The last two big ratings were settled with about a month to go before the **November 19** launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## Japan: CERO Z, with crime and violence icons

[Game*Spark](https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2026/10/17/173739.html) noticed that the Japanese PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store pages switched from "rating pending" to **Z**. [Denfaminicogamer](https://news.denfaminicogamer.jp/news/261017h) confirmed the same rating on both storefronts. The rating carries two content icons: **crime** and **violence**. GTA V got the same pair in Japan.

The rating was still listed as pending in the [launch trailer](/articles/gta-6-launch-trailer-flower-in-a-hurricane/) on October 15, per Game*Spark. Some quick context on what Z means:

- CERO Z is the only Japanese tier that's a hard sales restriction. [PlayStation Japan](https://www.playstation.com/ja-jp/legal/ratings/) defines it as "18 and over only."
- The Japanese store page says CERO Z games **can't be sold to anyone under 18**. They also have to be paid for by **credit card**, whatever your PSN wallet balance is.

## South Korea: 19+ from the GCRB

Inven says the **Game Content Rating Board (GCRB)** made its decision on October 16. The private body took over adult PC and console ratings from the government's GRAC in November 2025. [Electronic Times](https://www.etnews.com/20261016000308) notes that GTA 6 is the most closely watched game the GCRB has rated so far. The rating clears the game for its Korean release on November 19.

[HeistAtlas reports](https://heistatlas.com/en/updates/2026-10-18-gta6-rated-adults-japan-korea/) that the Korean PlayStation Store lists five descriptors: crime, drugs, language, sexual content and violence. The rating wasn't a sure thing, Inven points out. GTA V passed in 2013, but Korea refused to rate *Danganronpa V3* in 2017.

## Will Japan get a different version?

This is the big open question. CERO also has icons for sexual content and drugs, and GTA 6 carries neither. Meanwhile, the PlayStation Store rating notes in Australia and New Zealand specifically mention "Sex Scenes," [Game*Spark notes](https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2026/10/17/173739.html). Game*Spark points out that the Japanese release of **GTA V** had altered sex scenes and some gore removed. As of October 18, **Rockstar hasn't said** whether the Japanese GTA 6 will differ from other versions. Until it does, treat any talk of cuts as speculation.

Elsewhere, Electronic Times lists the ratings already known: **ESRB M** in the US, **PEGI 18** in Europe and **R18+** in Australia.

## What's next

- **November 12:** pre-loads open, seven days before launch ([how pre-load works](/articles/gta-6-preload-date/)).
- **November 19:** GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- Rockstar is still polishing the build, so watch for any word on regional differences as it [heads toward gold](/articles/gta-6-not-gone-gold-final-push/).

If you're buying for someone else, check the account's age settings now. A blocked purchase on launch night is no fun.
