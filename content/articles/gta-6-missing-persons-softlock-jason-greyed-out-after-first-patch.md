Rockstar's first real GTA 6 patch landed on **November 23**, just four days after launch, and it did kill some nasty bugs. But for a chunk of players stuck halfway through the story, nothing changed. [Game Rant reports](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-patch-jason-grayed-out-cant-switch-bug/) that people are still trapped in **Chapter 3** after the optional **Missing Persons** case: Jason is greyed out on the character wheel, Lucia has nothing to do in the story and Jason won't even pick up the phone.

## What patch 01.002.002 actually fixed

Rockstar's [official Launch Updates page](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/sN5pEdFYZdlvijKXd6xpJ/grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-updates-ps5-xbox-series-x-or-s) lists seven fixes for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, as also reproduced by [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114835/first-gta-6-update-fixes-weather-game-crashes-wanted-level-glitches-and-more/index.html):

- Several crashes
- Weather that refused to change for long stretches
- Autosaves that didn't save correctly
- Phone calls that failed to trigger, "including those that progressed the story or unlocked mission content"
- Wanted Levels you couldn't shake
- An unusable gym deadlift bar
- Partners not handing over bags to sell to fences

That phone-call fix is why stuck players rushed to download it. The notes never mention Missing Persons or character switching, though.

## The softlock that survived

According to [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-soft-lock-bug-chapter-3-game-breaking/), the problem went viral after X user @SirGrotti posted a clip showing the switch back to Jason permanently greyed out. That's a dead end, because the next main story beat needs Jason. Polygon adds that the Chapter 3 softlock is the most-viewed entry on the community tracker [GTA6Bugs](https://gta6bugs.com/bugs/gta-6-chapter-3-jason-greyed-out-softlock-after-missing-persons), and that the patch "didn't seem to fix" it.

The frustration goes beyond the bug itself. Players claim Rockstar Support simply closes their tickets about it, [per Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-patch-jason-grayed-out-cant-switch-bug/). That's a player report, not something Rockstar has confirmed, and Rockstar hasn't commented publicly on the softlock. It's another sour note in a launch week where the game's [Metacritic user score](/articles/gta-6-metacritic-review-bomb-99-metascore-user-score-ocarina-of-time/) already sits far below its 99 from critics.

## What you can do right now

There's no official fix yet. These are the routes outlets have reported:

- **Load an older manual save.** The workaround most often reported to work, laid out by [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-soft-lock-bug-chapter-3-game-breaking/): load a manual save from before you drove to the Port Gellhorn warehouse for the final Missing Persons meeting. Play Dealer's Choice as Jason until you can switch to Lucia freely, save to a new slot, then finish Missing Persons.
- **Try calling Dunk from Lucia's phone.** Both outlets mention it, but results are mixed and some players say it did nothing.
- **Don't overwrite your stuck save.** Save it to a spare slot so you don't lose it in case a future patch repairs it.
- **Haven't reached Chapter 3 yet?** Make manual saves before big missions. Game Rant points out that GTA 6 doesn't let you replay missions.

While you wait, the open world still works. Side content, random encounters and hidden secrets like the [Vice City portal easter egg](/articles/gta-6-vice-city-portal-easter-egg-tommy-vercetti-shirt-bike/) are still there, except anything that needs Jason.

## What's next

Rockstar hasn't announced a date for the next GTA 6 update. Its Launch Updates page still lists only the November 23 patch. Polygon calls this one of the most reported game-breaking issues, so a targeted fix is the obvious next step. We'll update this story when Rockstar confirms one.
