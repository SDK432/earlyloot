One day. That's all it took. According to [Sensor Tower estimates reported by the BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9zr5nyv5dj2o), GTA 6 moved roughly **24.58 million copies** and pulled in about **$2.36 billion** in gross sales once pre-orders are counted. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/gta-6-day-one-sales-124649589.html), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/grand-theft-auto-gta-vice-city-playstation-15c69f76548ab5659bc3fe44a69756d8) and [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/gta-6-sales-launch-day-surge-videogame-1237229514/) all picked up the numbers. Big caveat up front: these are **estimates**. Rockstar and Take-Two haven't released a single official figure. [Per the BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9zr5nyv5dj2o), the only thing they've said is that demand has been "unprecedented."

On Wednesday we covered the [17.1 million early estimate](/articles/gta-6-out-now-99-metacritic-17-million-sold-no-performance-mode/), and before launch the [$1 billion pre-order mark](/articles/gta-6-1-billion-pre-orders-sensor-tower-xbox-take-two-10-year-deal/). The new number blows past both.

## The comparisons that make it look absurd

- **vs. GTA V:** Rockstar's last mainline game needed [three days to hit $1 billion](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/gta-6-day-one-sales-124649589.html). GTA 6 is estimated at more than double that in roughly a day.
- **vs. Hollywood:** *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, 2026's highest-grossing movie, earned [$2.5 billion over its entire theatrical run](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9zr5nyv5dj2o). GTA 6 got within about $140 million of that in one day.
- **vs. its own budget:** the game reportedly cost [more than $1 billion to develop](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9zr5nyv5dj2o). If the estimate holds, it paid that back more than twice over by day one.

## What Wall Street was expecting

[Yahoo Finance reports](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/gta-6-day-one-sales-124649589.html) that Morgan Stanley, using earlier Sensor Tower data ($1.8 billion and about 19 million copies through Thursday morning), said its 30-million-copies-this-quarter forecast was "within reach on day 1." The updated $2.36 billion already equals almost **82%** of that quarterly target.

BofA had said the game needed at least **$1.9 billion** to meet investors' fiscal 2027 expectations, and the estimate already beats that. BofA analyst Omar Dessouky expects Take-Two to announce its first official sales by **November 23**, [according to the same report](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/gta-6-day-one-sales-124649589.html).

## Where the players came from

- **PlayStation dominates:** about three quarters of sales were on PS5, and the rest on Xbox, [Sensor Tower told the BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9zr5nyv5dj2o).
- **Download spikes:** Cloudflare saw UK PS5 downloads peak at **8.6x** normal. New Zealand spiked **14.8x** four hours earlier, which hints that some players switched regions to start early.
- **Online traffic:** Rockstar's online services hit **11.2x** normal in Australia and **9.3x** on the US East Coast, [per the BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9zr5nyv5dj2o).

## True or False?

> **"Take-Two confirmed GTA 6 made $2.36 billion."** FALSE. It's a Sensor Tower estimate. Take-Two hasn't published any numbers yet.
>
> **"GTA 6 beat GTA V's three-day $1 billion in a single day."** TRUE, at least according to the estimates.
>
> **"GTA 6 is already on PC."** FALSE. Rockstar hasn't given a PC date, and there's no date for multiplayer either ([BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9zr5nyv5dj2o)).

## What's next

[Niko Partners expects](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9zr5nyv5dj2o) GTA 6 to pass **$3 billion** worldwide this year. Sensor Tower even predicts it will cut the time people spend on other single-player games by about 10%. The next big date is **November 23**, when official numbers could replace all this guesswork. We'll update you as soon as Take-Two talks.
